Describes the open scene as JSON: playhead, length, playback, chosen subject, editing mode, the output size the stage is framed in, soundtrack; each figure's name, whether it is humanoid, its height and keyed parts with frames; camera keys and the free view; the look's changed scene settings, lights, effect stack and edited figures with their key counts.

- Call it first, and again whenever the person may have changed the scene.
- Name figures exactly as given; a repeated model name reads `name (2)`.
