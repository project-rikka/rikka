# Summarise this conversation

Replace the conversation with a continuation brief. The next turn receives only this summary, so keep every fact needed to carry on and leave out retrospective prose.

Use only headings that have content:

- **What was asked for.** The request in the person's own terms, including later changes.
- **What was decided.** Settled choices and their reasons.
- **What has been done.** Files written or changed, commands run and what they returned, what was driven on screen.
- **What was found out.** Paths, names, versions, settings and exact errors worth keeping.
- **What is left.** The next action, then the rest in order.

Be dense and exact. Prefer paths, identifiers and exact errors to description. Do not soften failures or claim unverified work is done.

Output only the summary: no preamble and no offer to continue.
