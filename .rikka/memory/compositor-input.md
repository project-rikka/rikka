- `input/`: `SeatHost` in `seat_host.rs` (person-seat events go to `person_event`; `VirtualPointer`, idle and agent-seat warps are routed first); `launch.rs` adds `seat-0` with `Seats::add` and `rikka-agent` as its carried seat (`Seats::add_carried`, visible to agent-socket clients only) before the compositor exists, `seats.rs` gives the host `person()`/`agent_seat()` (`SeatMut<Compositor>`, only for input delivery; they settle first) and sets capabilities (`seat_up`), and registers `rikka.agent`/`rikka.caret`; `pending.rs` queue (a motion without relative travel, from absolute devices such as KVMs, tablets in mouse mode or absolute virtual pointers, gets travel derived from its position change, so relative-pointer and locked clients still move); `deliver.rs` hit-tests with `desktop.element_under` and applies click/pointer focus; `keys.rs` filters bindings, and takes nothing (not even VT switching) while locked or shortcut-inhibited; `actions.rs` runs shortcuts; `grabs/` holds grab types (popup chains per seat); `translate.rs` queues `rikka_compositor_input` events, which already carry the shared seat vocabulary (only scroll factors and touch positions are translated), and `tablet.rs` keeps the device → tablet/pad/tool id bookkeeping; `absolute::place` maps normalized devices to their named output; `warp.rs` (`pointer_follows_surfaces` each frame dispatch, `warp_pointer` for hints and `wp_pointer_warp`); `ime.rs` places input popups below the caret and draws them into `Parts::compose`'s drawn list; `idle.rs` inhibits idling while any live surface holds an inhibitor, visible or not; `turn.rs` runs once per reactor turn (idle, agent, caret re-announce).
- Every compositor-side cursor move (`cursor_to`) calls `Backend::pointer_warped`, so the KMS input thread's shared pointer position and cursor plane follow.
- `agent/` owns the agent seat: virtual pointers on `rikka-agent` move `Agent.at` and that seat's focus only; over an X11 window or caption the agent seat gets no focus and pointer and keys are replayed on the person's seat; `socket.rs` serves the agent socket; `ring.rs` draws the accent ring as a scene node after `Scenes::compose`; `rikka.agent` calls act on the compositor directly (placement, show, idle waits). `caret/` publishes the person's text-input caret (`SeatEvent::Caret` + surface origin) as `rikka.caret`.
- Activation tokens are admitted only when the person's seat issued their serial (`Seats::issuer`) no earlier than its keyboard enter (`Keyboard::issued_since_enter`).
- X11 (`x11/`): `X11` owns Xwayland and the `Xwm` (`server.rs` lifecycle, launched with `ClientOptions::xwayland()`); `x11.get()` reads, and intent commands (`configure`, `map`, `close`, `focus`, `enlarge`, `minimize`, `restack`, `offer_selection`, `read_selection`, `read_drag`, `drag_*`, `answer_drag`, `finish_drag`, `show_drop_proxy`) do nothing without Xwayland and log failures at their own level.
- Selection (`selection/`): `SelectionHost` in `host.rs`; `input/seat_host.rs` forwards every `SeatEvent` to `rikka_compositor_selection::seat_event`; `bridge.rs` is the X11 clipboard/primary bridge (X reads are gated on X focus inside `rikka-compositor-xwm`); `drag.rs` gives `hit_surfaces()` (skips the toplevel being dragged), moves a dragged toplevel through `windows::follow_drag`/`drag_released`, and requests frames as the icon follows; the icon draws at pointer + offset under the cursor.
