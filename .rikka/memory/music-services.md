- `music-stream`'s `Fetcher` (one tuned HTTP client per service, `.ahead(n)` pieces in flight, `.tries(n)` with Range resume) runs a `Plan` into a `Spool`: `Plan::file(url, size)` is 1 MiB ranges, `.piece` adds segments, `.lead` writes bytes first, `.header` adds request headers, `.framed(f)` prefixes each piece with a record header computed from its length. Each service states its policy where it builds its `Fetcher`. A piece answered with a status maps through `Error::from_status`; only `Unreachable` ones (429, 5xx) are retried, so an expired signed URL fails at once as `Refused`.
- Adapter kit in `music-stream` besides the player: `OffsetPaging` (offset/total listing rule; Qobuz pages every favourite and playlist listing with it), `Scan` (cursor for scraping web-client bundles: `find`, `expect`, `take(count, class)`, `matches(prefix, parse)`; no regex in the area). Adapters' one-shot HTTP goes through `music_stream::{api_client, answer, answer_bytes, answer_text, answer_length}`; every non-2xx status becomes `Error::from_status` (401/403 Refused, 404 Missing, 429/5xx Unreachable, else Unreadable), and an unparsable 2xx JSON body is Unreadable.
- Each service is an adapter crate implementing `Adapter` (const `SERVICE: Service { slug, name, mark, kind: Kind::{Streaming, Local} }`; `mark` comes from `rikka_symbols`, adapters never depend on `rikka-ui`, `open(&Setup)`) and `Provider` (`resume(folder)` / `sign_in(folder, prompt)` → `Link { account, catalog, playback }`). `Mark` is a Font Awesome Brands or Material Symbols codepoint.
- Every `Track` carries its `service` (the adapter's `SERVICE`) and `isrc: Option<Isrc>`; `Track::is` compares service and id. `Catalog` also owes `create_playlist(name, description)`, `arrange_playlist` (exact contents, in order) and `lookup` (same-song candidates; default text search via `query_for`, Spotify and Qobuz try the ISRC first).
- `Providers::new(Roots { cache, state }, &audio, &hifi).adapter::<A>()` (or `.try_provider(service, |setup| -> Result<P>)` for adapters built from outside state, e.g. the local library) gives each adapter `Setup` folders `<root>/<slug>`, its audio shelf and the shared `Deck`, panics on a duplicate or malformed slug, and hands out `Registered` entries whose resume/sign_in answer a `Connection` carrying the `Service`. A resume answering `Ok(None)` or `Err(Refused)` means the kept sign-in is gone.
- Adding a service = the crate (its own `BUILD.bazel`), one `deps` line in `apps/system/music/BUILD.bazel`, one line in `src/services.rs`. `SignInStep::Browse` opens the system browser; `SignInStep::Web` shows `src/portal.rs`: one private rikka-web `Session` of its own ephemeral `Runtime`, new windows load in it, engine exit falls back with `music.signing.page-closed`. Once the page sits on the request's landing host under `cookies_of`, it reads that site's cookies (HttpOnly included) from the engine's store with `Storage::cookies` in the session's partition until the proof cookie appears, then answers with them.
- `AudioCache` (music-core) is the LRU of played audio at `$XDG_CACHE_HOME/rikka/music/audio/<slug>/`, bounded live by `set_limit` (0 = keep nothing), pruned on a `rikka-music-cache` thread by max(atime, mtime); `prune_and_measure()` prunes and answers the size. Spools live in hidden `.spool/<slug>/<pid>-<n>` and are swept when their process is gone.
- `music-stream` is the shared file player: `Streamer::start(Arc<dyn Origin>, ProviderCache, Deck) -> Result<Streamer>` (thread spawn failure is `Error::Spawn`) implements `Playback`. An `Origin` either names a local file (`locate` → `Located { path, gains }`, played with no spool or cache) or `fetch`es bytes into a `Spool` and turns a `Stored` view into a reader (`Origin::open`); `gains` supplies ReplayGain (Qobuz from `track/get` `audio_info`). Streamed playback starts while the download grows; completed downloads are kept under `<track>.<quality slug>`, and a kept file its origin cannot open is deleted and fetched anew.
- A `Stored` reader blocks for bytes still arriving and reports `complete_len` only once the download is complete; announcing the length earlier makes the decoder's trailing ID3v1/APE probe wait for the whole download. `rikka_audio_decode::reader_decoder` turns it into a decoder.
- A fetch that fails with `Error::Refused` before its track opened makes the Streamer publish `PlaybackEvent::Refused` (the Player stops and names the service); every other fetch failure ends as `Unavailable`.
- `music-youtube`: InnerTube (`WEB_REMIX`, hl=en) with a cookie jar and English-label parsing; audio from guest `VISIONOS` URLs or the signed player solved by QuickJS (solver cached in `$XDG_CACHE_HOME/rikka/music/youtube-music`). Its `YouTubeOrigin` downloads AAC in 1 MiB ranges into the spool.
- `music-qobuz`: app id, secrets and OAuth `privateKey` come from play.qobuz.com's bundle.js, cached by player version in `$XDG_CACHE_HOME/rikka/music/qobuz/bundle.json`. Sign-in is browser OAuth to an `rikka_oauth::Loopback::ip` listener with a nonce path → `oauth/callback` → `user/login`; the token sits in the keyring (`session`); accounts without a streaming subscription are refused.
- Qobuz calls are signed with md5 of method + sorted args + ts + secret (favourites sign bare). The production secret is the Berlin-seeded one and doubles as the CMAF seed; it is validated by `session/start` (profile `qbz-1`), whose session `Api` caches and renews.
- Qobuz playback is CMAF only: `file/url` (27 → 7 → 6 for Highest, 5 → 6 for Low, `X-Session-Id`) gives a `$SEGMENT$` template and a `qbz-1` wrapped key; content key = AES-128-CBC unwrap with HKDF-SHA256(salt/info from session `infos`, IKM = hex secret). `Cdn` streams segments in order into the spool (3 requested ahead, Range resume on a cut).
- The kept Qobuz file (`sealed.rs`, a `rikka-file-format` binary header `RKQOBUZS`) is the CDN's encrypted init and segment records plus the content key sealed with AES-256-GCM under a key kept in the keyring (`content-keys` of the vault folder); `Decrypted` serves FLAC by locating and AES-CTR-decrypting single frames, so seeks and playback never decrypt ahead. Catalog `maximum_bit_depth`/`maximum_sampling_rate` (kHz) become `Resolution`, capped to CD when not `hires_streamable`.
- Every Qobuz cipher (`content_key`, `decrypt_frame`, the AES-256-GCM `Vault`) lives in `music-qobuz-keys`, the only Qobuz crate built without Cranelift (`cranelift = False`); `music-qobuz` names no cipher type, so AES-NI code is never monomorphized into a Cranelift-built crate.
- `music-soundcloud` speaks the web's api-v2 with `Authorization: OAuth <token>` plus the web `client_id` (from soundcloud.com's `apiClient` hydration, else the asset scripts), cached in `$XDG_CACHE_HOME/rikka/music/soundcloud/client-id`. Sign-in is the rikka-web portal on soundcloud.com/signin; `oauth_token` (+ `oauth_refresh_token`) go to the keyring (`session`). A 401 refreshes the token at secure.soundcloud.com/oauth/token through `rikka_oauth::Client` (client id from the token's JWT claims), else renews the client id, then retries once.
- SoundCloud ids: tracks, users and sets are numeric; system playlists keep their `soundcloud:system-playlists:` urn. Albums are sets with `is_album`. Set track lists hold stubs that are filled through `tracks?ids=` in batches of 50. Share links come from `permalink_url`s remembered from every answer (`links.rs`).
- SoundCloud playback picks a transcoding (AAC over MP3, tier lq/sq/hq by quality; no Opus, ABR or encrypted), resolves it with `track_authorization`, and downloads progressive files in 1 MiB ranges or HLS segments sized by HEAD. For fragmented MP4 the init plus first segment are fetched first and their `mdhd` and first `sidx` are stamped with the playlist's total length, since the decoder takes the length from the first `sidx`.
