---
title: Cinematic look pipeline in rikka-renderer-3d
description: Frame::look(Look) turns on the environment, figure styles, G-buffer, screen-space effects and compositor graph.
about: crates/graphics/rikka-renderer-3d/src/look
tags: [renderer, look]
---

- `Frame::look(Look)` switches a 3D frame to the cinematic pipeline; frames without a look keep the legacy flat-ambient, single tonemap path (viewer and tests rely on it).
- `Look` (pub fields, `Default` is the AAA starting point): `environment: Environment` (`None`, `Sky(Sky)` default, `Image(EnvironmentImage)`; see renderer-sky), `fog`, `overrides: Overrides` (scene-level material overrides, see material-system), `shadows`, `occlusion`, `reflections`, `illumination`, `denoise` (each effect picks a `Tier`, default Auto; see the ray-tracing note), `lines`, `particles` (with its own `focus` distance), `emitters` (analytic VFX emitters, see renderer-emitters), `composite` (the post-processing DAG, so `Look` is `Clone`, not `Copy`; see renderer-composite.md, effects in render-graph.md and effect-pack.md), `characters`. Never presets; apps change fields. Effects are off at strength 0 and depth of field at an infinite aperture; `Tone::Clamp` skips the tone curve.
- `GpuSky.environment` says whether SH irradiance and the prefiltered map are valid (sky or image), `GpuSky.physical` whether the atmosphere is (sky only). With `Environment::None` sky irradiance and specular fall back to `frame.ambient`; without the atmosphere aerial perspective, shafts, sun and moon are absent and the frame's own lights and background colour remain.
- `Sky::to_sun()/to_moon()` derive directions from hour/day/latitude/heading; `look/resolve.rs` adds the sun (with the shadow) and moon as directional lights before `frame.lights`, coloured by the CPU transmittance in `look/atmosphere.rs`.
- Per-figure overrides: `Frame::figure(Figure { head, overrides, rig, shadow }) -> FigureId`, `Draw::figure(id)`, `GpuModel::drawing(instance).figure(id).onto(frame)`. The GPU figure table holds heads plus the figure's shadow slot, key light and flags (index 0 = none, filled in every frame, looked or not); `GpuInstance.figure` indexes it and the G-buffer stores it.
- GPU data: `GpuLook { sky, space, scene }` (`look/gpu/*.rs` mirrored by `RikkaSky`/`RikkaSpace`/`RikkaScene` in `shaders/lib/rikka/common.slang`), reached as `rikka_push.frame.look` (a `RikkaLook*`); `frame.figures`, `frame.environment` (SH9 sky irradiance / π, GPU-written) and `frame.previous_positions` (deform history) are in `RikkaFrame`.
- Shared Slang contracts: `rikka.sky` (`rikka_sky_lit`, `rikka_sky_irradiance`, `rikka_sky_specular`; every consumer goes through them, so it never knows the source), `rikka.space` (`rikka_occlusion` → AO and bent-normal bend, `rikka_screen_shadow` → leading-light screen shadow), `rikka.surface` (G-buffer readers). `Look::shadows` (`Shadows { tier, distance, samples, screen: ScreenShadows }`) is described in renderer-shadows.
- Pass order, declared into the render graph by `record/mod.rs` `Record::declare`: deform, cull, cluster, prepass/HZB, `rikka.ray.build` (traced tiers only), surfaces (single-sample depth + RGBA32_UINT normal/velocity/figure/gloss, plus an R32_UINT object-layer target from the material's `SurfacesLayers` variant when a reachable composite node reads layers; includes depth-writing transparent PMX; also run for unlooked frames with fluid or temporal antialiasing), atmosphere, ray guides, occlusion (GTAO or RTAO), shadows (+fluid shadow splats), RT shadows, illumination (RT GI or SSGI), fog volume, `rikka.three.emitters.evaluate`, shade (+particles and emitters; split into two render passes around the temporal opaque capture, refraction or scattering), emitter motion and layers, fluid (depth, thickness, blur, smooth, composite into the chain, stamp; see renderer-fluid.md), `rikka.ray.keep`, then compose: outline, reflections (SSR or RT trace into the same resolve), lines, fog, temporal resolve/upscale + RCAS (everything after runs at display resolution; see temporal-antialiasing.md), the reachable composite nodes in index order, then `rikka.three.present` (dither + selection outline into the output texture).
- Areas own directories: `src/sky/`, `src/space/`, `src/post/`, `src/fluid/`; each declares its passes (`declare_*`) with closures over `Record`; `Record` methods there carry area-specific names because inherent methods share one namespace. Compute bodies use `Record::dispatch_kernel` (`record/command.rs`) and `graph::groups`; each area lists its kernels in `KERNELS`; sky/space/post kernels compile in the background when a frame first carries a look, fluid kernels with the first fluid layer, so plain 3D apps never build them. Sky tables and the environment rebuild only when sky, fog or time (or the panorama's texture, scale, clamp or rotation) change (`SkyState::lit`); the BRDF LUT (`pbr/lut.rs`) is written once.
- Per-draw (non-indirect) draws, i.e. transparent, overlay, see-through, hair/face layer depth and depth-writing transparent surfaces, go through `Record::draw_direct` (`record/direct.rs`) with a `DirectPass` that names the recipe, draw state and pass flags; consecutive draws of one bucket reuse its pipeline. A new direct pass is a new `DirectPass` variant.
- Warming: `Renderer3dState::precompile_for` starts one `src/warmup.rs` `Warmup` per kernel set (look: sky/space/post/pbr kernels plus `look_recipes`: fog apply, panorama background, the composite's fragment programs (`PostState::recipes`), space filters, particles, G-buffer and emissive view variants of known materials; denoise; rays), counted down by `Compiling::when_ready`. A viewport with `ViewportConfig::warming(Warming::DrawPlain)` (rikka-ui's viewport store sets it) draws looked frames without their look until `look_ready`, and `Viewport::is_converging` stays true meanwhile so the host redraws; the default `Warming::Wait` (offline renders, tests, benchmarks) blocks on the builds. Pipelines build on up to four `rikka-pipelines-N` threads (`core/pipeline.rs`).
- Every 3D image is a graph image: per-frame transients (`ImageSpec`), per-viewport history (`.history(key)`, e.g. exposure), renderer-wide (`.shared()`, sky LUTs, in `look_state.images`) or imported viewport/atlas/output images. Images whose slots travel in frame constants (`GpuLook`, `GpuFluid`) are `publish`ed: kept for the whole frame and left sampled after their last write; `SceneImages::publish` writes their slots after `ViewGraph::realize`. Storage images must be RGBA16F or R32F (binding 3 declarations). `record/chain.rs` `Chain` advances the HDR image (`filter`, `filter_compute`); each step is a new transient the aliaser folds back into two images. `MeshPipelines::kernel(shader)` caches compute pipelines (`prepare_kernel`/`when_kernel_ready` compile them in the background).
- The shading model's `gbuffer` hook (built-ins: parameters `gloss.roughness`, `gloss.reflection`) feeds SSR through the G-buffer.
- Post (`src/post/`): auto exposure snaps unless `frame.time` advances continuously, so apps pass playback time; its meter reads the G-buffer surface and weighs figure pixels by `Exposure::figure_metering` (default 0.25) so a stylized figure's own lift is not metered away; the Grade node (`shaders/post_grade.slang`) adds a scotopic night shift (`Grade::night`) from its exposure context.
