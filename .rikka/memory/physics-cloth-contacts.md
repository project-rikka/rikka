- Wraps (body vertices vs cloth triangles, candidates and their side judged with the body at the epoch start in `wraps.slang`) push only by the deepest-weighted average over candidates, at 0.3× the corner body radius (min over corners), fade out within 15 % of a triangle rim, need ≥ half the barycentric weight on movable corners, and shift `starts` so the push never becomes velocity. Opposed body vertices (a hand pressing the cloth from outside) stay candidates while they have crossed the cloth plane by at most the corners' body radius, so coarse proxy triangles that slid just past a resting hand are pushed back under it; deeper crossings and same-facing surfaces are never candidates once crossed.
- Several body pushes combine as Σ nₖaₖ / (1 + Σⱼ|nₖ·nⱼ|·min(aⱼ/1 mm, 1)): one contact pushes fully, opposed contacts meet in the middle, crossing ones add. A particle between opposed body surfaces (normals < −0.3) uses half their gap as its radius (`snug`, floored at `min(radius, 0.5 mm)`). Inside/outside verdicts keep a `JUDGED_INSIDE` flag-word bit with a 0.15-radius margin (`VERDICT_MARGIN`); probe candidates already held get a 3 mm key bonus so near-equal triangle sets stop alternating.
- Body sides (`probe.slang`): `body_eval` evaluates each body triangle over the swept particle/triangle path (`x`, `x_mid`, `x_end`) identically in both `consider` and `hold_previous` (required for GPU determinism across atomic grid insertion orders). Every candidate carries REMEMBERED plus an ABOVE bit. A per-figure inside verdict comes from the nearest candidate (ties → largest |plane distance|). In the solver, when the nearest candidate says outside, triangles in front count as ABOVE and a triangle behind is ignored unless either the epoch path or substep path (moved with the triangle) crossed it.
- Collider rescue (`settle.slang`): C0-continuous with `contact.settled` accumulation (no exit velocity kick) and per-substep cap `max(PUSH_SPEED * h, RESCUE_STEP)`. When `!outside`, triggers at `found.x < 0.0` toward surface `0.0`; when `outside`, triggers only deep inside at `limit = min(-0.08 * inradius, -4 mm)` toward `limit`. `collider_exit` falls back to shortest outward direction if `came_from` was already deep on the opposite side of the capsule core. Hull distances stop early (`collider_distance_past`, `hull_pair_past`).
- Contact word 23 (`RESIDUAL_WORD`) carries the penetration a push left unresolved; the `PUSH_SPEED` (3.0 m/s) cap applies only to that. `starts.w` holds a pressed code (10-bit octahedral contact normal + 1, 0 = free); retidy scales pressed-end weight by `1 - TOUCH_LOCK * touch_i * (1 - touch_j)` (`TOUCH_LOCK = 0.5`) so cloth slides tangentially instead of sticking and tearing, while `clamp_outward` blocks inward motion up to `HARD_TEARING` (1.8×).
- Body contact radius ramps from `NEAREST_GAP` to `radius` over geodesic root distance up to `rooting` (`ROOTED * thickness` for sheets; `min(ROOTED * radius, 25 mm)` for strands) in `bake.rs`; `inside` disables body contact only when `clearance < -radius` or (`clearance < NEAREST_GAP` and `root_distance < rooting`), so off-body particles near pins keep body collision without lifting at rest. A strand's cloth-contact thickness (`trait.b.z`) is `min(radius, style.thickness)`.
- Cross-layer cloth contact: `obstacles.slang` sweeps cloth triangle AABBs over `epoch_motion * epoch_span` (capped at 4 cm, `Settings::epoch_span`) plus drawn `cover`. In `probe.slang`, sheet-over-sheet pairs evaluate swept `layer_cloth_eval` (through `offer_layer`, shared by `consider` and `hold_previous_layer` for determinism) within `inner_reach` (`INNER_MARGIN = 18 mm`, `INNER_KEY_BONUS = 12 mm`, widened only for own layer triangles in `walk`); its `swept_end` uses epoch motion like the broadphase, and only pinned outer corners read `targets_to`, because probe pushes unpinned targets in the same dispatch. Outer sheets always see inner sheets (keeping their rest side within `INNER_REST_RANGE = 6 cm` in `layered_side`, never setting `TANGLED`), and inner sheets see upper outer sheets (`depth < 0.45`) only when the inner particle was behind the outer triangle at rest (`rest_under_outer` within `OUTER_REST_RANGE = 8 cm`) as inward containment (`REMEMBERED`, tracked skeletally in the solve via `outer_point` so moving waistbands stay accurate without velocity-extrapolation ping-pong). `snug_layer` checks `apart - cover < SNUG_LAYERS * thick` so rest-tight covered layers never over-push. `self_contact` caps layered pushes at `max(PUSH_SPEED * h, LAYER_STEP)` (6 mm), `finish.slang` couples outer-layer velocity against already-solved inner layers (inner triangles only hold particles before `inner_end`), and `post_project` clamps layered sheet pairs (`POST_LAYER_STEP = 6 mm`, outer containment only when `body_free`).
- Body probe: 16 lanes per particle, 4 particles per 64-thread workgroup; lanes merge their top-K lists in a 4-level tree (order-independent because every comparison breaks ties by id). On layer-0 sheets, opposed body triangles in front rank at `OPPOSED_KEY` (0.6) × distance. `nearest` holds `NEAREST_WORDS = 9` words per particle: 4 facing body triangles (`BODY_NEAREST`), 4 inner-layer cloth triangles (`LAYER_NEAREST`), and 1 packed pair of nearest collider indices (`COLLIDER_NEAREST`).
- `drape.slang` push-out accepts binds with `gap >= -6 mm` (`MIN_DRAPE_GAP`), gathers up to `PUSH_PLANES = 16` deduplicated body planes across all weighted influence triangles (`PUSH_FACING = 0.1`, face-closest or `edge_near` within `max(required, 0) + 7 mm`), projects cyclically (`PUSH_ROUNDS`), then collects unique `COLLIDER_NEAREST` colliders across all influence triangles once and runs C0-continuous collider push-out to `min(required, 0)` (second round only if pushed with >1 unique collider); `present()` uploads colliders from `figure.shown` only on `capture`/`checkpoint` restore paths (`advance_into` reuses the colliders already uploaded in `record()`).
