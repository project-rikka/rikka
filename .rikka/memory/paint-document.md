---
title: Paint document model (paint-document)
description: paint-document opens, edits, records history, previews and saves PSD-native documents for rikka-paint, UI-free.
about: apps/general/paint/crates/paint-document
tags: [application, paint]
---

- `paint-document` (UI-free) owns opening (`Document::open(path, &Imaging)`, 2 GiB working-memory guard), the layer view tree (`Layer`, `LayerId` = pre-order index in psd order, shown top-first), `composite()` (the picture, or the stored PSD composite until layers are touched, then rikka-psd-render), `tiles(side)`, thumbnails and start-page `preview`. Internals: `state.rs` (`State` = content + selection, `Swap`), `history.rs` (`Timeline`), `develop.rs` (PSD layer ↔ canvas picture), `reshape.rs`, `resampler.rs` (GPU `rikka_psd::Resampler`), `convert.rs`, `save.rs`.
- Layer edits: `Edit::check(&layers)` holds every rule on the `Layer` view (the GUI uses it for `enabled`, `apply` runs it first); `arrange.rs`/`merge.rs` (merges flatten through rikka-psd-render) rebuild a cloned psd through `rikka_psd::NodePath`s (`layer.rs` maps `LayerId` ↔ path), `restyle.rs` swaps one record. A plain picture is promoted with `save::picture_document` on its first layer edit; saving it flat afterwards answers `Saved::Copy`. The Background (bottom root layer without a transparency channel) cannot move, be moved under, rename, fade or blend (`Error::BackgroundLocked`); the last root layer cannot be deleted (`Error::LastLayer`).
- New Image (Ctrl+N) is a window-level `rikka_widgets::dialog` (`new_image.rs`) that opens an untitled tab (`Tab::create`, `path() == None`) on `Document::new_blank(&Blank, ..)`, a PSD with a resolution resource; saving an untitled tab asks for a destination. The Layers panel is the layer list plus an icon footer, with no inline property controls; name, visibility, opacity and blend change only through the Layer Properties dialog. Its `panel/grab.rs` turns row presses into choosing, the context menu and drag-reorder (`Placement`); `tab/arranging.rs` holds `Layering` orders, the drag aim, the context menu and closed groups remapped by name path.
- Plain pictures stay GPU-resident as `rikka_image_edit::Picture` at their own depth and profile; workers use `imaging::shared` and the app opts in with `App::computes_in_background()`. `Tile.area` is in document pixels; its frame may be smaller (previews) and is stretched over it.
- Every change is an `Edit` (`Show`, `Select { region, by: SelectionAction }`, `Crop`, `Develop { layer, recipe }`, `Fill`, `Erase`, `Reshape(Reshape)`, `Convert`, `Undo`, `Redo`, `Revisit(i)`, then the layer edits `NewLayer`, `DuplicateLayer`, `DeleteLayer`, `MoveLayer { to: Placement }`, `RenameLayer`, `SetOpacity`, `SetBlend`, `SetProperties`, `MergeDown`, `MergeVisible`, `Flatten`, `LayerFromBackground`) applied by `Document::apply`, answering `Change { pixels: PixelChange (Unchanged < Content < Canvas), selection, history, layers, layer }`; `layer` names the layer to choose next (made, moved or merged; on undo/redo the one before/after), since `LayerId`s shift after structural edits. Edits are atomic: they build the new state first, then swap it in.
- History: each edit records an `Action` (first `Open`, or `New` for `Document::new_blank`) plus reversible `Swap`s (old picture, or only the changed `Patch` when a plain picture keeps its size and under ¾ of it changed (`Swap::from_pictures` over `Picture::changed_area`/`piece`, exchanged fallibly with rollback), old psd layer, visibility flag, old region, whole old psd document, or the whole old content when a plain picture turns layered); undo exchanges them so the same entry redoes. Limits 50 states / 1 GiB estimated bytes, oldest dropped first; `history()` is a borrowed view, `is_modified()` compares with the saved state.
- The selection is document state (`selection()`): crop and resize/rotate clear it, turn/flip/canvas size transform it. `Fill`/`Erase` and GUI recipes mask with `Region::mask(w, h)`; no selection means the whole layer; erasing an opaque layer paints the given background colour.
- `preview(layer, recipe, scale)` renders through a cached `imaging::Preview` (pictures on power-of-two proxies; PSD layers at full size, swapped into the psd for rikka-psd-render) until the revision or layer changes; `end_preview()` drops it. `histogram(layer, within)` hides pixels outside the region.
- PSD reshapes use `rikka_psd::Document::{flip, rotate_quarter, resize_canvas, resample}`; resampling runs per channel on the GPU via `Transform`/`Resize` on linear-tagged pictures (weight = alpha, so premultiplication is alpha weighting). PSD `Convert` needs 8/16-bit RGB. Arbitrary rotation sizes the canvas with `rikka_psd::Geometry::rotate` (ceil) for plain pictures too, through an imaging `Transform`.
- `save(path, &Saving)` picks the format with `FileFormat::for_path` (`FileFormat::writable()` lists them with `takes_quality`/`keeps_alpha`), writes atomically through `rikka_file_edit::replace` (keeps permissions and symlinks; Cut's `.rcut` save uses the same writer), refreshes a stale PSD composite and thumbnail, turns plain pictures into a one-layer PSD, flattens alpha-less formats on white, and answers `Saved::Document` (saved point marked) or `Saved::Copy` (layered document exported flat).
- `Edit::check` also refuses `Bucket`/`Text` on non-pixel or hidden layers and wand/layer sampling of non-pixel layers; `apply` runs it for those edits too. `Document::compose(name, |document| ..)` runs any edits (reading `layers()`, `selection()` or `measure_text` between them) as one `Action::Batch(name)` entry, gathering every swap and layer focus (first `before`, last `after`), undone in reverse swap order and rolled back whole when a step or the closure fails; `Edit::Batch { name, edits }` is the same for a fixed list.
- `paint-document/src/reductions.rs` keeps the last few detail-level reductions and the plain picture's thumbnail by revision: every recorded or travelled `Swap` reports what it touched (`Swap::touched`: a patch's rectangle, a selection nothing, anything else everything), each revision logs it, and a cached reduction is reused, updated over the touched rectangle with `Picture::resized_since`, or redone when the log has a gap. `tiles`/`thumbnail(s)` therefore take `&mut Document`.
