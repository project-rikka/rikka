- Layout: caption holds only icon + File/Edit/Insert/Playback/Markers/View `menu_bar`; top half is the preview (`monitor/`: tool row (`tools.rs` over `tool::Tool`), the `stage::Stage` widget, transport row ▶/■, start, prev edit, prev frame, `Seekbar`, next frame, next edit, end, master volume) beside the `panel/` property panel (the item editor for exactly one selected clip: shape/line/text groups plus 描画; footage gets 描画, 映像 (corner radius, flips) and 再生 (speed) when it has a picture, 音声 (keyable volume 0–200% and pan, mute, fade in/out lengths and curves) when it has sound); bottom half (shared `Ratio` split) is the timeline toolbar (zoom slider, add media, undo/redo, cut/copy/paste/split/delete, snapping, `trimmer::Trimmer` tiles, add marker, new/open/save), ruler (markers row above the timecodes), layers and scrollbar; `status.rs` is the bottom bar (live timecode/frame `Clock`, format button opening project settings, project name).
- Every action is a `command::Command` (`BINDINGS`, `enabled`, `run`); labels live in `wording::command`, and menus, toolbars and the right-click menu reuse them. Keys: Space, ←/→ frame, Ctrl+←/→ edit point, Ctrl+B split at playhead, Ctrl+X/C/V, Delete, Ctrl+Z/Y, J/K/L, I/O and `.` (stop and return); V selects on the stage and the timeline, B/N/Shift+Y/Shift+U arm ripple/roll/slip/slide, Q/W/Alt+Q/Alt+W/E trim to the playhead, M/Alt+M/Shift+M/Ctrl+Shift+M/Ctrl+Alt+M handle markers.
- The UI-free model is `cut-project` (cut-project note; keyframes and projection in cut-animation); the preview compositor is `cut-render` (cut-render note); thumbnails and waveforms are cut-filmstrip and cut-waveform (cut-glance note).
- `Cut` window state splits into `cut/{editing,clipboard,drawing,effecting,files,importing,playing,popup,styling,writing}.rs`; per-document view state (selection, edit point, keyframing, marker editor, popup, layer resize, pointing, text draft, transition preview) is one `cut/session.rs` `Session` that opening or starting a project replaces wholesale, and the modal drafts (export, reformat, relink, paste attributes) are one `sheet: Option<Sheet>` (`cut/sheet.rs`, read through `draft`/`draft_mut`); the export job and the unsaved prompt stay separate because they overlay a sheet; the clipboard is app-internal (`Clipboard { pieces, layer }`). `drawing.rs` adds/draws/restyles graphics (tool buttons only arm a `Tool`; the Insert menu and the timeline menu add at the centre; `art.rs` holds defaults); panel rows are generic over `Adjustable` (`Graphic`, `Treatment`, `Region`; `cut/styling.rs` `restyle`/`preview_style`); sliders and colour wells preview through `LivePreview` (Rc, Observed `Appearance` overrides) and commit one `Redraw` on release. `writing.rs` keeps one `Draft { clip, document, typing }` for the single selected text: the stage and the panel's `text_editor` edit the same `TextDocument`, and `commit_text` commits it before any other edit, undo, save or selection change.
- `Transport` (Rc, Observed) is the frame clock (`playing()` hands its running clock to the preview); play is asked and starts on the next tick once `Preview::is_ready` (the newest request rendered with exact footage frames) or after 1 s. `Cut::tick(now)` only drives `Playback::listen`: `Voices` (one `AudioEngine`, a streamed voice per layer+`cut_render::Alignment`, at the clip's speed, `Mix` re-applied every tick, taken from `LivePreview` while a slider is dragged, master volume); shuttling and parking silence it.
- `Lanes` is one widget for all layers (`hit.rs`, `grab.rs`, `paint.rs`, `wedges.rs`, `strip.rs`, `wave.rs`): drag to move (vertically across layers), edges trim, animation preset ramps' inner edges resize presets, the selected animated clip's disclosure opens its key lanes (cut-keyframing-ui note), empty drags band-select, right click opens the context menu pinned at the click, file drags dropped on it land at that frame/layer (the window `drop_zone` is `content_first`). Timeline widgets call `keep_focus` on press so their clicks leave keyboard focus where it was.
- `timeline/rows.rs` `Rows::layout(project, resizing)` holds cumulative row tops for variable heights (rows past the last extrapolate with the default height). Each header ends in a `sizing::Sizing` grip: dragging previews through `Cut::pull_layer` (tracked `resizing`) and commits `Cut::resize_layer` on release; the View menu's レイヤーの高さ runs `Edit::ResizeAll`. Thumbnails and waveforms are mapped in the cut-glance note.
- `stage/` paints the preview: each paint builds a `cut_render::Request` (project `Arc`, playhead, `Quality::scale` of the stage's device-pixel scale, `LivePreview::overrides()` plus the typing draft, the manipulated visuals and the drawing-in-progress graphic, `Transport::playing()`) and hands it to `playback::Preview`, which starts the `Renderer` on the shared imaging engine and receives frames into one `Computed` from the render thread (no UI-thread work per frame); the frame is drawn snapped to device pixels in the format rect, with offline notes for footage whose reader failed and dashed outlines for selected shape regions on top. Items are `Visual`s, so footage moves, resizes (uniform zoom, no side grips) and turns with the same grips as graphics; one manipulation commits one `Redraw` of `Appearance`s. Gestures: an armed drawing tool draws on any drag (even over items; Shift squares/aims, Alt from the centre), adds nothing on a plain click and returns to Select after drawing; the Text tool clicks into existing text or writes new text; Escape disarms. With Select, grips resize/turn (`figure::Manipulation`), bodies move with snapping to frame and item edges/centres (`guides.rs`, off with Ctrl or the snap toggle), empty drags band-select, double-click or Enter types into text (`TAKES_FOCUS` while typing), arrows nudge 1/10 px. It is focusable, so a press on it takes keyboard focus.
- `LivePreview` (Rc, Observed) holds a `cut_render::Overrides` of previewed `Appearance`s and effect stacks (`show`, `show_effects`, `appearance`, `effects`, `commit`), a reworked project from `preview_edits`, and a peek frame (`show_frame`, `peek`) the stage renders instead of the playhead (overlays hidden) until `commit`. `Cut::quality()`/`set_quality(Quality::{Auto, Full, Half, Quarter})` (`quality.rs`) picks the preview scale, capped by what the stage shows; the View menu lists `Command::Quality` entries.
- The panel is a `component` over `Cut` reading `shown_project()` and `LivePreview`, so it shows values at the playhead and follows previews; its rows, diamonds, animation presets/camera groups and the easing picker are mapped in the cut-panel-motion note. 範囲 for regions (`panel/region.rs`: whole or a `ShapeKind`, size, corner, feather, invert) and 映像エフェクト (`panel/effects.rs`) for every visual clip: one foldable card per `EffectSlot` (enable, name, up/down, remove) around `effects::form` whose `Tuning` previews via `Cut::preview_effect` → `LivePreview::show_effects` and commits `Cut::retune_effect` → `Edit::Effects` (unchanged stacks skipped); the add button opens a `Family` menu. Cards are keyed by (clip, slot, kind); never read `palette()` inside their `local_state`.
- Keyframes: diamonds (filled = key at the playhead, accent = animated, muted = still, dimmed outside the clip) toggle keys on click and open the easing picker on right click; the panel's キーフレーム row and Playback menu step the playhead over `Clip::keyed_frames` (`Command::{PreviousKey, NextKey}`). Effect operations live in `cut/effecting.rs`, key operations in `cut/animating.rs`.
- Popups (`cut/popup.rs` `Popup::{Effects, Easing(Picking), RangeShape, …}`) are anchored popups held in `Cut`, cleared on selection change.
- Effect ranges: `Tool::Range(ShapeKind)` (tool row tile + chevron menu choosing `Cut::range_shape`) draws with the shape gesture rules and a dashed outline; Insert/timeline menus add an effect range (centre) or a whole-frame effect. `insert_region` inserts then `Document::amend`s a Gaussian blur, so one undo step removes both. Region clips are purple (accent×danger) with a wand badge and their effect names.
- Edit-point transitions, the transition gallery tab and audio crossfades are mapped in the cut-transitions note.
- Export (video, audio-only, still frame) is mapped in the cut-export note.
- Sound levels, fades, markers, trim modes and project settings are mapped in the cut-editing note.
- Media relinking (Link Media sheet, Replace Media) and Paste Attributes/Effects are mapped in the cut-relink-attributes note.
- Icon controls carry tooltips: `controls::tool` names itself from `Command::tooltip()` (`wording::command` plus its chord); other glyph buttons chain `.tooltip(tr!(..))`.
