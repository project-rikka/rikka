- Every edit marks the node in the scene's journal (content, bounds, order, layer, kept, new, shifted by its group, moved). The batcher reads only that journal and the removed list, so a frame costs O(change). A rect edit that keeps the size is `MOVED`, not content: same-size moves of continuous shapes (`Rect`, `RoundedRect`, `Circle`, `Arc`, `Capsule`, `Image`) and moved text nodes whose every glyph keeps its subpixel bin and moves by the same whole pixels (`emit::whole_shift`; glyphs are placed from the node's whole-pixel top) only update their entry in the per-node offset table (`draw/moves.rs`, binding 5, added to the group offset in `primitive.slang`'s vertex entry, reset on emission); anything else is emitted again. A zooming text node (changed `TextPaint::scale`) is emitted again every frame at its exact placement.
- A steady draw allocates nothing: pass labels are written into a stack buffer per pass, the submit reuses its lists, recording tickets are reused once no image holds them, and `RenderOutput::damage` is an inline `Damage`.
- Batch preparation (glyph raster and atlas placement, emission) runs in a `Preparer` (`renderer.preparer()`, one per thread) without the renderer lock; `draw`/`record_image` only upload and record. A scene not prepared beforehand is prepared inside the draw by the renderer's own preparer.
- The batch (arena, chunks, segments, glyph references) lives in each `Scene` (`draw::SceneCache`), tied to the renderer that built it. Dropping a scene hands its glyphs and GPU store back through the `Reclaimer`.
- `GpuInstance` is 128 bytes (2 cache lines): `kind`, `flags` and `color` pack into `meta`, and per-node `clip` and `cut` shapes live in a compact `ClipPool` (`draw/clips.rs`, binding 7, shared across all glyphs of a text node), making binding 0 (`instances`) vertex-only. Small tables (`groups`, `group_clips`, `shown`/`captured`, `moves`, `node_clips`) upload straight into per-frame host-visible `RingTable` buffers (zero staging copies and zero `COPY` barriers); `Store` and `BackdropStore` expire by wall clock (`UNUSED_FOR`).
- Paint order is cut into chunks that carry their region, `has_textured` and a stable id (`RetainedBatch::chunk_index`). Instances keep stable arena slots with room to grow; identical rewrites upload nothing. Arriving or regrown nodes are slotted beside their paint-order neighbour in the same layer (`draw/placing.rs`: split its chunk if needed, extend or insert a chunk, zero the vacated instances) instead of relaying every chunk; edits inside layers or to glass stay in place and `fades::reframe` widens capture regions/layer bounds and patches backdrop regions. Only a layer change, a backdrop segment appearing/vanishing, a reorder, a layer crossing full/zero opacity, a missing same-layer neighbour or too many chunks relay whole (`restructured`). A scattered arena is packed by copying, not re-emitting. The ledger records the moves (`Relocation`), and a store one version behind packs its own buffer in place through a shared scratch buffer (`draw/shuffle.rs`), while a buffer that grows carries its contents into the new one (`carry`); only a store further behind uploads whole. A store buffer over 3/4 full has its doubled successor made ahead on the provision thread, so growing rarely allocates in a frame.
- Instance uploads stage each changed arena span straight into mapped staging memory (`StagingBelt::stage_gathered`) with vertex-stage barriers only; arena ledgers keep at most as many changed instances as the arena holds (older stores upload whole), and stores find their first unseen change by binary search.
- `Renderer::frame_work()` exposes cumulative `FrameWork` counters (walked, emitted, reused, restructured, uploaded instances and bytes); `since` gives a frame's share. `next_free_frame()` lets a caller wait for a frame slot outside its lock.
- Occlusion (`draw/occlusion.rs`, kept scenes only): `RetainedBatch::occluders` (maintained per change by `track_occluder`) lists big solid Rect/RoundedRect fills, images declared opaque with `Node::opaque_within(rect)` (untilted, opacity 1, non-empty emitted span) and glass at opacity 1, without layer or cut-out. Each frame `ScenePass` cuts the damage into pieces under the topmost occluder core (shape minus corner reach and stroke, clipped with 1 px edge inset; images inset 2 px), and draws each piece only from its occluder's position (segment, chunk, instance) up; a single damage rect covered completely by occluders skips `LOAD_OP_CLEAR`. Glass occludes only when its blur is reused this frame and the scene background is opaque; an occluder counts only where a `Chunk::has_textured` chunk or backdrop lies beneath it, and never inside the reads of a backdrop re-blurred from the target below it.
