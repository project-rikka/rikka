- Glass: a retained dual-filter blur pyramid, with lens refraction and a rim for liquid glass. Up passes climb back to the half-resolution level; `Blur::chain` matches sigma, with exact tap margins (`1.5` down, `0.75` up). Passes declare their sample reach, so write regions are derived backwards. Blur passes are `PassExtent::Plan` (`BackdropPass::extent`): they cover the plan region at every level, map `output.region`'s exact scene rect into `src_rect` and get their taps clamped to the valid input texels through `params[1]` (`BackdropPass::params_clamped_to`); `PassExtent::Input` passes (the default, custom effects) cover their input. A plan's source reach adds only the first pass's write grid (`first_write_rounding`).
- Lens refraction is a full-resolution pass (`refract.slang`, `shaders/lib/canvas/lens.slang`) that `refract::bend` puts before the blur chain, so the bend is drawn sharp and then softened. A turned output's blur source is turned upright by one small composite pass first.
- Joined glass: `Joined::new(host, limb)` (public, `None` unless the limb hangs square off a straight, square-cornered host side with room for its fillets) makes two touching backdrop nodes one lens body. Every node of the body carries `.joined(j)`: `j.host()` and `j.limb()` (both snapped to a whole-pixel seam) and each present `j.fillets()` piece (`[start, end]` options of square + hollow cut-out, as round as the limb's far corners); the shell paints its fillet tint from the same `fillets()`. The refract pass then bends by the body's interior distance (no rim along the seam) with the host's bevel and bulge centre, in a canonical frame where the limb hangs below; joined damage uses an isotropic `RefractionField` accounting for aligned `bevel + bulge` displacement and `softened + sample_margin` reads. fx passes carry 20 params (`effect::PARAMS`).
- A lens's corner radii are left out of the backdrop cache signature (`BackdropPass::corners_at`). The cache entry remembers the `RoundedRect` it was blurred for, and `Plan::reshaped` makes the changed corners, grown by the reach from the refract pass on, stale for the executor and for damage expansion. So re-rounding a pane re-blurs only its corners, whoever owns the `BackdropCache`.
- Damage touching a backdrop's zone (`Plan::lift_of` so foreground over the read area never burns into the cache) grows when that blur must be made again (the cache can't hold the plan: the whole region; a cached blur with stale rects: each stale rect's reads), but only by the parts of the zone the backdrop itself or anything painted above it covers (`RetainedBatch::painted_above`, chunk bounds after its segment): elsewhere the target already holds the frame's pixels. Overlapping implicit glass panes propagate newly expanded damage to other panes in a fixed-point loop, and skipped clipped/occluded stale backdrops invalidate their cache entry so reappearance re-blurs fresh pixels.
- Lens reach is exact per region, not per pane (`fx/field.rs`, `RefractionField`): rim cells (32 px) bound both outward bevel displacement and inward `lens_swell` (`bulge`) pull per axis; everything inside `flat_core` is drawn in only by the swell (`Lens::profile_for_size` → `RefractionProfile`; `BackdropPass::core_taps`/`core_stale_reach` are the reach without bevel displacement). A change deep under a big pane re-blurs and repaints near itself instead of the whole pane.
- `Spread::new(shape, effect, lens, joined)` (public) answers which output rects a change behind a pane makes stale (`stale_regions`) and how far outside the pane changes matter (`reads`, `sensitive_region`); the compositor's panes and the renderer's own caching both use it. Explicit caches are `Node::backdrop_cache(BackdropCache::{current, stale})`.
- Backdrop nodes without an explicit `BackdropCache` keep their blur themselves per `Viewer` (`scene/backdrop_caches.rs`): each frame's fresh damage goes through `Spread::stale_regions` into a viewer-scoped stale `BackdropCache` keyed by scene, viewer, node, era and an epoch bumped whenever the node goes untracked (hidden or off the viewport for that viewer). `FrameBackdrops` computes `Optics`/`Plan` once per frame per visible backdrop and shares it across damage expansion, occlusion gathering and backdrop drawing. The painter keeps those cache entries alive each frame (`FxExecutor::keep_alive`), as `retain_backdrops` does for its keys; both are refreshed at frame end, just before entries unused for `UNUSED_FOR` expire.
- A plan's region is cut to the viewport, so a pane reaching past the target only has a blur for its visible part. The pane quad's glass uvs are therefore mapped linearly over the whole pane (`EffectOutput::uv_of`), and `primitive.slang` clamps each fragment's uv to the valid texels (`uv_bounds`, push `glass_bounds`); never clamp the quad's corner uvs, which stretches the backdrop.
- A pane keeps its backdrop per key across rotated targets.
