- Entry points take the caller's recognised `rikka_humanoid::Anatomy`, so a figure is recognised once: `ClothAnalysis::new(model, &anatomy, &ClothSettings)` (analysis internals: `analysis/mod.rs` `Analysis`, `group.rs` `Grouping` → `Candidate` pieces) (roles, painted spreads + pieces for the UI), `ClothAsset::build(model, &anatomy, &ClothSettings, Budget)` and `Wearer::from_model(model, &anatomy, &ClothAnalysis)` (`wearer.rs`: the welded Body-role mesh, no colliders, plus each sheet piece's rest `Garment` whose vertices carry `give` = `Style::yield_at` over render-mesh geodesic reach from its pins, loose parts pinned like the solver, 0 for disabled pieces; rikka-animate's body fitting uses it); all are CPU-only and bit-for-bit deterministic, with independent per-vertex and per-part searches on the crate's `PARALLEL` (thread-count independent, see model-performance note). `Figure::weld` is the one same-position weld.
- `ClothAsset::from_analysis(model, &anatomy, &ClothAnalysis, Budget)` builds the same asset as `build` from an existing analysis of the wanted settings (it keeps the head frame and caches the decimated skin body shared with `Wearer::from_model`); `build` analyses and then does the same.
- `Style::reach_at`/`backstop_at`/`yield_at` are the one definition of how far the solver lets a particle leave its skinned place (reach × spread × max_distance(depth); backstop widened by `BACKSTOP_TIP_GAIN` toward sheet tips); rikka-physics-cloth's `bake.rs` uses them.
- `ClothAsset::pick(at, radius)` picks the nearest loose particle to a rest point (outer layers win on equal distance) and its piece's loose neighbours with a smooth falloff.
- Pipeline: `figure/` skins every render vertex at rest (global vertex index ↔ `VertexKey{node, vertex}`) and borrows the caller's `Anatomy` → `analysis/` (structural classes → regions → hints/geometry → crop → paint → pieces) → `sheet/` or `strands.rs` build a `Proxy` → `constraints.rs` adds edges, bends, reach/depth, parent tree, tethers, anchors → `bind/` → `layers.rs`; `body/` builds the body mesh and fitted colliders.
- `body/`: `skin` (behind `Wearer::from_model`) welds the Body-role render vertices into the body mesh (≤ 8000 triangles) and its `Shell`; `colliders` groups Body vertices by strongest bone (small clouds fold into parents), `fit.rs` scores tapered-capsule chains (1–3 lobes per PCA axis, `chain.rs`/`circle.rs`) against one k-DOP hull (`hull.rs`) by excess skin depth, and shrinks a solid by the top-10%-trimmed rest cloth intrusion to clear by 2 mm (at most 35 % of its thinnest size). Colliders live in their bone's rest frame (`BodyCollider { node, shape }`).
- The analysis reads structure, never bone or material names; renaming every bone and material leaves it unchanged. `Skeleton::core`/`under_head` come from `Anatomy::region`/Head; `hints.rs` marks the anatomy's non-flesh swings (PMX dynamic bodies / VRM springs; breasts are flesh swings) and their descendants as carried (`FREE_SHARE` 0.25 of weight marks Free).
- `analysis/class.rs` classes each surface: `hard.rs` words (metal, jewel, weapon… the only name table) → Solid; ≥ 25 % of vertices on swings → Hair (swing anchored under the head) or Garment; ≥ 80 % on the head, < 0.8 × skull and inside the skull box ± 2 cm → Detail; ≥ 24 vertices and ≥ 60 % innermost → Skin; head surfaces reaching the crown or below the neck → Hair; else Unknown. `inner.rs` bins non-carried vertices around human-bone segments (1.5 cm × 16 sectors) and calls a vertex innermost within 5 mm of its bin's smallest radius. Then geometry against the skin shell (tight ≤ 8 mm pins). When any hair or garment region is hinted, unhinted ones of that kind stay skinned/body.
- `analysis/parts.rs` welds Free/Pinned vertices into parts; a part is an *attachment* (`Part::is_attachment`, never cloth on its own) when its material is `Class::Solid` (metal, jewel, weapon…) or it is rigid: ≥ 90 % of vertices strongest on one carried bone, or (sheets) ≥ 90 % single-influence on carried bones.
- `analysis/attach.rs` joins a sheet part to a larger non-attachment host when it touches it (≥ max(4, 5 %) vertices within 1 cm) or lies on it (≥ 50 % within 1.5 cm) and ≥ 50 % of its free vertices' strongest bones are the host's: trims, overlays and hanging gems become one piece. Attachment objects (touching attachments joined) follow the non-attachment host they have most vertices within 1.5 cm of and share bones with, when ≤ 30 cm and wholly within 8 cm of it; others stay skinned (a skinned ornament beside a swinging sash floats where the skinned sash would be). Linings meeting a garment only at a seam stay separate pieces.
- `analysis/group.rs` skins garments whose free extent is < 2.5 cm or (sheets) whose free vertices mostly lie within 2 cm of hair (no cloth–hair collision). Preset (`suggest.rs`): sheets standing above their pins → Accessory; free vertices ≥ 80 % on swings hung from Hips/Spine with one root behind the hips and a round elongated shape → Tail (a Strands piece); else small → Accessory, narrow → Ribbon, ≥ 50 % hung from arm bones → Sleeve, wraps the body's vertical axis in ≥ 12/16 sectors → Skirt, wraps a limb → Sleeve, high near shoulders and behind → Cape, high near shoulders and in front → Ribbon, else Skirt. Hair: ≥ 22 cm LongHair, front root Bangs, else ShortHair. `layers.rs` orders overlapping sheets by majority signed-surface side votes against each other's rest `Shell` (≥ 2× margin, ≥ 4 votes) before falling back to median body clearance, and resolves layers topologically.
- Pieces group garments by (kind, surface title of non-attachment vertices, preset); `PieceKey` is the smallest member key, and `ClothSettings` settings re-attach by key or by ≥ 50 % overlap of a 32-vertex member sample.
- `ClothTuning { sway, response, weight }` (0.5 = unchanged, returned as is) maps onto a `Style` in `tuning.rs`: sway scales shape keeping, reach, bend, compression, buckling and extent both ways, lowers damping/attenuation when livelier and raises `settling` when calmer; response runs inertia from fully carried (0) to twice real inertia (`carried` −1) with stronger centrifugal; weight scales gravity against air. `ClothSettings::tuning` is a figure-wide feel the analysis applies on top of every piece's own style.
- `ClothSettings::same_topology` is true when two settings differ only in tuning/style (paint and the cutting settings — disabled, preset, kind, layer — equal), i.e. a cloth asset can be restyled instead of re-tailored.
- Sheets become ONE continuous envelope per piece, ignoring render topology (`sheet/`): same-distance weld → `cluster.rs` simple-distance clustering (Magica step schedule, valence cost, X-guard only for adjacent pairs, pinned pairs merge only within half the radius) at `TOPOLOGY` (1.75) × spacing so pleats, slits, linings and frills collapse → `envelope.rs` re-topology (mapped render triangles ranked by area, greedy manifold with a fold check, flood orientation, bow-tie split, small-hole ear fill, Delaunay flips, crumb removal) → Loop `subdivide.rs` while the mean edge exceeds 1.75 × spacing and the envelope is still coarser than the render vertices. Bow-tie copies left at one position are moved 10 % toward their own fan (`part_pinches`). Sheet envelopes set `shear: false`, leaving out-of-plane bending solely to dihedral hinges.
- `Sheet::reduce` builds the envelope, so `count()` is the final particle count the budget loop sees. `Budget { particles (High 4500 / Medium 3500 / Low 2500), piece_particles (1024 / 768 / 512), spacing }`: the loop widens the spacing (× 1.2 per round) of over-limit pieces only, largest first, so small pieces keep their resolution; the total follows the probe's cost (≈ 0.11 ms per 1000 particles). Subdivision and bow-tie copies are listed in `Envelope::blended`; `Proxy::blend` gives them halved normalized skin weights and `Proxy::spread_mass` spreads the gathered fabric area over envelope triangle area (subdivided particles have no homes).
- Hair: clumps (`Clump`) = index-connected islands (double-sided copies merged), geodesic bands (≥ spacing, ≥ median edge, ≤ 40 bands), band components → one node if round (≤ 12 cm) else farthest-point seeds; straightest-parent tree; each node = guide particle (even index) + offset particle (odd index).
- Cross links (`strands.rs` `cross_links`, appended in `constraints.rs` `add_cross_links` after the reach/parent/tether graph work) are `Structural` guide–guide edges that keep a bundle's cross-section: within a clump between same-band nodes (4 nearest), across clumps whose pinned roots lie within 2 cm (one tail) between nodes at similar geodesic depth (2 nearest per clump); 1–12 cm long, shortest first with at most `MAX_CROSS_LINK_DEGREE` (6) per guide, offsets and pinned nodes never cross-linked (links to other clumps' pins over-pin the root and fight body contacts).
- Render binds are `VertexBinding { target: BindTarget, gap }`: sheets use `BindTarget::Triangles` (≤ 3 `Influence`s in `Frame::from_triangle` frames over `particle_normals`); hair uses `BindTarget::Strand`, chain skinning over `Piece::strand_nodes`.
- `bind/` picks each sheet vertex's ≤ 3 influences from the 4 nearest proxy triangles by closest distance + 0.5 × centroid distance, evaluated at the vertex's same-distance weld centroid so crease duplicates share one influence set; weights `(1 − (dᵢ − d₀)/w)³` minus the 4th's weight. Offsets are exact (no depth clamp), so pleats, lining and frill are offsets of the envelope and rest is exact; `bind/patches.rs` still restricts a drawn component to the proxy components its housed vertices sit on. A drawn component whose closest point lies farther than `max(4 × spacing, 8 cm)` from the proxy stays skinned (`blend` 0); inside a component, vertices fade to skinning between half and all of that distance. `gap` = `min(rest body distance, 1 mm + 4 mm × (lift − lowest lift)/(lift range))`, so pushed layers keep their order.
- `Particle::render_offset` (`constraints.rs`) is the highest outward render lift (weighted offset along the frame normal, capped at 2 × spacing) of the binds whose nearest proxy triangle touches the particle, 0 for strands; the cloth solver adds it to the contact distance of outer layers against this piece. `Particle::clearance` treats an "inside" verdict deeper than 5 cm against an open body shell as a sign error (outside).
- `Particle::relief_height`/`relief_depth` (`relief.rs`) are the highest drawn point above and deepest below the proxy triangles touching the particle, measured at rest from drawn vertices and drawn-triangle centroids/midpoints that project onto a triangle face (16 nearest, ≤ 2 × spacing). After `layers.rs`, `underlay.rs` gives every triangle bind of a sheet with lower-layer sheets `VertexBinding::under` = the nearest inner-layer proxy triangle (face-closest preferred, ≤ 10 cm) of its drawn rest position; the cloth's drape uses both to keep outer drawn surfaces over inner ones.
- A `StrandNode` is one ribbon node: frame from guide (origin), offset particle (side) and parent→main-child guides (along). A `StrandBinding` weights ≤ `MAX_STRAND_NODES` (16) strand nodes; it moves its rest offset from the weighted guide axis by the weighted mean of knot turns (`StrandNode::turn` = frame now × rest frameᵀ, re-orthonormalized), so cross-sections stay rigid and rest is exact.
- Strand weights (`strands.rs` `StrandWeights`) weigh every node of the clump by geodesic distance (2.5 band widths) × sideways distance (12 cm), so laterally split chains render as one bundle; the strand node of guide `g` is `g / 2`. `Piece::drape` is the CPU reference the GPU drape must match (the GPU also presses face-up relief flat, which the CPU reference leaves out).
- `Preset::Skirt` (`preset.rs` `fn skirt`; `Preset::ALL` has 9 presets, unified without a separate soft-skirt variant) holds the modelled A-line silhouette without sagging while producing smooth real-cloth waves and S-curve lag: gravity 0.15, gravity_falloff 0.5, damping 0.03→0.013, drag 1.0, lift 0.6, compression 0.26, bend 0.27, buckling 0.30, waist-focused restore (0.035→0.005→0.001→0.0004), soft angle limit (`limit_stiffness` 0.45), depth-widening `backstop` (4 cm at waist → 6 cm at hem), attenuation 0.20, flutter 0.002, almost no carried motion (carried 0.03, carried_turn 0, depth_carried 0.06) and centrifugal 0.85.
- `Style::extent` (Skirt 0.84, Cape 0.85, others 0) is the share of each tether's rest pin distance a particle keeps (`EXTENT_HELD` 0.15 when still).
- Style semantics are only in code: gravity × scene gravity, curves over depth, `max_distance` × particle reach × spread, `limit` radians, stiffness-like values 0..1 per 1/60 s. Air forces (`drag`, `lift`) use the physical fabric density (0.25 kg/m²) while weight is scaled by `gravity` (0.5 for cloth), so lift above ≈ 0.1 lets narrow flaps kite sideways in a dance (Ribbon uses 0.1).
- The crate is public and GPU-free: apps and their model crates depend on it directly. Besides the asset it owns the solver vocabulary they need: `Wind`, `Grab`/`MAX_GRABS`, `Quality` (`Budget::from(quality)`; the cloth builder takes the same enum) and `Style`. Its only model dependencies are rikka-model-core and rikka-humanoid; surface names include `Model::material_aliases()` (a PMX material's English name).
