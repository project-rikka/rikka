- `rikka-png` (internal) is the one PNG codec, under rikka-image and rikka-text-raster, over `rikka-deflate`: `Decoder::{decode, rgba8, animation}(data, &Limits)` (native layout `Picture` with ICC and Exif bytes, straight RGBA8 into a reused buffer, APNG frames with their `FrameControl`), `probe(reader)` (size, layout, text chunks, Exif without inflating pixels) and `Encoder::new(Level).encode(Pixels, &Metadata, &mut out)` (adaptive filters, iCCP, tEXt/iTXt). CRCs of critical chunks are enforced, damaged ancillary chunks are skipped, and the inflate limit is the exact size the header implies. The `png` crate is only its tests' oracle.
- `rikka-image-resample` (internal; only rikka-simd) is the one CPU resampler: `Kernel::{Nearest, Bilinear, Bicubic, Lanczos3}` (`support`, `weight`, also the kernels of rikka-psd's affine `CpuResampler`), `Axis::{fit, place}` and `Resampler::resample::<T, C>(Job { x, y, footprint, threads }, target, load, store)`, a separable pass over f32 rows the caller loads and stores (pixel formats, premultiplication and transfer curves stay with the caller) that keeps only a ring of filtered rows.
- `Footprint::Filtered(kernel)` widens the kernel when shrinking and renormalizes at the borders (picture resizing); `Footprint::Coverage` is exact area coverage when shrinking and a tent when enlarging, fading outside the source (glyph strikes placed at fractional offsets). Results are bit-identical at every `rikka_simd::Level` and band count.
- `rikka-material-color` (internal, no dependencies) is the Material colour science behind rikka-theme and rikka-theme-seed: `Rgb`, `Hct::{of, solve}` (CAM16 hue and chroma, L* tone), `TonalPalette::{new, from_hct}.tone(t)`, `harmonize`, `Scheme::new(source, Variant, dark, contrast)` (primary, error, the six surfaces, on-surface, on-surface-variant, outline, outline-variant for the nine variants), `quantize(pixels, max)` (Wu boxes refined by weighted k-means in Lab) and `score(colours)`.
- Its arithmetic keeps material-color-utilities' operation order (fused multiply-adds included); `tests/oracle.rs` asserts exact agreement with the `material-colors` crate, which is only that test's oracle.
- `rikka-barcode` (public) reads 1D/2D codes and writes QR codes through nixpkgs zxing-cpp's C API (hand-written FFI linking `ZXing`): `read` for scenes, `read_framed` enlarges small crops and restores a missing quiet zone; `QrCode::encode(text)` gives level-M modules and `rasterize(module)` (pixels per module) a black-on-white RGBA `QrImage` with the 4-module quiet zone, the one QR drawing every app uses.
