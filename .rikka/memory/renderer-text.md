- Text nodes carry a `rikka_text::TextPaint` (a caller-made `Arc<TextLayout>` in logical px, inks, scale); the renderer never shapes or breaks lines. Glyphs are placed with `LayoutRow::placed(origin, scale)` (quarter-pixel x bins, pixel-snapped baseline) and coloured by whole-text byte offset at emit; underline and strikethrough come from the rows' decorations. Atlas keys are `AtlasKey::Text(GlyphKey) | Icon(IconKey) | Curves(CurveKey)`, chosen per glyph by `text/routing.rs`; colour glyphs arrive premultiplied. The glyph atlas adds pages instead of growing; glyphs held by retained nodes are reference-counted and never evicted, unreferenced ones age out. Routing decides each glyph's raster scale (colour glyphs rastered smaller are drawn up), carried on the held `ImageSlot`.
- `edit.text(paint)` with the same `Arc<TextLayout>` and scale but other inks (streamed-text fades) damages only the rows covering `Inks::differing` (plus one font size of reach), not the whole node; anything else damages the node's bounds.
- Text bands (`draw/emit.rs`): a kept text node emits the rows inside its clips (`shown_band`) plus, at rest, neighbouring rows within 256 px whose glyphs are already resident (`ahead_band`); a group shift re-emits it only when the shown rows leave that band (`Band::covers`). Rows within 384 px (`ahead_band`) not yet looked at are drawn ahead on the workers. A re-emitted node whose routes are unchanged keeps its `GlyphRefs` (routes and atlas refs per node) without touching the atlas.
- Mask pages are R8 sampled nearest (glyphs sit on whole pixels); colour pages are premultiplied RGBA8 sampled linear. Colour glyphs raster at most 256 px and are scaled up bilinearly.
- Curve path: outline glyphs above 64 px, and every outline glyph of a text node whose `TextPaint::scale` changed, draw as `KIND_GLYPH_CURVES` quads (bbox + 1 px) from rikka-text-raster curve blobs. Blobs live in `text/curve_store.rs`: one storage buffer (binding 4 of the per-frame primitive set), a CPU mirror and first-fit word ranges. Atlas and curve store share `text/residency.rs` (`Residency`: entries with refs and last use, hold/release, empties, the KEEP/SWEEP sweep and a cached oldest-first victim ranking) and `text/pending_uploads.rs` (pending/sent uploads, commit, revert); growth makes a new buffer and copies the old one into it on the GPU (whole mirror only after a reverted frame). `shaders/lib/canvas/glyph_curves.slang` is the port of rikka-text-raster's `coverage_at`.
- Zoom tracking (`draw/zooms.rs`, `Phase::{Moving, Converting, Settled}`): a text node draws from curves while its scale changes and for 4 still frames after; then it converts to the atlas only once all its atlas glyphs are resident (missing ones are drawn ahead), after 30 more frames synchronously. Meanwhile `Scene::restage_settling` (after the frame takes damage) re-marks it as changed content and damages it, so it is emitted again and frames keep coming. `Scene::is_animating()` says a scene still owes such frames; rikka-ui's embed painter reports it for its mirror scene and the embedder presents once more per frame callback until it clears.
- Glyph holding (`text/glyph_holder.rs`, `GlyphHolder`): every missing glyph is drawn before the frame (`GlyphWorkers::draw_all`, shared with the workers from 12 keys). Holding works per unique route (key, scale), adding refs in bulk with `add_refs`; `GlyphRef { key, slot }` records each glyph's slot.
- Coverage: SDR targets are UNORM, so blending happens on sRGB-encoded values. Mask, icon and curve coverage is blended as-is, with no gamma or contrast remap.
- The glyph atlas is shared behind its own small lock: look up and hold, raster outside the lock, then place and stage. Glyphs are held from placement until the frame is recorded; new pages reserve a slot and are bound at record. Failed frames requeue their glyph copies.
- `text/workers.rs` (`GlyphWorkers`, in `Shared`): up to three lazily started `rikka-glyphs` threads, each with its own `GlyphRaster`. A preparer missing a dozen or more glyphs splits them across the workers (urgent jobs) and draws a share itself; glyphs drawn ahead (`draw_ahead`, registered with `GlyphAtlas::begin_warming`) are inserted into the atlas by the worker, unheld; a refused insert marks the key so nobody waits on it.
