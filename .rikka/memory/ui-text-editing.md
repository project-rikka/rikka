- `TextDocument` (rope with caret, selection, IME, undo) backs both `text_field` and `text_editor`; one engine lives in `rikka-widgets/src/text/editing`. Its `Content`, `Rope`, `Splice`/`Place`, `LineEnding`, `Travel` and `Erase` live in `rikka-text-document` (text-document note) and are re-exported by `rikka_ui::text`.
- Word steps and double-click words come from `rikka_text`'s windowed boundary helpers over the rope (it implements `TextSource`) under a `rikka_text_unicode::WordRule`: the engine passes `WordRule::Code` when the editor is `.code(true)` or has a `.language(..)`, every other field and figure typing `WordRule::Prose`.
- Pointer selection is granular (`Granularity::from_click_count`: Character, Word, Line; secret fields treat Word as Line, single-line fields' Line is the whole text). The pressed unit becomes a `SelectionOrigin` (rikka-text-document); drags and Shift+click extend through `SelectionOrigin::extend(unit)`, so the origin unit always stays selected. The engine keeps a Word/Line origin as `editing/gesture.rs` `Gesture`, valid only while the content's id, revision and endpoints are unchanged (otherwise it extends by character from the anchor); selectable rich text keeps it in its `selection::Session`.
- A hit's `Affinity` names the grapheme under the pointer (`After`: the one starting at the offset, `Before`: the one ending there), so `word_range_at(text, index, affinity, rule)` and `Content::unit_at` pick the word actually clicked.
- `text::KeyEdit::from_key(key, modifiers, multiline)` is the one key-to-edit table (`Travel`, `Erase`, `Newline`, `Tab`, `Type`), applied through `TextDocument::travel(to, extend, caret_line, rule)`/`erase(erase, rule)`. `caret_line` is the `ParagraphLayout` of the caret's line (from the template kept from the last paint; figure typing takes `Typeset::paragraph`), or `None` for secret or composing lines, which move logically.
- Every key, Up/Down/PageUp/PageDown included, is applied when it arrives, so keys within one frame keep their order. Clicks, context-menu presses (`place_cursor_unless_selected`) and Up/Down keep the hit's affinity through `place_cursor`; carets are drawn with `DisplayText::affinity()`. `TextDocument` setters notify only when something changed.
- The engine draws through `rikka_ui::text::DisplayText` (the document plus composition, or bullets for `.secret`); every drawn-text cache keys on `DisplayText::revision()` (text, composition, secret mode).
- Text measure, selection and drawing share one `Arc<TextLayout>` (`rikka_text::layout`, `layout_approx`, `recent_layout`, `measure_text`, `draw_laid_text`); a text leaf's `Leaf` keeps the layouts it measured and `draw_text` paints from them when the layout `serves_width`. Before every solve dirty text leaves are pre-laid on the helper pool (`task/helpers.rs`, which also runs `in_background` jobs), skipping widths already laid.
- Selection bands come from `TextLayout::selection_rects(range)` in laid coordinates; carets from `TextLayout::caret`.
- `text_editor`: `.fits_text(range)` (measured live through `MeasureSpec::document`), `.language(..)`, `.edits(Edits)` gutter, `.search(&Search)`, `.place(&Place)`. Wrapped-row counts are a sum tree updated from splices; a new width recounts lines near the reader and a helper thread settles the rest, keeping the top visible line in place.
- The engine (`text/editing/block.rs`) lays text in fixed 16-line pieces over a band one page beyond the view (kept up to two pages), re-lays a piece only when its own text, spans or style changed, and draws inside `ctx.scrolled` sections, one `ctx.part` per piece, so a scroll frame re-emits nothing. Geometry is in sheet coordinates (`Frame::sheet_top`, `origin`).
- `text_editor(..).code(true)` turns on code assists (`text/editing/assist`): bracket and quote pairs with step-over, surround and pair backspace, indent detected from the file, smart Enter, Ctrl+/ comments, and completion from `rikka_syntax::Lexicon` in a `topmost` popup placed by `drop_panel` plus a ghost of the chosen word; Tab/Enter accept, Esc dismisses.
- A ghost is shown text not in the document (`Content::set_ghost`, valid only for the revision and caret it was set at, hidden by a composition), drawn in the hint colour.
- A focused editor always reports a caret to the IME (parked at the area edge when scrolled out), because a missing caret disables the IME. Only IME events that change the document follow the caret.
- Carets glide with `motion::CARET` in sheet coordinates (scrolls and window moves never glide) while the IME hears the final place. Every caret fades through `rikka_ui::text::Blink::alpha` (1060 ms period), which wakes at the end of each steady phase and asks for frames only while fading.
- Single-line cells go through `rows::draw_cell` (clip plus ellipsis). `no_wrap` `text` and `rich_text` shrink with an ellipsis decided at layout (`TextRun::ellipsized`).
- Clipboard: `rikka_ui::clipboard::{text, set_text, text_of, set_text_of, has_text, has_files, files, read, publish, mime_types, watch, Selection}`. Runtimes call `clipboard::install_session(session)` with any `rikka_clipboard_core::Session` (the Wayland `rikka_clipboard::Clipboard` is one), so rikka-ui links no Wayland; without one an in-process `Offer` per selection is the clipboard. Widgets paste through `clipboard::read_text`/`read_text_of(selection, handler)`, a blocking job answered on the UI thread.
- `rich_text(..)` and `markdown(..)` can be `.selectable(key)`: runs with the same key select as one region and copy as prose; links follow on release; selection is not a tab stop. `Gather::new(region)` grows one whole per build; `Gather::shared(&SharedWhole::for_region(region), from)` lets runs built at different times read one replaceable whole. A held selection survives appends and is dropped when earlier text changes.
- `markdown` over a few KB builds per block (`deferred`), each digested by its own source and the reference definitions; a source extending a recently parsed one re-parses from the second-to-last block. `markdown_text` is the text as read, for search and copy.
