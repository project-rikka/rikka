- Public API: `Endpoint { endpoint, token, server, channel: ChannelId, group, user: UserId, session }` (Debug hides token and session; `group` is the DAVE group: the voice channel, or `rtc_server_id - 1` for streams), `Role::{Voice, Broadcast, Watch}` and `Transport` (`open(endpoint, role, VoiceSink)`, `send`, `speaking`, `send_video`, `show_video(Option<VideoShape>)`, `refresh(source)`, `close`, close on drop).
- `Role::Voice` keeps the old audio-only identify and codec list. Stream roles identify with `video: true` and one `screen` stream, offer every video decoder (`discord_rtp::Payload`: AV1 109, H265 103, H264 101, VP8 105, VP9 107, RTX = +1) and encode only H.264; `Broadcast` speaks with the SOUNDSHARE flag and stays announced while video is shown.
- `open` spawns one `rikka-discord-rtc` thread running a current-thread Tokio runtime; it resolves through a oneshot once the session description arrived, so it works from any executor. Dropping the `open` future ends the session.
- `send`/`speaking` only push into `outbox.rs` (16 frames, drop oldest; `speaking(false)` queues five F8 FF FE frames); video keeps at most 8 frames and drops the backlog whole, reporting `KeyframeWanted`. The session drains it at the top of every loop turn.
- One nonce counter per connection seals every RTP and RTCP packet (`Cipher::seal_rtp`/`seal_rtcp`); RTCP keeps its first 8 bytes clear, and RTP padding is cut after decryption.
- Sending video (`session/broadcast.rs`): `prepare_h264` rewrites SPS VUI (bitstream restriction, no reorder; filler NALs are already gone, dropped by `rikka_video::VideoPackets` and rikka-vulkan-video; Annex B units and RBSP bit coding come from `rikka-annexb`) before DAVE so receivers' rewrite is a no-op, DAVE `encrypt_video`, H.264 STAP-A/FU-A packetization (1100-byte payloads), Discord's native one-byte extension ids on every video packet (`video/extensions.rs`: 5 transport-wide sequence, 6 playout delay 0/0, 7 screenshare content type, 11/12 RID "100"; the older id-5 playout-delay mapping is rejected), a `rikka_rate_control::Pacer` at 2.5× the target that holds plaintext and seals at release so nonces and transport sequences leave in order (the session sleeps until `wake_at`, no fixed tick), RTX history for NACKs, PLI/FIR → `KeyframeWanted`, RR loss and REMB into `rikka_rate_control::LossBased` → `Bandwidth` when it moves 5 %, server sink wants → `Wanted`, sender reports every second, plus a debug-level per-second tally (frames, unwanted, unsealed, packets, repairs, PLIs). `keyframe_interval` from op 4 and op 14 reaches the broadcaster as `VoiceEvent::KeyframeEvery`.
- Watching (`session/watch.rs` over `discord_rtp::VideoReceiver`): op 12 maps owner SSRCs (RTX → primary), per-source `Assembler`s reorder, NACK with RTT-based retries, wait for keyframes and give up on gaps after 1 s; depacketizers mirror libwebrtc's output byte-for-byte (4-byte start codes, AV1 OBUs with sizes) so DAVE `decrypt_video` authenticates; receiver reports every second and media sink wants `any: 100`.
- Layers: the app-private sans-IO crate `discord-rtp` (over rikka-annexb and rikka-rate-control) holds RTP/RTCP (`Header`, `Fields`, `parse_rtcp`, report/NACK/PLI builders), `Cipher` (AES-256-GCM preferred, XChaCha20; rtpsize seal/open), payload types, IP discovery, `VoiceStream`, and video (`VideoSender` with RTX history, `VideoReceiver`, `packetize_h264`, `prepare_h264`, `depacketize`), tested with RFC and known-answer vectors. discord-rtc keeps `gateway/` (JSON and binary frames, codec offer; user ids arrive typed as `UserId`), `dave/` (wraps `davey::DaveSession` as input → replies), `session/` (WebSocket, UDP, heartbeat, resume, events) and `roster` (SSRCs → `UserId`s).
- DAVE follows discord.js's DAVESession semantics: passthrough while the group is not ready, protocol frames (FA FA marker) dropped until joined, 36 consecutive failures trigger invalid-commit + new key package.
- Close codes: below 4000 and 4015 resume (op 7 with `seq_ack`), 4014/4022 end with `Ended(None)`, anything else ends with an error; before `open` resolves every failure is returned from `open`.
