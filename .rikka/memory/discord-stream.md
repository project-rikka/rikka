---
title: Discord Go Live media engine (discord-stream)
description: discord-stream turns a Cast into a paced H.264 broadcast and a watched stream into Feed pictures plus mixed sound.
about: apps/system/discord/crates/discord-stream
tags: [discord, video]
---

- Public API: `Broadcaster::start(discord_client::Broadcast, Cast, Option<AudioCapture>, Quality, thumbnail: impl Fn(u32, u32, Vec<u8>))` with `pause`, `set_quality`, `updates()`, `end()`; `Watcher::start(discord_client::Watch, StreamSound, picture: impl Fn(u32, u32, Vec<u8>))` with `set_volume`, `fit(w, h)`, `updates()`, `leave()`; `Quality { Resolution, FrameRate }` (serde) and `StreamUpdate`.
- `Quality::fit` shrinks the source to the chosen height (never past 3840x2160, even sides); `bitrate` is 0.06 bits per pixel per frame, 1–40 Mbit/s. There is no Nitro gating.
- `rikka-discord-stream-send` builds its own `rikka_gpu::Gpu` (no renderer) and, when `VideoPackets::takes_dmabufs` (Vulkan Video H.264), asks the `Cast` for dmabufs (`offer_dmabufs`); dmabuf frames are imported once per PipeWire buffer (`Previewer::import`, cached by `BufferKey`) and coded by `VideoPackets` with `Feed::Dmabuf` (in-place `set_bitrate`, `request_keyframe`), memory frames by `VideoPackets` with `Feed::Memory` (FFmpeg, hardware first, software last); a failed import or GPU encode offers shm again and streams from memory. It paces captured frames to the frame rate, opens a coder per source size and feed (in-band parameter sets, keyframes on request), announces `VideoShape` before frames, forces a keyframe every server `keyframe_interval` (2 s without one), repeats the last frame after 500 ms of stillness and on `KeyframeWanted`, follows `Bandwidth`, and stops encoding while `Wanted(0)` (nobody watches). `Showing` reports the captured size as soon as frames arrive, watched or not; ten seconds without any captured frame ends the stream with an error.
- It also emits a JPEG `StreamUpdate::Preview` after 3 s and every 5 min for the app to upload, and hands a 640x360 frame to the `thumbnail` sink every 200 ms (dmabuf frames shrink on the GPU through `Previewer::preview`) (the app forwards both sinks to its `Feed`s; the crate does not depend on rikka-ui) (`THUMBNAIL_INTERVAL`).
- The send thread is a `rikka_worker::Worker` over a command channel (`Pause`, `Quality`, `Stop`); one `wait_readable` covers the commands, the `Cast`'s frame fd and the `VoiceEvents` (polled with the command channel's waker), until the next domain deadline (the 500 ms repeat or the 10 s no-picture limit).
- Sound rides beside it: `discord_voice::Soundshare` encodes the `AudioCapture` onto the same broadcast line.
- `rikka-discord-stream-watch` is a `rikka_worker::Worker` over a command channel (`Fit`, `Stop`) that polls the stream connection's events with the channel's waker and blocks on its eventfd: video from the owner's source goes through `LiveDecoder` (opened per codec) into the `Feed`, decode failures call `WatchLine::refresh` (picture-loss request), and Opus packets go to the `StreamSound` mixed by the call's `Voice`.
- The send thread works on `Cast` and `BroadcastLine`, the watch thread on `WatchLine` and `StreamSound`, directly; only `Quality::fit` is covered by tests.
