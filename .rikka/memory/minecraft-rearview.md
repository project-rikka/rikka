- `RearviewMod` (`rearview`, OverlayHudMod, 256×144 HUD units, default top right) requests a frame in `extract(HudCanvas)` and draws the target as a feathered rounded `GuiMesh` with its own GUI pipeline (`core/rearview.fsh` ignores texture alpha, since world passes leave alpha < 1). Render targets are GL-oriented: image top is v = 1.
- The HUD extract sizes the target to the physical pixels it covers × `resolution` and allocates it there, never during render, because this frame's GUI already references the view. Nothing renders when the HUD was not extracted (F1, no level). `FramePacer` caps the rate; a new target forces a frame.
- `RearviewHooks.levelDrawn` runs from `GameRendererMixin` right after `LevelRenderer.render`. `RearviewRenderer` builds its own `CameraRenderState` and `RearviewCamera` (a `Camera` subclass) at the eye looking back (yaw + 180), with a reverse-Z render projection and a culling projection whose far plane is the mirror distance. Fog is the main fog with render distance and sky end shortened to that distance.
- Reused vanilla parts: terrain sections from the shared occlusion octree (`SectionOcclusionGraph.addSectionsInFrustum`), drawn by `prepareChunkRenders(Indirect)` with the section list overridden through the `RearviewLevel` duck on `LevelRenderer`. Entities, block entities and break cracks come from `LevelExtractor` invokers. Submission goes through `rikka$submitFeatures` into its own `SubmitNodeStorage`, plus its own `SkyRenderer`, `FogRenderer`, `GlobalSettingsUniform` and `ProjectionMatrixBuffer`. Clouds and weather replay the main frame's prepared buffers, translated by main camera − eye. Transparency is always classic, even when the main view uses OIT.
- Particle group render states are shared objects that only append, so the mirror resets the main `ParticlesRenderState` (already consumed) before extracting, and resets its own after its feature frame closes; otherwise mirror particles leak into the next main frame.
- Vanilla compiles and resorts only sections in the main view's lists. The renderer registers its lists with `rikka$followSections`. `LevelExtractorMixin` joins them into the dirty-section scan in `extract`, and `LevelRendererMixin` joins them into `scheduleTranslucentSectionResort`. Both are cleared when the view area is rebuilt.
- Every RenderSystem state the pass touches (projection, fog, lights, globals, model view, `isRenderingLevel`) is saved and restored. A render failure disables the mod. `onDisable` detaches the lists at once and defers GPU closing with `Minecraft.schedule` (`execute` runs inline on the render thread), after the frame that may still sample the target. Resizes reuse the `TextureTarget` object, so its `SkyRenderer` survives them.
