- Each emitter is `Subject::Emitter(EmitterId)`, listed after the figures; `Cast` keeps `EmitterTrack`s (emitter `Arc` + `Sheet::fluid`) sorted by id and `fresh_emitter` never reuses an id.
- Add Liquid (Edit menu, water-drop bar button) places a Water/Stream/Ballistic emitter in front of the chosen or first figure (`stage::fluid::Placing`), else where the view looks, pouring from the playhead for `Pour::LENGTH`. Remove (bar ×), duplicate, rename and every structural edit (shape, aim, anchors, pour span, liquid formula, resting values) are `Edit::Replaced` snapshots (`update::<Emitter>`); replay re-chooses an emitter that comes back.
- Keys are `Key::Fluid` over `Lane::Fluid(EmitterSetting)`; `SubjectMotion::Emitter` makes paste, shift, delete and curve bending (`Channel::OF_VALUES`, the `KeyKind::Fluid` default curve) work as for looks.
- Edit pattern: changing an unkeyed setting sets its resting value at once; changing a keyed one holds it in `Drafts::pour` (one emitter, one frame, dropped on a new frame or playback) until Register keys it; the operations are the generic `SettingSubject` ones shared with wind. Register keys held settings plus the chosen lane, else every row.
- Pour timing: `animate/timing.rs` (retime, start/end at playhead); the inspector's Emitting rows (`animate-inspector` `inspector/liquid/timing.rs`: start frame, duration in seconds, span and litres); `Sheet::fluid` carries a `Pouring` (`Sheet::pouring`), so the dope sheet shows a `Row::Pour` bar right after the sound row (`animate-timeline`: `layout::pour_row`, read cheaply by `Timeline::pour_row` for hover and cursor; `dope/pour.rs`: grips, `PourDrag` with playhead snap) whose body moves and edges trim, reported once on release through `Dope`'s `on_pour`.
- `animate/liquids.rs` (subjects, settings push), `pouring.rs` (values, keys), `shaping.rs` (structure, formula) on the app side; `animate-inspector` `inspector/liquid/` draws the emitter, emission, liquid and scene cards on the Motion tab above the curve editor.
- `animate-stage` `fluid/` (with the drags and paint in `animate-viewport` `fluid/`): `Liquids` (all emitters + `FluidSettings`, set by `Stage::set_emitters` on every refresh), `Aiming` (built each frame for the chosen emitter: state, placed launch/target with their anchor frames, flight path, heading, every shown bone as a `Joint`), `SpotDrag`/`Gizmo` (drag in the view-facing plane via easy `Follow`, Shift pushes, Ctrl-drag drops onto the nearest bone to attach with zero offset or detaches to World) and `paint`.
- Hooks: `Stage.liquids`, `Stage::emitter_state(emitter, time)` (sample plus unkeyed values), `Stage::locate(figure, bone)` and `Stage::locator()` (bone worlds at any frame, physics-free, used to keep keyed spots in place when an anchor changes). A fluid `.rmotion` dropped while an emitter is chosen replaces it (`LoadRequest.emitter`), others join as new emitters.
- `FluidSettings` persist in `rikka.animate` through `preferences::Preferences` (`liquid-*` keys beside `output-*`); scene sliders draft (`Controls::scene_draft`) and set on release.

## Simulation (`animate-stage` `pour/`)

- Animate keeps only what is its own; the bake, display, scene, mixing and emitter physics are the shared `rikka-physics` crate (physics-layer note).
- `pour/liquid_scene.rs`: `LiquidScene` (figures as `CastMember`s with their `TakePlayback`, `Liquids`) implements `rikka_physics::SceneSource` (imported as `SceneSource`): its `Layout` from `FluidSettings` (floor 0, drying, figures keyed by `FigureId::key`), one `LiquidEmitter` per emitter (Surface emitters name their figure by index), `pose` through `stage::actor::perform`, `spouts` through `pour/emitter_state.rs` (samples the emitter at the step, locates bones on the scene's figures, litres to m³, a blob burst at its pour start). `Render::update_liquid_scene(hint)` pushes a new one whenever the stage re-sources it (add_figure/dismiss, the physics switch, emitters, wind and `pose(.., changed)`, hints merged by earliest step) and when cloth assets change; `LiquidScene::changed_from` gives the first stale step: emitter key and pour edits from `Emitter::changed_from`, motion edits from `Take::changed_from`, anything structural from 0.
- `pour/grid.rs`: `GRID` is the 60 Hz `StepRate`, 2 steps per motion frame (`step_of_frame`, `frame_at_step`).
- `pour::bake(on_progress)` starts the `rikka-animate-pour` `Bake` (3 GiB cache, checkpoint every 30 steps); `Render.liquid` is a `Playback`: `Baked` in the viewport, `Simulated` in `stage::replica` (export and assistant captures), so exports match the viewport step for step.
- Figures draw `.wet(..)` (by `FigureId::key`) only while wetness is on; `SceneFrame::draw_fluid` gets `LiquidScene::materials()`, mixed once per scene. Failures become `StageError::Liquid` notices; `Animate::on_stage_notified` feeds `Timeline::set_liquid(Stage::liquid_cache())` (an `animate_cast::LiquidCache` of frame ranges), drawn as a band under the ruler (cached ranges, faint track to `reach` while working).
- Commands: Physics menu Compute Liquids (`Command::Liquids(bool)`, pauses the bake) and Recompute Liquids (clears the cache).
- The default particle size 0.004 m keeps typical scenes above the 60 steps/s the step rate needs; 0.003 drops slime, puddles and paint to ~55. Threads (lotion between palms) need 0.002.
