- Two depth atlases: `ShadowAtlas` (main, ≤ `LARGEST_ATLAS` 4096²) holds cascades and local lights; `ShadowAtlas::figures` (`FigureAtlas`, ≤ `LARGEST_FIGURE_ATLAS` 4096², 64 MB D32) holds figure and turned-key maps, so figures never take cascade resolution. `ShadowView::atlas` (`Atlas::{Main, Figures}`) picks the atlas; figure slots carry `FIGURE` and shaders read `RikkaView.figure_atlas` for them (`RikkaShadowTile.image`).
- `shadow/plan.rs` `Plan::size` makes requests and `fit_budget` per atlas halves the least important tile down to 128, then drops it. Main priorities: cascades (group 0) before local lights ranked by reach/distance. Figure priorities: 1 / camera distance to `Figure::head`. `Plan::place` reserves cached tiles, packs the rest largest first into each atlas's buddy `AtlasAllocator` (`Allocators { main, figures }`), then lays views.
- Figure maps fit the light-space box of the figure's visible part spheres (`figure_box` in `frusta::light_turn` space), rectangular ortho with power-of-1/8-octave quantised extents and texel-snapped centres (`frusta::figure_view`). A figure with a head gets a second view at the same tile size around the head (radius × 1.4): the body view carries `DETAILED`, the head view follows it, and `rikka_shadow_figure` blends head→body over 0.8–0.95 of the head tile.
- The figure pass is `rikka.three.figure_shadow` into the figure atlas (cleared on load, no fluid, no tint); `rikka.three.shadow` draws main views and the tint passes skip figure views. Fog ignores figure slots.
- Biases scale with world texel size (`frusta::biases`: depth bias and a 2.5-texel normal offset); the filters scale the normal and toward-light offsets by the receiver's slope and add a depth bias proportional to the kernel radius.
- `GpuShadowSlot` (128 B) carries `figure`, `channels`, `flags` (`PERSPECTIVE`, `FIGURE`, `WITHOUT_MAPPED_FIGURES`, `CACHED`, `TINTED`, `DETAILED`), `near`, `projection_scale`, `depth_range`, `world_texel`. Cull's `casts_into` applies channels, figure-only views and the exclusion of mapped figures from the leading light's cascades.
- Ray-traced shadows replace maps per light: `rikka_shadow_evaluate`/`rikka_shadow_hard` ask `rikka_shadow_traced` first, which covers lights whose `light.shadow` is one of `RikkaTrace.shadow_lights` (≤ 4, deduplicated by shadow index). Unturned keys share the leading light's shadow and trace with it. In the RT tier forward/hull fragments never reach the maps for those lights (traced shading variants, see the ray tracing note); maps still serve untraced lights, fluids, scatter and devices without RT.
- RT shadow tier without fluid layers: `plan::Traced::of` (decided before `plan_shadows`, counting shadowing lights like `traced_lights`) drops the leading light's `Source::Figure` maps (so cascades keep figures and `figure_shadow` is skipped) and the maps of turned keys that fit among the four traced lights; such a key's `light.shadow` is `UNMAPPED_SHADOW | key index` (`rikka_shadow_mapped` is false, the tracer still matches it). Overflowed turned keys and frames with fluids keep their figure maps.
- Tap rotation: `RikkaView.shadow_noise` is 0 when temporal antialiasing is off or settled (stable pattern) and `frame % 64 + 1` otherwise; `RikkaShadowPoint.rotation` (interleaved gradient noise per pixel) spins PCF/PCSS taps (`RikkaShadowNoise`).
- Caching: local lights whose `light_key` and caster hash match last frame keep their main-atlas tiles (`ShadowCache`, reset on regrow or when tint images appear); no caching while fluid layers draw.
- Coloured shadows: tint shadow classes (`build.rs` `shadow_class_for`: transparent casters that do not write depth, i.e. glass and transmission) draw `rikka.three.shadow_tint_depth` and `rikka.three.shadow_tint` at half resolution into `ShadowAtlas::tint` with main-atlas uvs; they only scale light (`light.shadow.filter`), never the map visibility.
- Depth-writing transparent casters (PMX materials, VRM TransparentWithZWrite) are masked casters: alpha-tested at the material's cutoff into the depth maps (main and figure atlases), so they self-shadow and shadow the floor like solids. `tests/three/lighting.rs` `depth_writing_transparent_casters_block_the_shadow_map` guards it.
- Facial features: in looked frames, draws whose layers match `Characters::facial_layers` (default `SEE_THROUGH_LAYER`, toon Eye/Lash) on a figure with a head get `instance_flags::FACIAL`; `rikka_shadow_receiver` (`material/context.slang`) tests their shadows 0.25 head radii along the head forward (raster maps and an inline RT trace, never the RT image), and `rikka_illuminate` drops GTAO, bounce and contact shadows for them, so recessed eyes shade like the face surface over them.
- Filtering: looked frames use PCSS (directional from `Light::source_size`, local perspective PCSS from the light's size); unlooked frames and size-0 locals use Castaño's 9-tap tent PCF. Cube faces use a 1.08 margin.
- `RikkaShadowQuery`: `sample()` → `RikkaShadowSample { visibility, blocker, penumbra, receiver, tint, blocked }` (raw values for toon ramps), `map()`, `screen()`, `visibility()`, `transmittance()`, `hard()`.
- Screen shadows (`space/screen_shadows.rs`, `space_screen_shadow.slang`): one compute pass per sector of 64-pixel wavefronts along lines through the light's screen point; every pixel is written exactly once (no clear pass): each thread owns the pixels between its row's and the previous row's line crossing, computed in 1/64-px fixed point (`crossing`, int64) so neighbouring rows agree bit-exactly, sectors split by integer `in_sector`, the light point is clamped to `FAR_AWAY` and quantized to 1/64 px on the CPU, and fans start one row below and end one row past the sector edge. A block's first row owns everything from the sector's lower diagonal (`sector_floor`) up, because near the light the fan's lines converge and the row below would leave the diagonal pixels unowned. Any unowned pixel keeps stale memory, so renders depend on what the viewport drew before (`screen_shadows_match_in_fresh_and_used_viewports` guards it). Back-facing or sky pixels (`dot(normal, toward) <= 0`) write lit without marching. Cost is per pixel (~110 µs at 922 kpx, ~250 µs at 1080p on an RTX 4060).
