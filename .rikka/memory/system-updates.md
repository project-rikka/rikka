---
title: System updates: runs, live logs, notifications and the viewer
description: rikka-nixctl records runs with decoded nix logs; the shell notifies; rikka-updates shows live and past logs.
about: crates/nix
tags: [nix, notifications]
---

- Every Apply/Update/Rollback is a `Run` (`RunId` = start ms, `Operation`, `RunState::{Running, Done, Failed, Interrupted}`, `Status { phase: Phase, tally: Tally }`). `rikka-nixctl` returns the `RunId` from the method call.
- `rikka-nixctl` runs `nixos-rebuild … --no-reexec --log-format internal-json` (and `nix flake update` with the same format); `--no-reexec` keeps exactly one Realise per run so phases are exact.
- `rikka-nix-log::Decoder` turns nix internal-json lines into readable lines (`name> line` for build logs, levels ≤ info), mirrors nix's progress-bar aggregation in `Tally` (builds, paths, copied, downloaded `Count`s; `fraction()`), reports `Realisation` and keeps the error `raw_msg` and a short plain-output tail for the failure reason.
- `rikka-nix-journal::Journal` stores runs in `$LOGS_DIRECTORY` (`LogsDirectory=rikka-nixctl`, 0700): `<id>.json` (`rikka_nix_protocol::RunRecord`, the same struct as on D-Bus) and `<id>.log`. It keeps `KEPT_RUNS`, marks runs left running as interrupted on open, rejects malformed records without failing, caps lines and transcripts, and pages logs (`PAGE_BYTES`) only from line starts.
- D-Bus `os.rikka.Nix1`: methods `Apply(a(sss) module/base/contents, s action)`, `Update`, `Rollback` → `t`, `Runs → a(RunRecord)`, `Transcript(run, from) → PageRecord`; signals `Started(RunRecord)`, `Lines(run, from, as)` (batched every 100 ms), `Status(run, StatusRecord)`, `Finished(RunRecord)`. A line's offset advances by its UTF-8 length + 1.
- Clients: `rikka_nix::Monitor::watch(Interest, report)` subscribes before it returns (`Interest::Progress` matches Started/Status/Finished only, `Interest::Transcript` adds `Lines`) and reports `Event::Exited` when the service leaves and `Event::Disconnected` when the bus connection ends; it follows the signals with `rikka_bus::Signals::hear` on its own system-bus connection (no thread; `report` runs on that connection's executor, so it must not block or call the bus), and dropping the last `Monitor` clone ends the watch, so keep it; then `runs()`, `running()` (no activation when the service is absent) and `transcript()`. `Tail::accept(from, lines)` drops overlap between pages and live `Lines` and reports a `Gap` to refetch. Signals are trusted only from the current owner of the name.
- Wire names, records and the run vocabulary are `crates/nix/rikka-nix-protocol` (used by `rikka-nix`, `rikka-nix-journal` and `rikka-nixctl`; `rikka-nix` re-exports the vocabulary): `Operation::{name, action}`, `Phase::{name, from_name}`, `Status::fraction()` (building only), `Run::interrupt(at)`, `RunId::from(SystemTime)`, `Outcome::refusal()` (refusals worth showing, `Denied` excluded).
- The shell's `shell-system-update` follows `Monitor` and posts one tagged notification per run: resident with progress while running (updated silently), then the result (critical on failure); click or "Show log" spawns `rikka-updates --run <id>`.
- `apps/system/updates` (`rikka-updates`, single instance) lists runs and shows the selected one's header, progress and a read-only `text_editor` log that pages in, then appends live (`TextDocument::append`) and follows the end unless scrolled away.
- Manual end-to-end without root: a TCP private dbus-daemon with ANONYMOUS auth as `DBUS_SYSTEM_BUS_ADDRESS`, a stand-in polkit, and `rikka-nixctl` under `unshare -rm` with `/etc/nixos` bind-mounted to a copy and `NIX_REMOTE=daemon`.
