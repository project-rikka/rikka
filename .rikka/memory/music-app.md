- `music-core`: items (`Track`/`Album` carry `fidelity: Option<Fidelity>` — `Codec` (stable `as_str`/`from_slug`), lossless, `Resolution`, kbps, DSD rate; `Fidelity::new`, `dsd_multiple`, `is_high`), pages (`Home` of `Shelf`s, incl. service-neutral RecentlyPlayed/Following/NewReleases/Acclaimed/MostPlayed/Curated/RecentlyAdded/Favourites), `Queue` (`following()` for gapless), `Clock`, `PlaybackEvent` (received as `Events`, a `rikka_worker::Subscription`; senders hold a `rikka_worker::Broadcast<PlaybackEvent>`), `drain` (blocks a thread draining a stream; `blocking.rs`, park + `std::task::Wake`), `PlayCounter` (play-count rule, fed by `observe`), `SavedAccount` (the `rikka_account_book::Record` of the music book), `fold` (the one text fold for library search and cross-service matching), `Deck`, and the audio cache. It depends on rikka-audio-decode and rikka-hifi only for codec and output types. The app has no lyrics.
- The app keeps a `rikka_account_book::Accounts<SavedAccount>` book at `$XDG_STATE_HOME/rikka/music/accounts.json` with one 0700 folder per account under `accounts/<key>` (same provider and account id replaces); a remembered account whose provider is not registered fails to resume with `music.accounts.unknown`. Sign-ins live only in the keyring: `keep_secret`/`kept_secret`/`renew_secret(folder, kind, ..)` (schema `os.rikka.Music`, attributes `folder` + `kind`: SoundCloud/Qobuz `session`, YouTube `cookies`, Qobuz `content-keys`). Private files go through `rikka_account_book::write_private`/`write_private_json`. `Error::unreachable` wraps transport failures; `rikka_account_book::Error` converts into `Error::Storage`. `Quality` stays outside `rikka_types::Choice` because music-core avoids Rikka settings.
- Every service plays through one `rikka_hifi::Hifi` made in `main`, shared through `music_core::Deck`: an adapter's `load` takes a `Lease` (`deck.acquire(&listeners)`), the previous holder goes silent and hears nothing more, and only the current `Lease` (`is_current`) may play/queue/unqueue/pause/seek. The Deck turns Hifi events for its own `SourceId`s into `PlaybackEvent`s (Playing, Paused, Position, Ended, `Advanced { from, to }`, `Sounding { fidelity }`); adapters themselves send only Loading, Length, Unavailable, Refused and a paused-load Paused.
- Gapless: the Player calls `Playback::prepare(next)` whenever `Queue::following()` changes; the adapter opens it and `Lease::queue`s it; `PlaybackEvent::Advanced` moves the queue without a reload. Volume is global (`Hifi::set_volume`); `Playback` has no volume.
- The services are the local library, YouTube Music, Qobuz and SoundCloud. There is no Spotify adapter: Spotify refuses access-point audio keys (`AesKeyError` 0x0001) to newer accounts, the same refusal as librespot#1649.
- Services, the adapter kit, streaming and each adapter are in the music-services note; the local library in music-local, cross-service playlists in music-blends.
- UI: `Music` root state with `Stage`, `#[state]` `Nav`, `Player`, `Library`, `Blends`, `Settings`, `Menus`, `Dialogs`, `Sound`, and `local` (the opened `music_library::Library`). Every remembered account is resumed at start and stays linked; the local service is always linked and never stored in `accounts.json`. `Player` owns the linked accounts and the browsed key (`Player::browsed`), and `Music::connection`/`connections`/`account_key` delegate to it, so switching never stops playback. `Player` routes each track to a linked account of its service (preferring the browsed one) and otherwise, or on Unavailable/Refused, to a `music_blend::find` match on other services in the `service-order` preference (local first by default; `Player::via`). `Music::queue_open` shows the right-hand queue panel (`src/queue.rs`). The window is either the desk (sidebar, pages, queue, player bar) or settings; there is no full-screen now-playing view, and the player bar's artwork is not pressable. `widgets::fidelity` draws the Hi-Res/Lossless badge from a `Fidelity`: rows and cards mark only high resolution, the album header and player bar spell the format (`wording::fidelity`).
- Pages never scroll sideways: a shelf (`src/gallery.rs` `Gallery`, on home, search and artist pages) previews one clipped wrapping line of whole cards (quick picks: 4-row columns), and its "Show all" opens `Place::Gallery`, a vertical `lazy_tiles` grid or a playable song list. `Place` carries the gallery's items, so back/forward needs no refetch.
- Fetches are keyed by account key and live in a process-wide table.
- Settings (`src/settings/`, `split_view`): Accounts, Library (folders, rescan, counts), Output (shared PipeWire / sinks / exclusive ALSA devices, ReplayGain), Quality and Storage. `src/preferences.rs` keeps `audio-quality`, `image-quality`, `audio-cache`, `output` (`shared` / `sink:<name>` / `hw:CARD=..,DEV=n`), `replay-gain` (off by default), `replay-gain-preamp`, `prevent-clipping` in `rikka.music`; `Music::set_output`/`set_replay_gain` persist and apply. `Music::stored` is the generation that re-measures the cache.
- `Sound` (`src/sound.rs`) holds the current `SignalPath`, verdict, `OutputState` and devices from `Hifi::events()`. `src/signal/` draws the player bar badge (`signal::badge`, no horizontal inset so it lines up with the title) and the signal-path popover (`signal::view`, `Music.signal_open`); its "allow rate switching" writes `rikka_sound::GraphTuning` allowed rates and applies them live with `Sound::allow_rates`.
- Playlist editing lives on `Catalog`; `Library` owns the browsed account's playlist pages (optimistic edits, `era` drops stale landings). Track menus and hearts offer save/radio/artist/album only when `Music::browses(track)`. Context menus wrap rows in `menus::target`. `src/music/playlists.rs`, `blending.rs` and `storage.rs` are child modules adding orchestration to `Music`.
