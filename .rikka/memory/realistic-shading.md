- `rikka/standard` is OpenPBR Surface v1.1.1 (2026-04-17 spec) as `RikkaOpenPbrSurface` + `RikkaOpenPbrModel` (`lib/rikka/pbr/{surface,openpbr}.slang`). Parameters use the spec identifiers (`base_color` colour3, `base_metalness`, `specular_roughness`, `coat_*`, `fuzz_*`, `thin_film_*`, `transmission_*`, `subsurface_*`, `emission_*`, `geometry_opacity`, `geometry_thin_walled`) plus texture slots and non-spec `specular_rotation`, `geometry_thickness`, `thickness_texture`, `thin_film_thickness_minimum`.
- Lobes (`pbr/brdf.slang`): EON diffuse (Portsmouth 2024), anisotropic GGX with height-correlated Smith and the spec's α_t/α_b mapping, dielectric Schlick from the modulated IOR (F90 = saturate(50·F0)), F82-tint metal, Zeltner LTC fuzz with the MaterialX analytic fits, Belcour–Barla thin film, coat darkening/roughening/view-dependent absorption from the spec. Layering is albedo scaling.
- Energy compensation is Fdez-Agüera style from a 32² DFG LUT that the `rikka.three.pbr.brdf` compute pass integrates once into a shared graph image (`RikkaScene.brdf`); specular AA is Tokuyoshi–Kaplanyan on α; indirect is split-sum over the sky environment. Standard (OpenPBR), skin, stage `RikkaStandardModel`, hair and eye take ambient through `RikkaIndirect`: diffuse along the bent normal with multi-bounce AO coloured by the diffuse albedo (`diffuse(n, albedo, material_ao)`), every sky-specular lobe (base, coat, wet film, hair sheen, cornea) times `specular_occlusion(normal, view, roughness, material_ao)`, and the geometric-normal horizon term `rikka_pbr_horizon` (normal-mapped reflections below the surface) kept beside it.
- Lights: punctual lights evaluate the lobes directly; area lights (`area`) use the renderer's LTC path: specular = directional albedo (dielectric/metal, energy-compensated) × the GGX LTC integral at the isotropic roughness √√(αt·αb) (anisotropy approximated), the second lobe mixed the same way, coat as its own LTC on the coat normal with the coat albedo, fuzz as the exact Zeltner sheen LTC (`rikka_pbr_sheen_ltc`), diffuse = EON albedo × clipped `irradiance`. Both use `shadow.transmittance()`, so tinted casters colour them.
- `RikkaOpenPbrSurface::transmittance` (coloured shadows) = (1 − opacity) + opacity · transmission_weight · (1 − metalness) · transmission_color (raised to thickness/transmission_depth unless thin-walled) · (1 − F0)²; opaque coverage without transmission blocks light, colourless.
- `emission_luminance` is in nits (cd/m²). The look's photometric scale is `NITS_PER_RADIANCE_UNIT` = 32 000 cd/m² per renderer radiance unit (`look/resolve.rs`, `RIKKA_NITS_PER_UNIT` in common.slang), chosen so `SOLAR_ILLUMINANCE` (4 units) is the 128 000 lux extraterrestrial sun; exposure metering then treats emitters and the sky alike. `rikka-model-gpu` maps glTF/FBX/the reference emission (renderer units) to `emission_color` with `emission_luminance = NITS_PER_RADIANCE_UNIT`. `transmission_scatter` and volumetric scattering are not modelled.
- Subsurface: models conforming to `IScattering` write, in the single-sample `rikka.three.scatter` pass against `scene_depth`, the pre-scattered diffuse and the per-pixel Burley diffusion length d = mfp/S(albedo) (`scattered` hook, two targets; named so it cannot collide with a template's `scatter` parameter group, which crashes slangc). Two `scatter_blur` dispatches blur it separably with the 1D normalized Burley profile, depth-aware and mask-limited, and the second adds it onto the HDR chain. Forward shading drops the scattered lobe when `RikkaScene.scatter` is set. Parts with a `thickness` get back-lit transmission whose path length is the raw shadow sample's receiver–blocker distance when the soft filters measured one, else `thickness`, tinted by the sample's tint.
- Lobe specialization uses the shared `[Feature(bit)]` mechanism (material-system.md): `coat_weight` 0, `fuzz_weight` 1, `thin_film_weight` 2, `subsurface_weight` 3 (+ `Role("subsurface")` for routing), `transmission_weight` 4 (+ `Role("transmission")`), `specular_roughness_anisotropy` 5, `specular_lobe_mix` 6; `rikka_feature(RIKKA_PBR_*)` gates them.
- Refraction: transparent buckets with `transmission_weight` split the shade pass before transparents; the opaque copy `capture_opaque` makes for temporal reactivity doubles as `RikkaScene.refraction`, sampled along the refracted ray over `geometry_thickness` (blurred by roughness, per-channel IOR from the Abbe number, Beer absorption). Without a look the transmission becomes alpha.
- Temporal antialiasing and upscaling: scatter, blur and refraction run at render resolution before the resolve, rasterize with the jittered projection like the G-buffer, and rotate their sample patterns with `rikka_temporal_noise` (interleaved gradient offset by the Halton jitter phase), so the resolve integrates them and settled frames (jitter stopped) stay bit-identical.
- Ray tracing: OpenPBR declares `[Role]`s `albedo`, `albedo_texture`, `albedo_weight`, `metalness`, `metalness_texture` (blue), `transmission`, `emission`, `emission_texture`, `emission_luminance`; `rikka_ray_response` turns them into a bounce albedo (diffuse base × (1 − metalness)(1 − transmission) plus F0 = lerp(0.04, base, metalness)) and emission in radiance units. Hits are lit (`rikka_ray_direct`) only by lights sharing the hit's `light_channels` and, for rig lights, its figure; local samples go through `rikka_light_sample`, so IES profiles apply.
- Looked frames only: SSS and refraction need `scene_depth`; unlooked frames render the subsurface lobe as Lambert with `subsurface_color`.
- `rikka/skin` = `RikkaSkinSurface` over the OpenPBR model: subsurface on by default (radius metres), dual specular lobe, cavity, detail normal. `rikka/hair` = Marschner R/TT/TRT (Karis' approximation) with flow and shift maps; draw it with `MaterialState::coverage()`. `rikka/eye` = refracted iris parallax through the cornea, limbal ring, caustic boost.
- `benchmarks/render` has `three: fox|knight|miku` and `three: subsurface skin` / `three: refracting glass` (each also `at 1080p`) scenarios.
- Importers fill `rikka_model_core::Pbr` layers: glTF KHR_materials_{ior,specular,anisotropy,clearcoat,sheen,transmission,volume,dispersion,iridescence,emissive_strength} (read and written), principled-BSDF inputs (both naming generations) with directly linked image textures for coat, coat roughness, coat normal, sheen tint/roughness, transmission, specular level/tint, FBX standard-surface and physical-material values and maps. Scalar maps keep their source channel in `MaterialTextures::channels` (`TextureChannel`); `rikka-model-gpu` splats that channel into all four before upload, so the OpenPBR channel conventions (glTF's) hold. Transmissive materials become transparent.
