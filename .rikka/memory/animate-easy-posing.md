- Ported from a reference tool's simple markers (`EasyPoseOverlay`, `ArmIkSolver`, `FootIkPoser`, `StandardRig`). `Handle` (16) sits on body segments: Centre (square, hips), LowerBody (rigid below the hips), UpperBody (55 % spine→neck), Head (crown), per side Shoulder, UpperArm (mid), Forearm (mid, bends the elbow), Hand (palm), Foot (ankle), Toes (tip). `Handle::point` is the marker; `fits(body)` filters.
- `DragSession::begin(body, pose, handle, pins, limits)` then `update(travel, DragScope)` moves the marker by `travel`, solving incrementally from the previous solution, so elbows and knees carry their bend plane continuously. Every clamp is `clamp_around` the drag-start pose.
- Motions (`drag/`): Centre `Carry` (translation kept in pin reach shells), LowerBody `pelvis` (root turns, spine keeps its world turn), UpperBody/Head `Bend` (minimal arc spread over the chain, twist muscles untouched), Shoulder/UpperArm/Toes `swing` (one bone, twist kept), Forearm `hinge` (elbow bend dial), UpperArm/Forearm over a pinned hand `swivel`, Hand/Foot `Reach`.
- `Reach` = `two_bone::follow`: analytic bend + minimal-arc aim of the grabbed point (palm grip carried in the hand frame), a bounded DLS `polish` when limits block a reachable goal, and for arms `clear.rs` swivels the elbow out of `Body::bulk`. Feet use `ankle.rs` (FootIkPoser): planted feet keep their world turn, lifting blends them to follow the shin, through `orient`.
- Feet always stay where they stand (session pins, foot IK), except Shift on Centre carries them (re-planted where they are when Shift lets go). Explicit pins never get further out of reach (`slack.rs` bisects the step).
- `DragScope::FullBody` (Shift, switchable mid-drag): a hand out of reach leans torso and collarbone by DLS; UpperBody adds root tilt and Head adds the torso, recomputed from the drag start each update.
- `twist(handle, angle)` (Ctrl): twist muscles of `Handle::twisted` (forearm for Hand, thigh for Foot, root yaw for Centre/LowerBody); `twist_axis` lets the app sign it so the near surface follows the pointer. `rotate` (Ctrl+Shift, or Ctrl where nothing twists) is turntable/trackball.
- `Finger::flex(curl, spread)` adds to a finger's three bend muscles and its base side muscle; the app maps vertical drag to curl and horizontal to spread, signed by the tip's screen motion.
- App `animate-viewport` (`easy/`): `part::Marker` = handle, finger, `Eyes` (ahead of the face along the gaze, `face.rs`) or `EmitterShape` (estimated from head and eyes); finger tips show only while that side's arm or fingers part is chosen; `scene::Pointed` = marker or palm ring; `drag.rs` kinds Move/Twist/Rotate/Flex/EyeDirection (eyes aim at the pointer, gaze mode Off)/Open (vertical drag opens あ)/Gaze (Point crosshair)/ParticlePick; `Follow::facing` is a view-parallel plane through the marker; hand drags lift targets out of the body (`clearance.rs`) and are never constrained by held cloth; the palm ring grasps cloth (`grasp.rs`, see animate-holds). `paint.rs` links markers, draws rings, fills the lit or held marker and names it (the viewport's own `wording::marker`, `wording::grasp`).
- `EyeDirection { across, up }` (animate-solve, each −1..1 of the eye muscles' reach, across + = the character's left) reads/writes the eye muscles directly; `EyeDirection::aim` aims one eye at a point within reach.
- Releasing a marker (click or drag) sends `Gesture::Focus(marker)`; a click on empty stage sends `Focus(None)`. The app's `card_for(marker)` (`animate/focusing.rs`: head → Face/Expression, eyes → Face/Eyes, mouth → Face/EmitterShape, hand or finger → Hand(side)) opens the viewport card in `Controls::card`.
- Releasing re-goals an already pinned grabbed end; pins are toggled only by the Pin command. Easy edits never key by themselves: everything goes through `Animate::preview(PoseSample)`.
- The assistant's `reach` and figure `points` use palms for hands and ankles for feet (`Handle::point`).
