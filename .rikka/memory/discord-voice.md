title: Discord call audio (discord-voice)
description: Call audio over discord_client::Call: 10 ms send chain (APM, Krisp, gate), mono Opus, playout receive, mixing.
about: apps/system/discord/crates/discord-voice
tags: [discord, voice]
---

- Public API: `Voice::start(Call, VoiceSettings, &Krisp, muted, deafened)` with setters, `updates()` (taken once) and `leave()`; `VoiceUpdate`; `VoiceSettings` (`NoiseSuppression::{Off, Standard, Krisp}`, `echo_cancellation`, `automatic_gain`, `InputMode`); `Krisp`/`KrispState`; `input_devices`/`output_devices`; `MicTest::start(&settings, &Krisp)`.
- `Krisp::new(cache)` is one cheap clone-able handle per process (the app keeps it on `Chat`); `prepare()` (returns `Error::Thread` when its thread cannot spawn; the app calls it whenever Krisp is selected, the send chain never does) runs `rikka-discord-krisp`: `Module::fetch` (falls back to `Module::cached` offline), then `Sdk::load(module, cache/work)`. `watch()` is a `rikka_worker::Subscription<KrispState>` starting with the current state (Preparing, Downloading, Ready, Unavailable).
- Send (`rikka-discord-voice-send`, blocks in `Capture::wait` on `Shared`'s send bell plus the microphone's `AsFd` (the `AudioCapture` eventfd, drained by every read); with no microphone open only the bell, which rings on stop, settings changes and hotplug): per 10 ms step `input/chain.rs` runs volume → `rikka_apm::Processor::clean` (HPF, AEC3, reference NS when Standard or while Krisp is not ready) → Krisp NC + VAD (`input/suppress.rs`) → AGC2 (`level`, fed Krisp's speech probability) → `Gate` (probability-driven when automatic). Two steps make one 20 ms mono Opus packet (64 kbit/s, FEC); one 20 ms preroll is sent on opening.
- Receive (`rikka-discord-voice-recv`): one `output::Stream` per SSRC wraps `rikka_playout::Playout<rikka_opus::OpusDecoder>`; 10 ms chunks are mixed (per-user gain, output volume, limiter), written while the speaker holds less than `Pacer::target()`, and pushed to the APM `Reference` so AEC3 sees what was played. The send and receive threads are `rikka_worker::Worker`s stopped through `Shared` (`impl Stop`). The receive loop waits once on `Shared`'s receive bell eventfd plus, with a speaker, the `AudioFeed` fd armed by `Speaker::watch_queued_below` (rung when the callback frees room, on stop and on device loss); with no speaker or while deafened it waits on the bell until the next 10 ms frame is due. The bell rings on stop, deafen, settings and per-user mix changes, attached stream sounds and their volumes, hotplug (`Hotplug::watch`) and, as the channels' waker, every voice event and stream packet. `MicTest` is a `Worker` over its own `Control` (stop, loopback) waiting the same way on its bell and the microphone.
- Devices open like the reference: 48 kHz with 10 ms periods (`EngineConfig::new().sample_rate(48000).buffer_size(480)`, `CaptureConfig::sample_rate(48000).period_frames(480)`), falling back to the device format only when refused; PipeWire resamples to its graph rate.
- `VoicePacket.arrived` is stamped by discord-rtc at UDP receive; playout uses it for its delay estimate.
- `io/` is the device seam: `Devices` opens `Microphone`/`Speaker`; `System` owns a `rikka_audio::DeviceWatch` that bumps the `Hotplug` generation on every device add/remove; `Attached` retries a failed or lost device only after the generation changes and reports `DeviceLost` once per loss.
- Krisp's FFI lives in discord-krisp.
- Go Live sound: `Voice::hear_stream()` → `StreamSound`, mixed like call sources; `Soundshare::start(AudioCapture, BroadcastLine)` is a `rikka_audio::SoundSender` (20 ms Opus Audio frames, 128 kbit/s music) whose sink sends packets and toggles sound speaking on `Sent::Quiet`; the app captures `CaptureSource::OtherApps` or `Apps(keys)` at 48 kHz stereo.
- Machine tests (`tests/machine.rs` → `devices`) need PipeWire; the Krisp mic test uses `RIKKA_DISCORD_KRISP`.
