---
title: Instant replay (rikka-replay)
description: rikka-replay keeps the last minutes of displays or windows as encoded packets in RAM; shell saves, cuts and drafts them.
about: system/rikka-replay
tags: [capture, service]
---

- `system/rikka-replay` is a session service (`rikka-replay.service`, `Slice=background.slice`, `Nice=10`, idle IO; `rikka-session.target` wants it). It owns the `rikka.replay` settings domain (`rikka_settings::session::replay`) and idles while `enabled` is false.
- It serves `rikka.replay` (protocol types in `rikka_session::replay`) on its own `replay.sock`; clients reach it with `SocketClient::for_capability`. Methods: `held` (`ReplayClient::status` → `ReplayStatus` of `BufferedSubject`s), `save {subject?, seconds?}`, `draft {subject}`, `keep {draft, from, to}`, `discard`; latest event `changed`.
- Nothing reaches the disk until a save: packets live in `replay-ring` (app-private crate: `Ring` of `Packet`s per `PictureId` plus sound tracks, trimmed by window and byte budget whole GOPs at a time; `Clip::cut` starts on a keyframe and retimes a still picture). The budget is capped at a quarter of RAM.
- Drafts (for the cut editor) are written to the session's runtime dir (`<socket dir>/replay-drafts`, tmpfs, emptied at start), one at a time; a superseded draft deletes itself; `keep` trims a draft losslessly into the InstantReplay folder. Saved files are claimed with `create_new` before writing.
- `engine/` runs one `rikka-replay` `rikka_worker::Worker` over `rikka_capture::Screen`, waking on its command eventfd, the compositor socket or the next capture, keyframe-refresh, trim or backoff deadline: one `Subject` per display (screen mode) or per window (apps/windows modes, at most 8, ordered by focus recency from `rikka.toplevels`). Subjects are reconciled only when `Screen::changes()`, the facts or a backoff move. On the GPU path windows are fitted into a fixed canvas of their output's size, so resizing never breaks the stream; the memory path reopens at the new size.
- Each subject captures through a `rikka_capture::BufferPool` (3 buffers; `BufferPool::dmabuf` picks the format with `DmabufFormats::sampled_by(&gpu)` on the subject's `rikka_gpu::Gpu`, `bind` imports every buffer, `capture` uses the first idle slot that is not the latest, `Frames::collect` folds capture events into `Captured::{Ready, Lost, Ended}`), the same pool rikka-remote and the recorder use.
- GPU path: dmabuf pool + `rikka_video::VideoPackets` with `Feed::Dmabuf` (rikka-vulkan-video, HEVC or H.264; automatic tries HEVC first). Without it, an shm pool and `Feed::Memory` (FFmpeg). Frames come only on damage; an idle subject re-encodes its last picture as a keyframe after 2 s. Ring and saved clips hold `rikka_coded::Coded` packets.
- `sound.rs`: a `rikka-replay-sound` `rikka_worker::Worker` with one `rikka_audio::AudioCapture` per track (desktop = `CaptureSource::SystemAudio`, microphone = `Microphone`/`Named`, 48 kHz stereo preferred). It polls the command eventfd and every capture's readiness fd (`AudioCapture: AsFd`), takes whole 20 ms blocks with `fill(.., ZERO)`, pads `Fill::Silent` gaps from the wall clock (waking only when a gap exceeds 150 ms) so tracks stay continuous, and reopens an `Ended` track after 5 s; both AAC on their own tracks. Engine failures are typed `Error` variants (`Health::Broken(Error)`).
- The compositor keeps captured background windows drawing: `Scenes::capture_drawn` sends frame callbacks to trees no output shows.
- Shell: the instant-replay chooser in `system/rikka-shell/crates/shell-trim` (`InstantReplay`) handles `Super+Alt+G` (save what is in front, notification with the trim errand) and `Super+G` (chooser → draft → trim editor with `Opening::draft`; saving calls `keep`, closing calls `discard`). Settings has a Replay section.
