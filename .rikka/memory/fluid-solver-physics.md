- Units: metres, Y up. Spacing `d = 2r`, kernel `h = 1.7d`, particle volume `d³/√2`; rest density from an FCC lattice (`lattice.rs`). Kernel `w = (1 - r/h)²`.
- Launch sizes: foam advect/collide/compact run over `spray_bound(foam two frames back, slots before, false)`, a proven upper bound on resident spray; `tri_bins` writes the indirect launch of `tri_place`; spawn records go to a persistent growing buffer (`gpu/spawns.rs`); bodies are refused past `Compute::widest_launch() × 128` vertices.
- Grid: dense wrapped grid (x fastest, ≤ 8 bins per particle) whose per-axis bits are refitted each advance to the bounds plus travel and emitters (`advance.rs`, `Grid::fitted`) and capped by `live * 8` from `basis`; the measured stats are checkpointed with the clock.
- Substep (`gpu/solver.rs`): sort (warp-coalesced `sort_count`, single-particle fast path in `sort_rank`) → gather + predict (floor clamp) → neighbours (single-pass 27-cell X-interval cache with early exit at 48 slots, 16-bit offsets when the candidate range allows) → per iteration [bodies: collide probe/between] → density (λ kept in `predicted.w`; reads `dilations` for closing; 3-level `shaped` skips normals when unused) → solve (unilateral PBF, step cap r, projection onto the stored wall plane, cohesion on the first iteration) → 2 coloured bond sweeps → collide last + finish (bodies) or `finish` (floor only) → curl (swirl in `positions.w`; the settle path swaps the velocity buffer) → viscosity CG interleaved stage by stage with the bond rebuild (`Viscous::stage`, `Rebuild::stage`); rebuild stages left over when the CG ends (`bonds::Pending`) are recorded beside the next substep's sort stages (`Sorting::stage`) or flushed at the end of `run`.
- Walls are planes (octahedral normal + offset, 8 B) so density and solve read the gap at the current position. Normals are f16.
- Bodies (`gpu/bodies.rs`): per-advance skinning (LBS, morphs) then `drape.slang`, which mixes skinned positions toward world-space cloth drapes (`Inputs::drape(body, node, &buffer)`, renderer drape layout, `blend > 0`; the body must be posed in the same `Inputs`; checkpoints hold the buffer clones, so producers must hand out ring buffers they never rewrite while shared) and dynamically sized plane-slab triangle bins (`triangles * 16` clamped to `[1024, max_tri_bins]`, O(1) `!slab.culled` fast path); per substep `tri_frame` writes spheres (sphere + end plane) and faces (end corners, travel, start plane, start edge planes). The probe keeps the 8 nearest candidates per particle (SoA cache) plus a clearance; between/last collisions skip particles whose clearance exceeds thickness + band. Adhesion pulls in every collision pass.
- Cloth reactions: the last collide pass of every substep (only when any body has drapes, `REACT` mode bit) adds, for a particle held by a body triangle, the normal momentum the body gave it beyond gravity (`m·max(0, (v_new − v_old − g·dt)·n)`, m = 1000 kg/m³ × particle volume, `Settings.reaction`) as −n impulse to the corners by barycentric weight, integer `atomicAdd` into `reactions` (ivec4 per body vertex, `REACTION_UNIT` 1e-9 kg·m/s, `REACTION_BYTES` 16, both defined once in rikka-physics-gpu and re-exported by fluid and cloth). Cleared per advance when drapes are present; at its end each draped node's slice is copied to a ring buffer, `reaction(body, node)`.
- Viscosity CG (`visc_start`, `visc_direction`, `visc_apply`, `visc_update`, `fluid/reduction.slang`): `diagonal` is compressed to 6-float SoA (24 B/particle); `visc_direction` updates a single `direction` buffer before `visc_apply` (avoiding double scattered neighbour reads and keeping `first != 0` NaN-safe without zero-fill); `visc_update` skips residual/diagonal/reduction on `last`; `group_sum` uses deterministic `subgroupShuffleDown`; `VISCOUS_CONVERGED` skips converged iterations; `visc_apply` caches each neighbour slot's coupling k on its first iteration (`couplings`, slot-major).
- Sticky contacts (`fluid/collision.slang`): with adhesion ≥ 0.3 a particle within thickness + 0.3h of a triangle stores an anchor (triangle | side bit, barycentric u, v, rest offset) in `state.anchors` and is PINNED; collide passes pull it toward the moving surface point, it breaks past 2.5a²h and re-attaches on the next contact. Adding or removing a body loosens all anchors.
- Emission: streams are ABC-stacked hex layers of the N lattice points nearest the axis (N = πr²/cell), no jitter; blobs and drips are FCC with 2% jitter.
- Substances (`substance.rs`, fields normalized 0..1): viscosity → ν = 1e-6·10^(6v); elasticity → bond 282·e^1.25 Hz at 4 mm spacing, scaled by 4 mm/d so the modulus, not the per-bond frequency, is resolution-independent; relaxation → Clavet plasticity α = 0.02·400^r /s; elasticity → yield ratio γ = 0.15 + 0.2e; viscosity → bond dashpot 0.005v s; cohesion → Akinci spline peak 30c(1 − 0.9e) m/s² (bonds hold elastic substances; full cohesion retracts thrown jet heads), only between under-dense pairs, damped by 1/(1 + k dt²) with k = 30c·18/h, and caps the substep at 0.5·2/√k; adhesion → 300a² m/s² pull, 200a² /s stick; friction → μ = 1.5f plus contact drag 200f /s on floor and bodies; XSPH 0.15 + 0.2v; spray → foam; wetting → 4w /s. PBF pulls only through closing (no plain density-deficit tension: truncated free-surface kernels made it accelerate thrown streams and shake puddles); no curvature term (it pinched thin jets).
- Closing (`closing_constraint` in `fluid/settings.slang`, `Settings.closing` = [1/steepest, 0.4, 1/3, 0.1] from `config.rs`): for bonded substances an under-dense particle's deficit is taken against the free-surface completeness for its colour-gradient steepness (`surface` table; `ghost.rs` integrates the half-space ghost that also gives the wall table), so a resting free surface reads rest density. The pull is bounded by 0.4 × interior weight (1 − n/3, n = steepness / half-space steepest) × stretch ramp (mean bonded r/L from `gather_bonds` `dilations`, full at 1.1), so only stretched material closes its voids; puddles and unstretched blobs feel nothing.
- Bonds are Clavet springs with dynamic topology in SoA layout (`partners`, `rests`, `bond_lambdas`, `proposals` at `k * stride + i`), solved as XPBD (strength (h − L)/(h − d), dashpot γ = τ/dt, pinned ends have inverse mass 0). End of each substep (`gpu/bonds.rs::rebuild`): `bond_relax` applies Clavet's rest update (L moves toward r beyond ±γL at rate α; pinned pairs never lengthen; L ≥ d) and drops bonds past L > h or r > 3h, tightening toward d the bonds of particles whose nearest bonded neighbour (`nearest`, from `gather_bonds`) is beyond 1.25d; `bond_propose` lists each particle's 12 wanted partners (caching existing partners locally); `bond_accept` keeps or creates a bond iff both ends list each other and fuses the first colour tally; 4 Jones–Plassmann `bond_colour` rounds colour new bonds (skipping already-coloured particles); `gather_bonds` packs owned bonds at the front sorted by colour so `bond_solve` indexes O(1) via `countbits(owned_mask & (bit - 1))` and subtracts only displacement from `clearances`. The colour count comes from stats word 10 (frame two back). Foam passes are skipped when no substance has `spray > 0` and resident spray is 0.
- Presets (visc, elast, relax, cohes, adhes, frict): water 0, 0, 1, 0.4, 0.05, 0.5 (`Substance::default`); milk 0.2, 0, 1, 0.4, 0.15, 0.5; paint 0.35, 0.05, 0.9, 0.35, 0.4, 0.3; honey 0.78, 0, 1, 0.5, 0.6, 0.6; slime 0.55, 0.55, 0.15, 0.8, 0.8, 0.8; lotion 0.6, 0.45, 0.7, 0.7, 0.85, 0.7.
- Behaviour at r = 2 mm: 6–8 mm water streams stay continuous over 30+ cm; water puddles stop spreading as one contiguous sheet ~1 particle thick (physical capillary depth); thrown lotion/slime keep to the ballistic path and lotion/slime puddles settle below 0.06 m/s. Lotion between parting palms closes into a solid bridge, necks into a beaded thread and breaks; the app scene (r = 1 mm, ~28 mL) is a continuous bridge at 10 cm and a thread at 16.6 cm, broken before 23 cm.
- Resolution limit of drawn threads: at r = 2 mm a 6 mL thread breaks at 6–7 cm with the neck still 4–5 particles across. At r = 1.25 mm single pulls are chaotic (±4 cm, break 20–85% across between neighbouring speeds); medians over 17 speeds: 6 mL 10.8 cm, 8 mL 12.3, 10 mL 13.3, 12 mL 17.3, break 54–58% across with sd 16–21%. Assert medians over speed sweeps, never single pulls.
