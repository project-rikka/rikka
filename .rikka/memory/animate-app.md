---
title: Animate application
description: rikka-animate plays and edits physics-driven PMX/VRM/FBX/glTF figures, any motion retargeted, camera and music.
about: apps/general/animate
tags: [application, animation]
---

- A format-neutral character animation studio: PMX, VRM, FBX and glTF figures (any recognized humanoid gets a `Body`, otherwise it is a generic bone figure); VMD, VRMA, FBX/glTF animation and `.rmotion` motions, each retargetable onto any figure through muscle space (animate-easy-mode note).
- The binary is split into app-private crates; the crate map, the `Studio`/`Workshop` panel traits and the catalogs are in the animate-crates note. The stage, transport, pacing and exports are in the animate-stage note.
- A figure holds `take: Arc<Take>` (`human`, `bones`, `switches`, `morphs`, `holds`), `body: Option<Arc<Body>>` (None when the model maps to no humanoid), `faces: Arc<Faces>` (morph catalog), `physique: Option<Arc<Physique>>` (body fitting) and `playback: Option<Arc<TakePlayback>>` (`DetailPlayback`, `MotionBake`, `MorphPlayback`). Easy mode is in the animate-easy-mode note, morphs in animate-morphs, fitting and motion style in animate-fit.
- Editing (posing, registering, keys, curves, the dope sheet, saving) is in the animate-editing note; liquids in animate-liquids, wind in animate-wind, holds in animate-holds, cloth in animate-cloth, the look in animate-look and its editing (node dock, slots, undo) in animate-shader.
- Everything enters through the bar's + button (one chooser) or a drop anywhere, routed by the chosen subject (`arrival::LoadTarget`): a PMX or VRM adds a model and becomes the subject; FBX/glTF is a model when it has meshes and no clips, a motion when it has one clip and no meshes, and otherwise opens the import sheet (`Arrival::Choose`, `dialogs/import.rs`, state in `animate/importing.rs`: add as model with an optional clip as its take, or apply a clip to the subject; the decision returns as a `Settled` batch reusing the read model); a VMD gives its body keys to the chosen model (`Take::from_vmd`) or its camera keys to Camera, a VRMA or clip goes through `Take::from_clip`, a `.rmotion` replaces the chosen figure's take or the camera motion, motion that fits nothing is refused (`Rejection::Unmatched`); music replaces the soundtrack. Batches load serially, models first.
- Each `Batch` carries an `arrival::Placement` (`Figure` for the + chooser and drops, `Stage`, `Accessory`): with a prop placement every model file (PMX/VRM/FBX/glTF) becomes a stage or accessory regardless of extension; `Animate::place_stage(path)` / `add_accessory(path)` and File → Add Stage… / Add Accessory… (`Command::AddStage`/`AddAccessory`) queue such batches (animate-props note).
- Loading (`arrival::load` on a blocking thread) returns `Incoming` motion with the snapshot it was baked onto; `accept` adopts the precomputed take or camera only while the subject still holds that snapshot, else applies it onto the live motion, and records every load as one undo step (`Edit::Replaced`). A figure `.rmotion` with humanoid keys is refused for a figure without a body.
- `command::Command` is the one table of app actions; `BINDINGS` (an `ActionMap`, several chords per command, the first shown in menus) feed `Shortcuts`, `enabled` gates menu entries and bar buttons, and `entry(label, animate)` builds the caption `menu_bar` items.
- `rikka_widgets::shortcuts(body, Command::pressed, Command::enabled, Command::run).standard(Command::from_standard)` (in `view.rs`) wraps the whole body as its keyboard home: focusable and autofocused, so any press that reaches no nearer focusable widget focuses it. Bar buttons and the subject combo are `focusless`, so Space and the arrows always reach the commands.
- `Animate::controls()`/`controls_mut()` expose `Controls`, the plain inspector switches: the next key's `tag`, Easy `hand`, the morph section's `morph_panel` tab, the motion-style `automation_draft`, motion `paths`, `linked` curve channels and the per-subject drafts. Actions with logic (`retag`, `apply_tuning`) stay methods.
- The area under the viewport is the dock (`Animate::dock`, `Dock { tab: DockTab::{Timeline, Nodes}, split, ratio }`): the bar's segmented Timeline | Nodes and split toggle, View → Node Editor (Ctrl+Shift+G, `Command::NodeDock`) and Timeline and Nodes Side by Side (`Command::SplitDock`). The Look menu resets the world or composite to the bare or cinematic starter (`Command::ResetLook(LookPart, Starter)`, one undo step).
- The inspector has three tabs (`Controls::pane`, `Animate::show_pane`): Motion (Easy: key tag, or the curve editor while a morph lane is chosen, then morphs and automation; Detail: morphs of a figure, then curves of the subject), Node (`inspector(desk, node)` places the binary-built `animate_shader_editor::node_properties`) and Physics (`inspector/physics/`), all in animate-inspector. The page sits in `turning(pane index, ..)`, so tab switches slide. Shared row helpers (`heading`, `labelled`, `choice`, `toggle`, `framed`, `glyph`, `key_controls`) are in animate-ui.
- Viewport cards (animate-inspector `cards/`): Face (Eyes with the goggle `Pad` widget and blink sliders, Expression moods, EmitterShape vowels; all write morphs through `Animate::express`), Hand (presets lit via `HandPreset::matching`, grip/spread/fingers, that hand's holds) and Pins; hidden while playing, outside Easy or under a dialog.
- Subject order: figures, the Stage (`Subject::Stage`), accessories (`Subject::Accessory(PropId)`), liquid emitters, Camera, Look (`Subject::Look`, `.rmotion` kind `look`) and Wind (`Subject::Wind`, `.rmotion` kind `wind`).
- The program has no tests of its own; its testable logic lives in the `animate-*` crates, whose tests read the `rikka-mmd` sample figure through `include_bytes!` of `crates/model/rikka-mmd/assets/mmd/`.
- `main` sets `SLANG_RUN_SPIRV_VALIDATION=1` before any thread so libslang validates the SPIR-V it emits for material graphs and composite passes.
- A loaded look `.rmotion` gets absolute asset paths (`Look::map_assets`), saving makes them relative to the file again; slots are keyed by figure name, so a look fits any scene with the same figure names.
