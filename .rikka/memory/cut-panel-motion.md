- The panel reads the selected clip from `Cut::shown_project()` (so `preview_edit`/`preview_edits` drags anywhere show here) plus `LivePreview` appearance overrides; graphics show `Clip::art_at(frame)`, cameras `camera_at(frame)`.
- `panel/rows.rs` builds every row: `*_with` variants take a trailing element (a diamond); `number_row`/`slider_row`/`toggle_row`/`picker_row` take plain `Fn(&mut Cut, T)` handlers (`Apply`), the `Adjustable` ones (`number_with`, `sliding_with`, `pair_with`, `colour_with`, `switch_row`, `choice`) wrap `Cut::restyle`/`preview_style`. Pair rows put the label above two fields. Number ranges, units and decimals are `rikka_effects::Scale` (fields and sliders through `ScaleControls`); transition knobs answer the same `Scale` from `TransitionKnob::scale`.
- `Adjustable` (`cut/styling.rs`) covers `Graphic`, `Treatment`, `Region` and `Camera`; its `preview` defaults to `LivePreview::show`, cameras preview as an `Edit::Redraw` through `preview_edit` because the renderer ignores camera overrides.
- `panel/keys.rs` `Keying::{diamond, on, group}` makes `panel/diamond.rs` `Diamond`s keyed `("cut-key", clip, Keyed::{Group, Property})`: click toggles the key (group: `Edit::KeyGroup`, or deletes the group's keys at the playhead when any exist), right click opens the easing picker anchored to it.
- Groups: look (shape/line/text with art diamonds per `Art::fields`), モーション (`panel/tween.rs`, before 描画/カメラ: header switch, 始点/終点 buttons that seek and light at the playhead, easing row, swap; `cut/tweening.rs` `Endpoint`, `set_tween` (On also seeks to the end), `ease_tween`, `reverse_tween`, `go_to_endpoint`, `open_tween_easing`), 描画 (`panel/placement.rs` `Row`; depth/tilts only with 3D on; whole regions only opacity), アニメーション (`panel/animation_presets.rs`: per `Way` a manner combo, length/period, easing thumbnail via the shared `rows::easing_row`, strength scaled per manner, 3×3 direction grid, text-only per-letter rows), カメラ (`panel/camera.rs`), 映像エフェクト.
- `cut/animation_presets.rs`: `PresetSlot::{In, Out, Loop}`, `PresetStyle::{InOut(AnimationPresetKind), Loop(LoopKind)}`, and the `Part` trait over `AnimationPreset`/`LoopPreset` (span, easing, strength, direction, letters); sliders call `Cut::preview_animation_presets`, commits `change_animation_presets` (one `Edit::AnimationPresets`, unchanged skipped).
- `cut/animating.rs` owns key operations on any animatable clip (visual or camera): `selected_animated`, `toggle_key`, `toggle_group`, `ease_keys`, `add_keys`, `remove_keys`, `clear_keys`, `smooth_key`, `bend_segment` (preview while dragging, commit on release), `set_segment_handle`; multi-edit commits go through `commit_edits` (one undo step).
- `easing/` is the reusable picker: open it with `Popup::Easing(Picking { clip, target: Target::{Keys(Vec<KeyRef>), AnimationPreset(PresetSlot), Tween}, anchor })` (`Cut::open_key_easing`, `open_group_easing`, `open_animation_preset_easing`, `open_tween_easing`); `panel::panel` renders it for any surface. Keys mode: AE interpolation buttons, Penner grid (`rikka_timeline::easing_grid` of `curve_tile`s; `easing::thumbnail` samples an `Easing`), `SegmentGraph` for the segment from the first held key to the next (`Segment` converts normalised handles ↔ `Handle` via `Channel::handle_toward`), speed (/s) and influence fields, smoothing, add/remove/clear. Animation-preset and Tween modes: Linear plus the grid (`presets`). `Popover` is the modal shell (outside press or Escape dismisses).
- Cameras insert with `Command::AddCamera` / `Cut::add_camera_at` (Insert menu and timeline menu) on the front layer.
