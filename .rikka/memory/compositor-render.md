- Rendering: `render::Scenes` (scenes.rs) keeps per-output and per-capture `Composition`s (composition.rs: trees, glass bands, icon, corners, plane candidates; pointer.rs: cursor surface and themed cursor image read by `rikka-xcursor`); node z order is counted by `render::Depth` and composes `desktop.drawn` items with `SurfaceTree`, a surface cursor tree or themed cursor image, and display corners (Full profile when the top layer shows or while locked); the background is black while locked. Each output keeps its last `Composed` (drawn list + `Staging`).
- Window shapes: the compositor alone rounds every managed window (xdg and X11, not override-redirect) to `Corner::Window` at its geometry and draws its 1-logical-px edge in `palette.outline` over the outermost pixels; rikka-ui's `window_frame` draws a square, borderless frame and leaves both to it. `Windows::shape` gives the rest `render::WindowShape` (rounded unless `Placement::Maximized`/`Fullscreen`); `StageContext` animates `rounding` 0..1 per window (`animation/rounding.rs`, reset when animations turn off) into `Staging::shapes`, and `windows::portrayed` puts it on views. `WindowShape::place` → `Shaped` (pixel-aligned like the surface rects) becomes the tree's node clip (only while rounded, so maximized windows stay unclipped for scan-out), the clip of its glass panes (`Behind::clip`; joints are kept unless the tree is a view), the edge node (`render/edge.rs`, owned by the `SurfaceTree`) and the focus ring. Opaque regions are cut to `RoundedRect::cores` (the two bands clear of the corner reach, shared with the renderer's occlusion), so rounded windows still occlude. Frozen snapshots keep the tree's shape and draw clip and edge too. Window captures stay undecorated (raw content for screen sharing); output captures show the display as is. `Scenes` holds `policy::Decor` (glass + edge colour). `animation::Stepper` is the shared elapsed-time step of fades, motions and roundings.
- Scan-out: `Composition::covering(whole)` returns the topmost shown, unanimated dmabuf surface covering the output, marked `covered` when scene nodes or glass bands lie over it (`Scene::painted_over`). `Parts::feedback(output, scanned)` returns `Presenting`, which tells only the scanned surface ZERO_COPY.
- Window views (`wayland/views/`): requests only mark the parent surface stale; `SurfaceHost::committing` stages a fresh `Cached` `Portals` list once per commit of a stale surface. Views are refused on subsurfaces. `windows::portrayed` resolves them per frame into `Staging::portrayed` (window root, geometry, destination, clip); `Composition::draw_portrayed` draws each as its own `SurfaceTree` tagged with the `ViewId` right after the parent's tree, with a node clip (geometry crop ∩ view clip) and its own glass panes, each intersected with that clip (never dropped for poking out). Trees, kept pane shapes and glass repaint layers are all keyed by (surface, view), since one window can be shown several times per frame; `Scenes::freeze` only snapshots untagged trees.
- Frame callbacks: a drawn root (including one drawn only through a view) with any surface left visible by `occlude` gets one every frame; an undrawn or fully occluded root waits in `render::Throttle` at the pace `windows::hidden_frame_pace` gives: 1 s (`HIDDEN_FRAME_INTERVAL`) for shown windows, layers and lock surfaces, never for windows on hidden workspaces, minimized windows or role-less surfaces.
- Glass (`glass/`): `Panes::place` reads `blur_region` per drawn root into backdrop nodes (`Behind` = placement origin, per-axis scale, display density, alpha, stack index); `kept` keeps one backdrop key per fitted pane; `hiding` stops anything over a pane reporting opaque inside its band; `repaint::Repaint` settles `BackdropCache::stale` regions per frame. `Composition` keeps the bands with their stack index, so a band above a surface counts as covering it for plane placement. Window captures draw the session's glass too, so their panes frost only the wallpapers under them.
- Joined panes (`glass/joints.rs`): unclipped panes of one surface that touch become one `rikka_renderer::Joined` body (first match per pane), plus two fillet pieces keyed from the limb's key with high bits; the compositor draws fillet glass outside the declared region by this convention, so a client that hangs glass off glass tints its own concave fillets with the limb's far-corner radius.
- Render staging: `Composition::staged` keeps frozen snapshots (texture ids kept alive by `TextureRefs` in `ClientTextures`, frost keys minted per composition) and panel nodes; `draw_trees` draws `Drawn::Snapshot` over its frost, the preview beneath the dragged window, leaving snapshots and the assist above windows, lifted windows, then the bar at `TOP_Z`.
- Capture (`capture/`): `CaptureHost::Toplevel = WindowId` (foreign handle → `Record::foreign`); `serve.rs` serves a frame in three steps (`measured_alike` + `capture_subject`, `draw_capture`, `deliver_capture`); `subject.rs` picks view/framing and a `Composed`: an output capture reuses the output's last composition (animations, snap panels, IME popups) and also gets the agent ring (`agent::Canvas::Capture`), a window capture uses `Desktop::drawn_window_on_wallpapers_into` at rest over the opaque background colour (an opaque frame showing the wallpapers, never other windows, Bottom layers or bars, while the display itself is unchanged); a frame is drawn only when its capture scene has damage; shm goes through readback + `rikka_compositor_pixels::write_rows`, dmabuf renders directly with a fence; a resized window re-announces constraints and fails the frame; `capture_committed` asks a frame when a waiting window or a wallpaper under it commits.
- Cursor sessions are placed in `each_turn` (`place_capture_cursors`), after requests and settled input, never only after frames; a new session learns where a resting pointer is on the turn it starts.
