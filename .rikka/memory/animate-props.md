- A prop is a model placed in the scene without humanoid, cloth or liquid features: `PropKind::Stage` (at most one, `Cast::set_stage` replaces it) or `PropKind::Accessory` (any number, `place_accessory`/`drop_accessory`). Subjects `Subject::Stage` and `Subject::Accessory(PropId)` follow the figures.
- `animate-prop` (GPU-free) owns `PropMotion`: resting `PropLevels` (x/y/z metres, pitch/yaw/roll degrees as `EulerRot::YXZ`, uniform scale, visible) plus per-`PropSetting` `PropKey` tracks (visibility holds), and attachment `Anchor { figure, bone }` by names, resting `anchor` plus `AnchorKey` tracks (`anchor_at` holds the key at or before the frame; a key may detach). `PropFile` is the file body.
- `animate_cast::Prop` is the whole entity and the subject's motion value (`SubjectMotion::Prop(Arc<Prop>)`): id, kind, name, path, model, faces, `BoneDisplay`, `motion: PropMotion` and `take: Arc<Take>` (VMD/clip bone and morph keys that drive the prop's own bones by name, no retargeting). `Keyed for Prop` covers motion and take; `PropTrack` adds the sheet (`Sheet::prop_with_take`: Placement group with an Attached-to row for accessories, then only keyed bones and the morph groups) and a `TakePlayback`.
- Adding, replacing and removing props are `Edit::Replaced` snapshots (`None` ↔ `Some(prop)`), so they undo like emitters. `Animate::sync_props` (in `refresh`) hands `Cast::props()` to `Stage::set_props`; a prop whose model the stage lacks (undo re-add) gets a `Prepared` on a blocking thread, `Stage::supply_prop` delivers it (loads supply theirs first).
- `.rmotion` kinds `stage` and `accessory` (`animate_document::PropRecord`: placement plus optional take) apply to the stage or the chosen accessory (`Arrival::Placed`, else `Rejection::NeedsStage`/`NeedsAccessory`). A VMD or clip while a prop is the subject becomes `Arrival::PropMotion` on its take.
- Editing reuses the keyed-settings code: `SettingSubject for PropRole` (binary `animate/props.rs`), drafts in `Drafts::props`, Register keys drafts or every setting (and the attachment while its lane is chosen). Attaching with no attachment keys changes the resting anchor; otherwise it lays an `AnchorKey`.
- Rendering (`animate-stage` `props.rs`): `PropActor`s with their own `GpuModel`s, placed in `Render::compose` after the figures are posed (so live, captures, exports and replicas agree): take playback through `actor::perform`, then `instance.set_transform(bone world × placement)`, the bone resolved on the first figure with the anchor's name (missing → world). Props draw on the default light channel into the main shadow cascades, are dressed by the look's slots under their own name (`Dressing::stage_with` + `Dressees`), highlight via `Stage::highlight_prop`, and are not liquid colliders. Visible props join `Stage::frame_all` (published `prop_bounds`); floor and shadows fit nothing to figure bounds.
- Gizmo: `Stage::show_prop(role)` makes the render publish a `PropFrame` (view-projection, anchor bone world as `parent`, levels incl. drafts) after compose. `Aim::Prop(role)` turns on animate-viewport `prop/` (`Rig` hit tests and screen-sized geometry in the parent's axes: centre = screen-plane move, arrows = axis, squares = plane, rings = rotation by screen angle; slides use `easy::Follow`, incl. `Follow::across` for any plane). Travel writes `Drafts::props`; release sends `Gesture::Arrange { role, settings }`, which `Animate::arrange` applies like the inspector rows inside one `in_undo_group` step; cancel clears the drafts. The viewport cannot choose subjects by clicking.
- Inspector: the Motion tab shows `inspector/prop.rs` (number fields, visible switch, figure and bone pickers, key buttons) through `PropEditing`; the slots panel lists a prop's materials. The assistant's Scene reply reports `stage` and `accessories`; there is no prop-setting tool.
