---
title: Screen-space fluid and wet surfaces in rikka-renderer-3d
description: Frame::fluid(FluidLayer) splats solver particles into screen-space liquid; Frame::wetness + Draw::wet darken meshes.
about: crates/graphics/rikka-renderer-3d/src/fluid
tags: [renderer, fluid]
---

- Public API: `FluidLayer::new(&ComputeBuffer, count, radius, &Ticket).spray(&buf, n).material(m)/.materials(..)`, `Frame::fluid(layer)`, `FluidMaterial { color, depth, cloudiness, roughness, ior }` (color is what light keeps after `depth` metres), `Frame::wetness(&buf, &ticket) -> Wetness`, `Draw::wet(wetness)`, `Wetness::starting_at(first_vertex)`.
- GPU contract (std430, written by a compute solver): particle 64 B (`center.xyz`, `center.w` = substance bits, rotation quaternion, `radii.xyz` all zero = dead slot, velocity), spray 32 B (`position.w` life, `velocity.w` = kind | substance << 8), wetness 16 B per mesh vertex (`rgb` = tint × amount, `a` = amount). Mirrored by the `RikkaFluid*` structs in `shaders/lib/rikka/common.slang` and `fluid/gpu.rs`.
- `src/compute_inputs.rs` (`ComputeInputs`) (shared with drapes): the frame waits on every ticket's timeline value and keeps each `ComputeBuffer` clone in the retire bin until the frame retires; buffers and tickets must come from this renderer's `Compute`.
- Substance indexes the layer's materials, clamped to the last; all layers share one depth/thickness surface, so overlapping layers merge.
- Pass order (`fluid/surface.rs`, `composite.rs`, after shade): depth (seed copies scene depth, then ellipsoid ray-cast splats), thickness (half-res additive into the `splatted` attachments), blur (separable depth-aware storage passes writing the published `thickness`/`scattering`; the second pass also writes the coverage mask), smooth (narrow-range filter, fills holes from coverage), composite into the post chain (+spray), stamp into the G-buffer in looked frames. Shadow splats run inside the shadow pass. Frames with fluid but no look still prepare surfaces.
- `rikka.fluid` (`lib/rikka/fluid.slang`) holds shared helpers (`rikka_fluid_coverage`, outline, spread, `rikka_fluid_shading_normal`); coverage defines silhouettes everywhere, so composite and stamp agree.
- `rikka_fluid_shading_normal` blends the 1-pixel depth normal toward a broad one (symmetric central differences at 2 × spread, taps weighted by depth likeness) by `clouded = 1 - exp(-scattering)`, so cloudy liquids hide splat lumps (no chrome look or dark speckles) while clear ones keep fine detail.
- `rikka.fluid.strand` finds strands a few splats wide by walking the smoothed depth across the coverage gradient and gives them a cylinder normal, so thin threads and streams get a Fresnel rim (`rim_reflection` in `rikka.fluid.composite`) instead of a flat grey band.
- Composite shading: transmission `exp(-optical)` of the refracted backdrop; the cloudy body splits `clouded` into a returning share `(0.5 + τ')/(1 + τ')` (τ' = 0.4 τ) lit by wrapped front lights and sky irradiance, and the rest lit from behind (lights through the back, HG glow when thin, the unrefracted backdrop) tinted by absorption and gated off where it is negligible; sky reflection and GGX highlights. Shadow lookups lift 2 radii along the normal and 2 toward each light. Thickness and scattering are half-res, depth and FRONT (`velocity.xy, roughness, ior`) full-res.
- Art style: liquids in looked frames take `Look::liquid: LiquidStyle` (`Physical`, `Plain` flat-shaded, `Cel { stylization, shade_softness, shadow_tint, ambient }`, default Cel 0.85/0.08/(0.88,0.84,0.96)/1); only frames without a look stay physical. `look/resolve.rs` writes it into `RikkaScene` (`styled`, `stylization`, …) and `Resolved::style` → `Stage::style`. `rikka_fluid_composite(frag_coord, styled)` in `lib/rikka/fluid/composite.slang` is entered twice (`fluid_composite.slang` physical, `fluid_composite_styled.slang` styled; the constant folds the cel code away); `FluidFrame::styled` picks the styled one when the style is stylized and some material is cloudy, because any cel code in the composite costs ~0.1 ms at 1080p through occupancy even where skipped. `rikka_fluid_style` (`rikka.fluid.style`): Cel gives its stylization, `0.78 × shadow_tint` shade, cel edge from `shade_softness`, local-light fill 0.3 and MToon-like sky (equalized 0.9 × ambient × half shade); Plain gives full stylization with a neutral 0.8 shade, near-linear shadow, full fill and the plain sky. Cloudy pixels blend their front light toward those tones; specular uses the cel shadow; rim, reflections, transmission and absorption stay physical. Spray applies the same rule per vertex.
- Wet BRDF lives in `rikka.wet` (`lib/rikka/wet.slang`: porosity from roughness/metal, darker saturated albedo × tint, lower roughness, water film with constant roughness and F0 0.02 weighting the base by `1 - film fresnel`); `rikka.material` uses it, and figure presets through the `npr.wet`/`npr.wet_glint`/`npr.wet_film` nodes (`lib/rikka/npr/wet.slang`: crisp film glint, sky sheen, Fresnel film reflection). Figure porosity is the presets' `porosity` parameter (class presets: skin/face/blush 0.3, hair 0.6, lash 0.5, cloth/stocking 0.85, eye/mouth 0).
- Figure wetness lives in the classic presets' graph (figure-shading note); a new figure preset must wire the wet nodes to take liquids.
- Avoid screen-space derivatives in shared material shaders: they hang lavapipe on AVX-512 CPUs with clustered point lights.
- `rikka-model-gpu`: `GpuModel::wet_vertices()` sizes the wetness buffer and `ModelDrawing::wet` starts each drawn node at `wet_offset(node)`, vertices summed in node order like rikka-physics-fluid's `Wetting` (a mesh drawn by two nodes takes two spans).
- Tests: `tests/three/fluid.rs`, `tests/three/wetness.rs` (`rikka_test_renderer::fluid_renderer`), `rikka-model-gpu/tests/wetness.rs`.
