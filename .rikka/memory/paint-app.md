title: Paint application
description: rikka-paint is the beginner-friendly image editor: tabbed documents, PSD-native model, tool rail, layers, GPU canvas.
about: apps/general/paint
tags: [application, paint]
---

- Goal: paint.net-level editing that beginners can use. PSD is the native layered format; other pictures open as one background layer.
- Layout: caption (icon, File/Edit/View/Image/Layers/Adjustments/Effects/Tools `menu_bar`, image `tabs` with cross-window transfer group `rikka.paint.tab`, `• ` before modified names), left icon-only tool rail grouped by `tool::Family` (names live in the Tools menu) with primary/secondary `swatches.rs` at its foot, the tool options bar (`options/bar.rs`) above the canvas, right side panel (`panel/`: Layers | History segmented, replaced by the running dialog's panel), bottom status bar (size, pointer, stroke length/size, selection, format, colour profile, drawing/saving spinner). With no tabs the body is the start page.
- Only working features appear in the UI; new tools go into `tool::Tool` (rail and Tools menu read `Tool::ALL`), new actions into `command::Command` (`BINDINGS`, `enabled`, `entry`); nothing checks the keymap for chords bound twice. Names of every imaging `Operation` live in `operation.rs` (`paint.operation.*`, effect operations through `effects::Kind::name`), history entry names and icons in `action.rs`.
- The UI-free document model (opening, layers, edits, history, previews, saving) is `paint-document`; see the paint-document note. Selections are in paint-selection, dialogs and the worker protocol in paint-sessions, objects in paint-objects, the assistant in paint-assistant.
- Selections (details in `paint-selection.md`) are `Region`s of per-row spans carrying partial `coverage` (`footprint()` drops it), combined by `Combine`; shapes (`Shape`, filled by imaging's `FillRule`), the magic wand and the bucket (`Flood`) are document edits (`SelectShape`, `MagicWand`, `SelectLayer`, `ModifySelection`, `MoveSelection`, `Bucket`), lines, arrows, rectangles, ellipses, freeform shapes and text are editable objects (`paint-objects.md`). The tab's `Selection` is only the immediate display and adopts the document's selection from a `Selected` report unless a newer local selection order is outstanding. Selection changes wait while a crop or reshape is pending.
- Each tab owns a `rikka-paint-document` worker thread holding the `Document`; it reports through a re-wirable `Sender<Paint>` mailbox, so tabs keep their worker (and running dialog) when dragged to another window. The worker protocol (`tab/worker.rs`, `Report`) is described in `paint-sessions.md`.
- A tab's document thread is deliberately detached (`tab/detached.rs`: `Detached::spawn`, held by the worker as `Detached`) so closing a tab never blocks the UI; it ends once its orders close and its GPU work is idle, and `tab::drain` waits for every detached thread after `App::run`, so no compute work is in flight at exit.
- The `Selection` view is `Observed` (held region, drag preview, cached outline); the canvas draws its outline as dashed black-on-white quads and the Rectangle Select tool maps buttons and modifiers to a `Combine` in `canvas/marquee.rs`, using the options bar's selection mode when no modifier is held.
- Per window, `Paint` holds `Colors` (primary/secondary, X swaps, D resets) and `options::Options` (widths of `Tool::sized` tools, paintbrush/eraser `Brushing`, one drawing antialiasing flag, selection-shape smoothing, wand and bucket `Flooding`, text `Letters`, gradient shape/mode, shape `Filling`, selection mode, picker sampling). `Tool::paints` covers every pixel-changing tool (marks, brushes, gradient, freeform, bucket, text) and drives `Tab::paint_refusal` for the canvas; the left button draws primary, the right secondary.
- Tool keys: V Select Objects, S cycles Rectangle/Ellipse Select/Lasso/Polygonal Lasso, W Magic Wand, H Hand, Z Zoom, P Pen, Shift+B Highlighter, B Paintbrush, E Eraser, F Paint Bucket, G Gradient, L Line, A Arrow, U Rectangle/Ellipse, O Freeform Shape, I/K Color Picker, T Text; arrows nudge the selection, or the picked objects under an object-picking tool. Canvas keys go to text typing first, then the polygon lasso, then stroke/gradient modifiers.
- A stroke is a `stroke::Stroke` (document-pixel trail, Shift/Alt constraints, `mark(offset)`), kept live in the tab's `Observed` `Ink`; the canvas shows it through `imaging.marks_overlay` at device scale, clipped to the bands of the selection's footprint (`canvas/overlay.rs`), and keeps the committed stroke shown until the drawing of its generation lands on screen. `Tab::stroke` commits one `Edit::Develop` of `Marks` masked by the selection's coverage; `Tab::draw_refusal` refuses non-pixel or hidden layers, running dialogs and pending reshapes.
- Paint has no pressure dynamics (brushes are size/hardness/opacity/flow marks); a pen drag only extends the freehand trail through every coalesced stylus sample (`canvas/mod.rs` `Drag::Draw`).
- The Color Picker orders `Document::sample(x, y, size, layer)` (sRGB, premultiplied window average; the composite is cached per revision) through the worker's `Sample` order; the answer lands as `TabEvent::Sampled`. Fill Selection is `Edit::Fill` with the primary colour.
- The options bar is one wrapping row: the tool's lead (icon, name, rule) followed by flat option groups, each tool function taking `lead` first; narrow windows add lines instead of squeezing. Choices are fixed-width `combo`s (`choice`), two-state options are checkboxes, and only icon groups stay `segmented`.
- Tall pressable faces (tool tiles, layer rows, recent tiles) are `container(..).on_press(..)`: `button` is a fixed-height control and overflows multi-line content. Side panels and the status bar sit on `palette().surface`.
- The canvas draws 2048 px `ComputedImage` tiles at the level of detail the zoom needs: canvas paint wishes `paint_document::level_for(scale × device scale)` into the tab's `Picture`, whose `Detail` (shared `AtomicU32` + `Order::Redraw`) makes the worker draw `Document::tiles(TILE, level)` from a shrunk composite shown through `Picture::show` over the document's last `TileState` (`on_screen`), so redraws convert only changed tiles or none; nothing is drawn until the first wish. Tiles are GPU copies (no readback), pixelated when a texel covers at least one device pixel, snapped to device pixels, over an image-anchored checkerboard. `Camera` and `Picture` are `Observed`, so pan/zoom and redraws repaint without view passes; the status readouts use `live_observed`.
- Every drawing after the first is staged, never refilled in place: `Picture::show(generation, tiles)` fills `incoming` (reusing the retired pieces as `spare` when areas match), `Picture::advance_swap` waits for `ComputedFrame::on_ready`, the canvas draws the incoming pieces with a transparent tint so they upload, and `ComputedImage::on_uploaded` on the visible ones resumes `Paint::resume_picture_swap` through the tab's re-wirable mailbox; a keyed 500 ms deadline (only while uploading) swaps regardless. `Report::Unchanged` folds into the pending generation (`Picture::unchanged`). `Tab::advance_picture` runs `Tab::reach` (overlay settling, preview retirement, reshape end, extent change) only for the generation that landed, so the old pixels never show between a retired preview/overlay and the new drawing.
- Wheel notches zoom through presets at the pointer, Shift/Ctrl+wheel pan, touchpad scrolls pan and Ctrl+touchpad zooms; Space or middle-drag pans with any tool; the Zoom tool clicks in, right/Alt-clicks out and drags a box to frame. Panning is unbounded at every zoom (a pan in Fit turns into that scale); Fit recentres. The camera model is `rikka_widgets::PanZoom` (Paint's `Camera` wraps it as `Observed`), shared with the Viewer's canvas and Discord's lightbox (`lightbox/framing.rs`).
- Recent pictures live in the shared `recently-used.xbel` under application `rikka-paint`; noting happens when a document opens.
- The desktop assistant works Paint as kit "Paint" (see `paint-assistant.md`): View → Assistant and Ctrl+K summon it, each window holds a `Seat`, and `paint/serving/` answers its orders on the main thread; changes run on the tab's worker as `Order::Compose` (a `Composition` closure over `Document::compose`), reads as `Order::Read`, and the answer waits for `Report::OrderDone { generation, refusal, notes }`.
