title: Animate stage, transport, viewport pacing and export
description: animate-stage's Stage/Render halves, the clock and Transport, live viewport pacing and TAA, output sheets and exports.
about: apps/general/animate/crates/animate-stage
tags: [application, animation]
---

- One `Transport` (Rc, animate-transport) owns the `Clock` (`Arc<Mutex>`, shared with the render thread) and an optional buffered `Soundtrack`. Observer sets: `position` (read by `time()`, so seeking repaints only time readers) and `state` (playing, length, repeat, sound). Audio is master: `sync_to_sound` re-anchors the clock past 50 ms drift and re-seeks sound on a repeat lap.
- The clock is touched only through `animate_cast::with_clock(clock, |clock| ..)`, so no guard outlives one expression; lock order is stage → clock, and the transport never takes the stage.
- The stage is two halves. `Stage` (`SharedStage`, `Arc<Mutex>`) is the short-locked scene: a `Scene` description (members with playbacks, camera, free shot, the `Arc<Look>` and the slot highlight, liquids, wind, `Drafts`, `Pending`, focus, cloth settings, physics switches) bumped by `revision`, capture requests, and what the render thread publishes back (`Projection`, `Aiming`, `Vane`, paint surface, cloth snapshot, bounds, liquid progress via `Stage::liquid_cache()`, restlessness, errors).
- `Render` (`SharedRender`) is the render-thread world (actors, GPU models, cloth `Simulation`, `Dressing`, liquid `Playback`, captures, pacer). `animate_stage::draw` takes an `Input` snapshot under the short lock, reconciles, uploads, renders captures and the frame without it, then publishes. The UI never waits on GPU work; only `animate_stage::replica` (export start) locks `Render`.
- `dressing/` turns the node-based look into each frame (`Dressing::stage`): `Plans::evaluate` for the frame (unchanged graphs are never replanned), autofocus from the focused head, `World::look` plus `rikka_shader_compile::composite` through `Resolving` (both the world `Resolver` and the composite `Resolve`), ambient, background, lights with channels and IES profiles, figures with rigs and overrides, floor (`floor.rs`) and grid. `passes.rs` compiles composite pixel passes on `rikka-animate-pass` threads with the `SharedCompiler`, creates them on the render thread, lets the image through until ready and destroys passes a frame no longer uses; built-in effects are created once. `assets.rs` loads textures (sRGB by kind), colour lookups, HDR panoramas, panorama suns per threshold and IES profiles on `rikka-animate-asset` threads. `materials.rs` gives each slot with a material graph the compiled template from `Shaders` (the last good one while compiling) via `GpuModel::apply_style` (imported values carried by name or role, graph sockets and slot values, `outline` from `MaterialSource::outlined`, the class preset `class_preset(GpuModel::class(..))` when the graph's schema declares it, which also seeds values a slot drops) and afterwards only `set_values` for changed values; slots without a graph return to the imported material.
- Emitters come from `World::look` (time = timeline time, so seeking and exports are deterministic); dressing keeps `EmitterMark`s (node path, shape, transform now) and `Render::build` publishes them with the view projection as `Stage::emitter_marks()` (`marks.rs`) for the viewport's emitter gizmo.
- Anything pending (asset, compile, template or pass readiness) marks the `Wake` waiting, so captures and exports wait until `is_settled`; finished loads, `Shaders` listeners and readiness callbacks rouse the next draw. Graph material slots settle only at `TemplateReadiness::Built` (a `Failed` optimized build is reported once per slot and settles), through `MaterialTemplate::watch`, which also rouses the live viewport (drafts are drawable) at every build step, so the optimized generation is adopted without input; the shader editor's chip shows `Ready` from the draft on.
- `Stage` keeps `GpuModel`s by `FigureId` apart from `Actor`s (instance, body, visibility), so replicas and the pour worker's troupe pose figures without GPU state. Figures pose from the global clock through `DetailPlayback` and the bake, not per-instance players; `actor::perform` poses a figure from its Take and is shared with the `LiquidScene`.
- `Figure::load` (loader thread) recognises the `Anatomy` once for the `Body`, `HeadRest`, cloth analysis, body fitting and cloth assets and builds the model's `rikka_model_gpu::Prepared`; the render thread only calls `GpuModel::upload_prepared` when it arrived. An export `Recorder` prepares its replica's members on the export thread (`Render::prepare`).
- `physics/` (`Simulation`) re-poses every figure from its Take inside `Stage::build` and runs the GPU cloth (animate-cloth note); `recast` marks it stale so the next settle un-rests every actor.
- The scene is rebuilt whenever actors arrive or leave or the mode changes; a new or cleared motion and Reset Physics re-seek, while edits (`Stage::pose` from `set_take`) keep the swing going. Physics on/off is `Command::Physics(bool)`; failures surface as `StageError::Physics`, and `Stage::notify_with` makes `Animate::on_stage_notified` show every error without polling.
- `Stage::next_draw` is due while the render has not built the latest revision, a notification roused it (`Stage::rouse`: bake progress, texture or shader readiness), the published state is restless, or time or size changed.
- The live viewport is capped at 60 fps by `pacer.rs`: `draw` builds only when the pacer is due (captures are never paced), and `Screen` schedules `wake_at(Stage::next_draw)` instead of `request_frame`.
- The live viewport uses temporal antialiasing following the look's `Sampling` (`Viewport3d::set_config`, `Animate::follow_sampling`); `Stage::build` marks `Frame::cut` when time runs backwards or jumps more than `LONGEST_STEP`. Exports and assistant captures keep a multisampled `rikka_renderer_3d::OffscreenViewport` (`capture::still_config`).
- The view is always a free `Shot`: only while the Camera subject is chosen (`Stage::film`) does playback follow the camera motion; paused, the shot is re-seated on the motion's pose whenever the frame changes and drags move it freely.
- `Screen::framed(resolution)` letterboxes the stage to the output aspect on whole device pixels (`Resolution::frame_in`) and renders at on-screen pixel size; the assistant's renders and Scene `output` follow the same resolution.
- `output::Output` (binary) holds the output `animate_cast::Resolution` and the export `animate_export::Encoding` (persisted in `rikka.animate` through `preferences::Preferences`, whose `output.rs`/`liquid.rs` own the `output-*`, `export-*` and `liquid-*` keys) and at most one open `Sheet`: `Size(Sizing)` (View → Output Size) or `Export(Exporting)` (File → Export Video, Ctrl+Shift+E). Drafts change nothing until OK; `Output::settle` applies the size and keeps the encoding.
- `dialogs/` draws every sheet with `rikka_widgets::dialog` anchored on `dialogs::STAGE` over an invisible press catcher; `dialogs::is_open` makes `Shortcuts::paused` turn the body `INERT`. `size::rows` is shared by the size and export sheets. Encoding choices are `(value, name)` tables in animate-export's `encoding.rs`, the names doubling as stored values.
- Exports are modal and run in `animate-export`: `Job::start` spawns `rikka-animate-export`, which builds its own offscreen `Renderer` and an `animate_stage::Recorder` over a `Replica` (assistant captures and the recorder render through `Render::render_still`), and hands readbacks (4 in a ring, 2 ahead) to the `rikka-animate-encode` thread running `rikka_video::VideoEncoder`. `Plan` maps output frames to timeline times and music samples exactly; progress and the end reach `Animate::on_export_report` through `Reporter`; cancelling or failing deletes the partial file.
