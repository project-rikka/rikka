- `crates/physics` holds every physics crate: the CPU rigid engine (`rikka-physics-{math,core,geometry,collision,rigid}` under the rigid-only `rikka-physics-world` facade), `rikka-physics-fluid` (GPU liquid on the renderer's `Compute`), `rikka-physics-gpu` (shared compute helpers), `rikka-physics-tailor` (public, CPU cloth data), `rikka-physics-liquid` (public, GPU-free liquid vocabulary), the GPU mesh cloth `rikka-physics-cloth`, and `rikka-physics`, the scene entry apps use. The fluid's ported and followed sources are credited in `crates/physics/rikka-physics-fluid/THIRD_PARTY.md`.
- GPU passes go through `rikka_physics_gpu::Pass` (`direct` or `indirect`): `.read/.write(_at)` push a device address and declare access; buffers a shader reaches through stored pointers (job records, body tables) must be declared with `.reads_through/.writes_through`, because `Batch` inserts barriers only from declared whole-buffer access sets. No shader grid-strides, so crates validate their sizes against `Compute::widest_launch()` up front; `Batch` fails a dispatch past the device limit. Readbacks decode through `rikka_physics_gpu::words(download, bytes, Error::ShortReadback)`.
- Cloth and fluid both take the step time beside their `Inputs`: `advance_into(batch, time, &inputs)`, so one `Inputs` serves several steps.
- `rikka-physics` is the coupled scene only (`Scene`, `Bake`, `Playback`, `LiveCloth`, `Layout`, …) plus `ClothBuilder`, `FluidBuilder`, `Drape` and `Surface` re-exported flat; it does not depend on the rigid engine. GPU-free consumers use the public vocabulary crates directly: `rikka-physics-tailor` (cloth assets, settings, `Wind`, `Grab`, `Quality`) and `rikka-physics-liquid` (`Liquid`, `Preset`, `Throw`); `Mix` maps a `Liquid` onto the fluid's `Substance` and the renderer's `FluidMaterial`. Drawing stays in rikka-renderer (`FluidLayer`, `Frame::wetness`, `Frame::drape`).
- Timeline policy for live simulations is `rikka_physics::{Follow, Pace, Step}` (`src/follow.rs`): `Follow::default().step(followed, time, pace)` seeks on the first run, advances by the timeline delta while `Pace::Playing` within leap 1 s / jitter 0.25 s (`Follow::max_jump`, `jitter`), advances by `Pace::Stopped { idle }` while stopped, and seeks on anything further.
- `LiveCloth` (`src/live.rs`) is the viewport cloth without liquid: `new(compute, ClothBuilder)`, `add_figure(index, Arc<ClothAsset>)` (re-adding warms up again), `remove_figure`, `set_style`, `restart`, `follow(time, pace, &LiveInputs::new().wind(..).figures(..).grabs(..)) -> Step` (seek = reset + one warm-up step; advance = fixed 1/rate steps from an accumulator, at most `cloth::READBACK_LAG` (4) per call, excess dropped; the cloth `Inputs` are built once per call; all steps of a call share one batch, so no still step waits on the stats of its own submit), `motion()`, `steps()`, `positions(&[index])` (one sync readback of positions for all asked figures), `drapes(frame, index)` → `(NodeId, Drape)` pairs for `ModelDrawing::drape`. `LiveCloth` and the scene's `ClothScene` share `src/cloth_figures.rs` `ClothFigures` (figure index → cloth `FigureId`, builds cloth `Inputs`).
- Apps describe a scene with `SceneSource` (`Send + Sync`, shared as `Arc<dyn SceneSource>`): `layout()`, `emitters()` (one `LiquidEmitter` per emitter: id, `Liquid`, optional surface `Region` by figure index), `pose(figure, time, instance) -> FigureMotion` (`Resting` stops re-posing until a recast), `emitter_states(step, figures) -> Vec<EmitterState>`, `wind(time) -> Wind` and `grabs(time, figures)` (cloth grabs by figure index, none by default).
- `Layout::new(FluidBuilder)` with `.cloth(ClothBuilder)` (gravity, quality, floor; the rate is forced to the step rate), `.drying`, `.step_rate`, `.figure(key, model)` or `.figure_with_cloth(key, model, Arc<ClothAsset>)`; layouts compare models and cloth assets by pointer, and equal layouts reuse the GPU solvers. Styles live in the cloth asset's pieces.
- `EmitterState`s are plain values: `EmitterShape` (Stream, Shower, Drip, Blob, Surface), enabled, rate and blob volume in m³, optional `Launch { position, velocity }`, bursts. The scene flows from the previous step's spout to this one's launch, narrows streams to the jet their rate fills, and lands bursts at this step's launch.
- `Solvers` (`src/solvers.rs`) is what a `Scene` builds: the fluid with every figure as a body plus a `ClothScene` (`src/cloth_scene.rs`: the `Cloth`, only when some figure has cloth, and its `ClothFigures` ids); `src/fluid_bodies.rs` adds every figure as a fluid body. The bake `Display` owns a `bake/replay.rs` `SceneReplay` instead: a display-only `fluid::Replay` (no solver buffers) plus its own `Dressing`, caching its `SceneFrame` after each show.
- Submits: one scene step = one `Batch` (cloth `advance_into`, then fluid `advance_into`, one submit, then `commit` both with the ticket); a bake step records step + captures (+ checkpoint) into one batch and waits once; a `Display` show and a restore are one batch each. Cloth→fluid (drapes, `Cloth::recorded_drapes`, valid before landing) and fluid→cloth (last step's reactions) dependencies rely only on the crates' declared buffer accesses.
- `Scene::build(compute, source)` step order: pose the `Figures` → cloth advance (figures, `SceneSource::wind`, last step's fluid reactions per draped node) → fluid advance with `Inputs::drape` for every drape of a dressed body (the fluid writes this step's reactions); `Scene::advance` is the only public stepping entry. `drawn()` gives a `SceneFrame` (`pour`, `wetness(frame, key)`, `cloth(key)`, `drapes(frame, key)`).
- The bake runs only while the source has emitters; a cloth-only scene without liquid is `LiveCloth`'s job.
- `Bake::builder().thread(name).budget(bytes).checkpoint_every(steps).spawn(on_progress)` runs a worker over the scene-level `bake/cache.rs` `Cache`: one GPU-resident `Capture` per step (fluid + cloth `Capture` device buffers plus the step's `Ticket`, counted in the budget), checkpoints (fluid + cloth) every N steps. The worker bakes the first uncached step at or after the playhead (the step `BakeView::frame` last asked for) up to the target, seeking the scene there through the newest checkpoint before it (or a restart); re-simulation is deterministic, so cached steps stay valid. An edit at `since` drops everything from `since` on. Over budget, the cache evicts whatever lies farthest from the playhead, shots or checkpoints, never the playhead's shot nor the newest checkpoint at or before it; when the wanted step itself would not fit, the bake rests until the playhead moves (`BakeState::full`).
- The worker runs on `compute.background()` (`Bake::set_compute` takes the render `Compute`); only when that lane shares the rendering queue (`!is_async()`) does it wait for its previous step's ticket before submitting the next (queue depth 1). It always waits for its last ticket before going idle. The cache mutex only guards map edits: shots and checkpoints are `Arc`s, checkpoints finish and restores run outside it.
- `BakeView` mirrors the bake's layout on the render thread's front lane and shows cached captures as device copies (cross-lane waits are the renderer's); a locked cache or a capture whose ticket is not ready is `Busy` (keeps one within 2 steps), never a CPU wait. A step is showable when it is cached and at most the frontier (the highest valid baked step; the frontier itself only while the worker is idle), so `Progress::working` includes the worker's `busy` flag: waiting for `!working` guarantees the last baked step can be shown.
- `Playback` is `Off`, `Baked { bake, view }` (viewport) or `Simulated` (continuous from 0, rebuilt when time goes back; exports), so both show the same particles and drapes step for step.
- A source whose emitters change is re-mixed (substances, regions) even when no step is invalidated, e.g. before the first step.
- Tests (`tests/support` builds a hand-made curtain `ClothAsset`): `bake.rs` (plank stream: bake = direct run, edit at step 20, liquid changed before step 0), `cloth.rs` (swaying curtain under a stream: bake = direct run for particles and drapes, edit at 20, `Simulated` = `Baked` every step; a thrown blob pushes the curtain against a dry run), `live.rs` (seek, play, owed half steps, cap, stopped settling), `follow.rs` (when the timeline advances or seeks).
