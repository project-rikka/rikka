---
title: Animate motion and camera editing
description: How rikka-animate registers keys, edits the dope sheet, bends curves and saves .rmotion, in Easy and Detail modes.
about: apps/general/animate
tags: [application, animation]
---

- `animate-edit` (private, GPU-free) owns `Key` (one variant per key type, methods dispatched by macro over the shared `Keyframe`, `Laned` and `Curved` traits), `KeyKind`, `KeyId` (a `Lane` plus frame), reversible `Change`s (laying, deleting, shifting, bending), `History<Subject, C: Undoable>`, the key `Clip` (`fits(accepts)`), curve `Channel`s (`Channel::of(kind)`) and the easing presets (`EasingPreset::all()`: classic linear, CSS ease keywords, then Penner families whose `rikka_keyframe::CubicBezier::fit` stays in the VMD square, quantised to 0..127; `tests/presets.rs` pins the bytes saved motions hold). `Keyed` is implemented once over `Tracks` for `Take` (bones, morphs, parts), `CameraMotion`, `Look`, `Emitter` and `WindMotion`. `animate-sheet` gives every track a `Lane`.
- Subjects are one model (`animate/subjects.rs`): `motion_of(subject) -> SubjectMotion` (Take, CameraMotion, look, emitter or wind; `Keyed`, `is_empty`, `document`, `accepts(kind)`), `put(subject, Option<SubjectMotion>)` installs one (set_take, film, place/drop emitter …), `replace` records `Edit::Replaced(Pair<Option<SubjectMotion>>)`, `update::<M>(subject, change)` edits a typed motion, `rewrite`/`commit` apply key `Change`s (`Edit::Keys`); `Edit::Cloth` is the only other edit. Undo and redo replay the same edits; edits with the same group (`History::record_in`) undo as one step, and `History::record_gesture(key, ..)` coalesces a drag into one step until `seal`. Look edits are `Edit::Replaced` snapshots recorded by `Animate::change_look` (animate-shader note). `keying()` names the key kind the next registration makes; `Curves` hold the default curve per `KeyKind`.
- Wind and emitters share every settings operation (`setting`, `draft`, `apply`, `key_setting`, `clear_settings`, `register_settings`) through `SettingSubject` (`animate/settings.rs`) and the stage's `Drafts` (`pour`, `wind`).
- Takes and tracks share storage (`Tracks` keeps each id's track in an `Arc`), so cloning a take for an edit copies only the map, an edit copies only the tracks it touches, and `changed_from`/`dirty_since` skip shared tracks; detail playback samples the take's tracks directly, so a rebind rebuilds nothing for bones. `Mode::Detail` on a human figure lists only bones the body does not drive, plus the morph groups.
- `Timeline` (Rc, `Observed`, animate-timeline) holds a `rikka_timeline::TimeScale` (zoom, first frame, width, end), the row scroll, open groups and the subject's sheet, so scrolling and scrubbing repaint `Dope`/`Playhead`/`Readout` without view passes; ticks while playing call `skip_view`. It also holds the selection: the chosen `Lane` (the bone being posed) and the picked keys, behind a separate `selection` observer set so scrolling never re-runs their readers.
- `animate-sheet` (GPU-free) turns a take into a dope `Sheet`: `Sheet::of_take(Listing, faces, take)` (`Listing::{Parts, Bones { display, shown }, Morphs}`: Easy parts and holds, or the bones of a `BoneDisplay` — PMX display frames with the Display·IK row (`from_pmx`), or for other formats one group per anatomy region labelled by its `Part` (`by_region`, `GroupKind::Region`) — then unlisted and unmatched bones; morph groups always) and `sheet.retaken(listing, faces, before, take)`, which patches only the tracks whose `Arc` changed (`Tracks::touched`) and rebuilds only when a row appears or disappears; `Figure::retake` uses it on every `set_take`. `Sheet::camera` is one row; `Sheet::fluid` groups an emitter's settings by `Section`, groups that `stays_open` like the Easy parts.
- The cast `Skeleton` lists PMX bones with their flags, or for other formats every skin joint and its mesh-less ancestors (all turn, roots move), with `rest_rotation` so pending `Local`s and handles act as rest-relative deltas.
- animate-timeline's `dope/` is `hit.rs` (pure row/key/lane queries), `paint.rs` (sheet, rows, `Ticks` ruler, `KeyGlyph` keys per tag, wave, box selection) and `widget.rs` (`Dope`, grabs, events); `mod.rs` holds `Names`. `Dope` reserves a `LANE` on the right and bottom for its scrollbars (`rikka_timeline::ScrollTrack` over `Timeline::extent(Axis)`); the wheel scrolls rows, Shift or the ruler scrolls time, Ctrl/Alt zooms at the pointer, middle-drag pans.
- The timeline sits directly below the stage with no card; `Dope` fills only its key area with `palette.surface` and shades past the motion's end on the ruler only.
- An unregistered pose is the stage's `Pending` (one figure, one frame): optional `pose` — `PendingPose::Bones` (Detail, bone overrides in VMD terms) or `PendingPose::Human(Posture)` (Easy: the edited `PoseSample` plus the `shown` pose drawn) — and `morphs` (pending morph weights, kept across both). It is applied after the animation in `Actor::animate`, and it is dropped once the frame changes or playback starts. Registering (Enter) keys every pending bone plus the chosen bone in Detail, every edited part in Easy, and every pending morph plus a chosen morph lane in both, as one undo step.
- `Stage::build` records a `Projection` (view-projection plus bone worlds and grips) for the focused figure and repaints `Stage::watchers`; `Screen` and the inspector's `Values` draw from it.
- `Screen` draws bones (square = movable, circle = turn only, orange = IK, red = chosen) and the bottom-right handles (`animate-viewport` `handles.rs`). Holding a handle locks and hides the pointer and its vertical travel moves at fixed rates. Rotate-only bones refuse the slide row.
- `animate_cast::MmdAxis` (`turn`, `slide`) converts PMX-space axes, units and degrees to canonical space and back. Handles act in PMX space, global or local.
- Register (an icon button, primary while `has_unregistered()`) and Reset Pose sit in the timeline bar. The right-hand `inspector` holds only interpolation: a channel dropdown with an all-channels switch, the curve editor (drawn on `rikka_timeline::CurvePlot`) and the preset table (Linear/Ease tiles, then `rikka_timeline::easing_grid` rows: Basic, then the fitted families). A curve applies to the picked keys, or else to the next registration (`Curves`).
- A stopped clock may stand past the motion's end, so keys can be laid anywhere. Playback still ends at the last key or the end of the sound.
- `Ctrl+S` saves the subject's take, camera motion, look or liquid emitter as `.rmotion` (`animate_document::Motion` JSON, MIME `model/x.rikka-motion` from `apps/general/animate/rikka-animate.xml`). VMD is read only.
