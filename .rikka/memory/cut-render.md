---
title: Cut preview compositor (cut-render)
description: cut-render composites a cut-project frame into one imaging Picture, supplies footage frames and runs the render thread.
about: apps/general/cut/crates/cut-render
tags: [video, graphics]
---

- UI-free; depends on cut-project and `rikka-image-edit`, `rikka-effects`, `rikka-figure`, `rikka-video` and `rikka-renderer`. Facade: `Compositor`, `Composite`, `Request`, `Playing`, `Overrides`, `Renderer`, `RenderedFrame`, `Sequence`, `Error`.
- `Request::new(Arc<Project>, frame).scale(output px per project px).viewpoint(Viewpoint).overrides(..).playing(Option<Playing>)`; `Overrides` carries previewed appearances (cameras too), effect stacks and the drawing-in-progress graphic.
- All geometry comes from `Project::scene_restyled(frame, request.seen_from(), overrides)`: draw order, animation presets applied, camera chain, and per-layer transition passages (both sides, see cut-transitions). Camera clips draw nothing; the drawing graphic uses `Project::front_shot` (default camera, above every layer).
- Modules: `compositor.rs` (gather, plan, compose), `layering.rs` (`Layering` context + final `Place` step), `graphic.rs`, `footage.rs`, `region.rs`, `sight.rs`, `wiping.rs`, `letters.rs`, `pieces.rs` (caches), `stack.rs`, `plan.rs`.
- `Sight::new(projection, local rect, format, factor)` clips the item's local rect to what lies in front of every camera and inside the frame (None = culled or singular); `stretch()` is the largest output px per local unit there and sets raster and reader scales. `matrix(to_local)` = output scale ∘ shot homography ∘ piece→local, normalised for f32. A piece reaching behind a camera, or wiped, gets a polygon `Mask` traced through the projection.
- Graphics: art (keyed values, reveal folded in) drawn by `figure::recipe` at `stretch` (stepped up 2^⅛ when the clip, its animation presets or a camera above animate), capped at 8192 px; effects and the animation-preset blur (`Stack::blurred`, Gaussian with 3σ padding) run on the piece. Text with letter stagings or reveal < 1 goes through `figure::Letterforms` (cached per slot and lettering, retraced only when the scale changes) with `LetterPose`s; its visibility and raster scale come from the posed letters' extent (`Pieces::letter_reach`), so letters flying beyond the rest bounds are neither culled nor soft. Typewriter shows letter i while i < reveal × count.
- Footage: placed straight from the reader picture (mirror, radius) or, with effects/blur, into a padded piece first. Readers `fit` to picture width × stretch; culled footage is not fetched.
- Regions: the local shape path, flattened at `stretch`, wipe-clipped and traced through the projection, becomes the mask; effects and feather scale by `factor × shot.path.stretch(position)` (camera magnification). A whole-frame region covers its frame rect on its own plane (`shot.path`): no mask when that covers the output, otherwise the traced quad (overview, zoomed-out or turned cameras); inversion and feather apply to shapes only. A single-step or unmasked opaque region runs in the main recipe; otherwise the canvas is developed, filtered and placed back under the mask.
- Wipes (and reveal on non-text) are `Wipe::shown` half-planes in item-local space across the item's extent.
- Caches key on everything that changes pixels: blank canvases per size; drawn pieces per slot (art, poses, scale, pad); letterforms per slot; effected pieces (source identity, stack, factor, framing); reaches per art. Unused entries are swept every compose. A plan equal to the last one returns the last picture, so still frames are never redrawn.
- `Supply` holds one `FrameReader<Picture>` per `cut_project::Alignment` (media, speed, origin; split halves share), uploading with `upload_rgba8` on the reader thread, `fit` stepped by 2^¼, reading 0.5 s × speed ahead while playing, pre-opening clips due within 1.5 s and dropping readers idle for 3 s. A missing frame answers the deck's last picture (`exact == false`); failed readers report their media in `offline`.
- `Renderer::start(imaging, publish)` runs `rikka-cut-render` on a `rikka_worker::Worker` (the compositor is built on the thread; drop stops and joins): newest-wins `submit`, `is_current`, `publish(&RenderedFrame { picture, frame, exact, offline })`. While playing it renders the frame due at now + latency + one display frame; it waits up to 80 ms (paused) or half a frame (playing) for footage; after 3 s idle it lets the compute pool trim.
- Offline rendering: `Sequence::new(imaging, cancel).render(&request)` waits on footage readiness or the `rikka_types::CancelToken` (owned by `cut_export::Job`, which cancels it) until every footage frame is exact (30 s per frame, then `Error::Stalled`); no polling. `Request::sized(w, h)` fixes the output size (scale = the larger ratio), `Request::rolling()` reads ahead like 1× playback without a clock.
- Tests (headless imaging) in `tests/`: `compositor.rs`, `renderer.rs`, `projection.rs` (an `Oracle` in `tests/support` unmaps every output pixel through the shot to check tilt, cameras and items behind the camera), `animation_presets.rs` (fade, typewriter); `tests/machine.rs` holds the frame-budget `timing.rs` check.
