---
title: Browser application
description: rikka-browser is the Arc-style browser over rikka-web (official Chrome): runtimes per profile, own chrome only.
about: apps/system/browser
tags: [application, web]
---

- `rikka-browser` (default for http/https/html in the session `mimeapps.list`): Arc-style left sidebar, top bar as caption with the address field, Spaces with icons and profiles, Favorites, Pinned tree, Today, archive, Split View (2–4), Peek, Little Arc, private windows, internal `rikka://` pages, reader, translation.
- Chrome draws everything page-owned on the nested display: dialogs, permission bubbles, `<select>`, find bar (Ctrl+F/Ctrl+G/F3 are not in the app's chord table), print preview (`Session::print`, bound to Ctrl+S and the menu), error/certificate pages, file choosers, notifications. The app never reimplements them.
- Kiosk Chrome shows no page context menu and no link status, so the app draws both: `Event::ContextMenu` opens `Subject::Page(tab)` in `menus/` (entries in `menus/page.rs`, actions in `shell/context.rs`, anchored at the page's `PAGE_EDGE` mark in `content.rs`); copy/cut/paste go through the desktop clipboard plus `Session::edit`. `Meta.hovered` (from `PageState.hovered_link`) is the bottom-left bubble in `status.rs`.
- Engines (`engines.rs`, `chrome/`): one `rikka_web::Runtime` per `ProfileKey`; persistent profiles use `$XDG_DATA_HOME/rikka/browser/chrome/<profile id>`, private windows `Profile::Ephemeral`. `Browser::engine` starts or returns a running one, resets its `chrome::Chrome` state, attaches its event feed and asks every window to `listen`; `running_engine` never starts one. Runtime events are handled by one window at a time (`relay::WindowFeed`) in `chrome/events.rs`.
- `chrome::Chrome` (in `Services`) holds per-profile `Phase` (Starting/Ready/Failed/Exited), Google account, extension list, privacy values and the bookmark-sync latch. On `Ready` the app registers the translation probe, hides Chrome's download bubble, reads the account and extensions and syncs bookmarks. `Failed` shows `chrome::failure_page` (extension missing/outdated; repaired by updating Rikka) in that profile's tabs; `Exited` turns live tabs Unloaded and shows `exited_page` until the person reloads.
- `page.rs`: tabs hold `Page::{Unloaded, Live}`; `Live` keeps the `Session`, its `Opener` (`App`, `Page` = opened by a page and closed when the page closes itself), the favicon URL last fetched and an event relay. `Browser::activate` opens a session with the tab's URL. Closing a window closes its sessions (not while a quit awaits confirmation).
- Restore: the store's saved sessions are the only source of tabs at startup; Chrome never restores its own session, so back/forward lists do not survive a restart. Tabs come back Unloaded and only activated ones open sessions.
- New pages (`shell/events.rs::new_session`, runtime-level in `chrome/events.rs`): `Popup{width,height}` → Little Arc popup window, `Window` → tab torn off into a new window, tabs → after the opener (cross-site `ForegroundTab` from a pinned/favorite tab → Peek); inside a Little window tabs go to the browser window. DevTools windows (`RuntimeEvent::ForeignWindow`) open as their own `devtools::DevTools` app window over `foreign_view`.
- The model lives in `browser-model` (`Model<Live>` through `src/model.rs`); `Shell::changed` also schedules `settle_bookmarks`, which syncs a persistent profile with Chrome bookmarks (`chrome/bookmarks.rs`) when its `bookmark_mirror` differs from the last applied one; `BookmarksChanged` syncs too; one sync per profile at a time, never for private windows.
- Every window shares one `Rc<Browser>` (`RefCell<Model>`, engines, preferences, services, window senders); `Shell` is per window. Sessions save 2 s after a change, every 60 s and at exit (URL and title only).
- History (`internal/history.rs`), Home top sites (`home/page.rs`, by visit count over 90 days, hidden URLs in the `home-hidden-sites` preference) and omnibox history suggestions (`omnibox/picking.rs`) read `runtime.history()`. Clearing data (`internal/clearing.rs`) clears Chrome with `Storage::clear_since` plus the store's closed tabs/archive.
- Downloads come from `RuntimeEvent::Downloads` through `browser_model::Downloads`; a new download opens the panel, finished/failed ones notify when no window is focused (`alerts.rs`, download notices only); actions go to `runtime.downloads()`.
- Favicons (`favicons.rs`): fetched with `Session::favicon` when `PageState.favicon` changes, decoded to ≤64 px and cached in the store per page URL.
- Site info (`site_info.rs`): origin, URL-derived security, clearing that origin's site data and Chrome's site details page. Extensions (`extensions.rs`): `rikka://extensions` and the settings section over `runtime.extensions()`, refreshed on `ExtensionsChanged`; no toolbar actions.
- Settings sections: Appearance, Tabs, Home, Profiles (with the Google account row), Search, Default browser, Translation, Privacy, Extensions, Performance, Chrome (`sections/chrome.rs`: account, links to chrome:// pages from `browser_pages::ChromePage`, toggles over `runtime.privacy()` disabled when not changeable), Clear data. Chrome pages open as normal tabs (`Shell::open_page`).
- Keys are one table in `keys.rs`; the web view reserves exactly those chords. Zoom chords call `Session::set_zoom` (reset = 0, Chrome's default); shown zoom is `PageState.zoom`. Ctrl+U opens `view-source:` in a new tab. Fullscreen (`Event::Fullscreen`) hides the app chrome; leaving it from the app uses `Session::exit_fullscreen`.
- Reader (`reader/`) extracts with `browser_reader::extract` and shows the rendered article in its own session as a `data:` URL; following a link closes it and navigates the tab.
- Internal pages are native: add a variant in `browser_pages::Internal` (title/symbol in `internal/route.rs`), render in `internal::view`, state in `Shell.pages`, setup in `Shell::prepare_page`; settings sections go into `Section`/`LISTED`.
- Preferences domain `rikka.browser` lives in the app-private crate `browser-preferences` (`Preferences::schema` feeds the committed settings catalog; run `xtask settings` after changing keys). Locale tables are per feature (`[browser.<feature>]`); en and ja keep the same lines.
