---
title: SVG renderer (rikka-svg)
description: rikka-svg parses SVG with rikka-xml and draws with rikka-vector: features, text layering, media, bounds, resvg oracle.
about: crates/graphics/rikka-svg
tags: [svg, raster]
---

- API: `Svg::open(path)` / `Svg::from_bytes(data)` (plain or gzip), `Svg::decoding(&decoder)` or `Loader::default()` with `.color_scheme(ColorScheme::Dark)` then `.open|from_bytes` (`Decoder = dyn Fn(&[u8]) -> Option<Bitmap>`, straight RGBA8), `size()`, `render(w, h) -> Raster` (straight RGBA8, fitted and centred, 64 Mpx cap), `follows_color_scheme()` (a `prefers-color-scheme` query in the document, a nested SVG picture or a glyph document), `render_glyph` → `GlyphPixels` for SVG-in-OpenType glyphs.
- Layers: `parse/` (internal DTD entities, `Dom` over `rikka_xml::parse_lenient` keeping text-content characters, `css` + `selectors` + `media` cascade applied into attributes, colours, lengths, transforms; path data through `rikka-path-data`) → `tree/` (`build/` with `marking`, `typesetting`, `filtering` children; resolved `Group`/`Shape`/`Picture`/`Glyph` nodes, paint servers, clips, masks, filters, `markers`, `text` layout, `glyphs` placement) → `render/` (draws onto a `rikka_vector::Canvas`; `filters`, `lighting`, `noise`, `glyphs`).
- Text layering: rikka-svg depends on rikka-text-layout (shaping, `Paragraph`, `Caps`), rikka-text-catalog (`catalog()` fonts and metrics), rikka-text-raster (`OutlineCache`/`PreparedOutline::trace` outlines and `Rasterizer::render` colour glyphs) and rikka-text-font, never on rikka-text. rikka-text depends on rikka-svg for SVG colour glyphs; rikka-text-raster hands SVG glyph documents over as bytes and rikka-svg parses them itself, so there is no cycle.
- Colour glyphs (a colour face whose glyph has COLR or an SVG document, or whose face has CBDT/sbix; any face without outlines) become `Node::Glyph(GlyphPicture)`. SVG-in-OpenType documents are parsed while building (`Loader` with `SvgGlyphs::Skipped`, so a glyph document never embeds SVG glyphs; at most 8 documents kept, their bytes charged to the budget) and drawn as vectors. COLR and bitmap strikes are rasterized at draw time at the device ppem (at most 512, then scaled) through a thread-local `Rasterizer` with a 32-entry cache, and drawn as a bilinear pattern. They need a fill: its colour is the COLR foreground and its opacity applies; strokes skip them.
- `<text>`/`<tspan>`/`<textPath>` become one `Shape` of glyph outlines per styled span (bounds = the whole text's cell box, for paint servers): whitespace collapses unless `xml:space="preserve"`/`white-space: pre`; `x`/`y`/`dx`/`dy`/`rotate` lists, chunks at absolute positions with `text-anchor`, per-span shaping, `font-variant`/`font-variant-caps` as OpenType features (nothing synthesised when the font lacks them), `letter-spacing`/`word-spacing`, `textLength` on text/tspan/textPath (innermost first, per chunk; `spacing` spreads the difference between the element's clusters, `spacingAndGlyphs` scales them), `dominant-baseline`/`alignment-baseline`/`baseline-shift` from the face metrics, `text-rendering: optimizeSpeed` aliased. Vertical writing modes are not implemented.
- `text-decoration`/`text-decoration-line` (underline, overline, line-through) propagate to descendants and take the fill and stroke of the declaring element (the `<text>` when declared outside it). Lines use the face's post underline and OS/2 strikeout metrics (overline at the ascender with underline thickness), break at dx/dy/rotate and per cluster on a textPath; overline and underline paint before the glyphs, line-through after.
- Markers on path/line/polyline/polygon follow resvg's vertex angles, `orient` (auto, auto-start-reverse, angles), `markerUnits`, `viewBox` scale, `refX/refY`, overflow clip, `context-fill/stroke` and `paint-order`; each instance pays its content's budget cost.
- Filters: blur, offset, flood, merge, composite, blend, colour matrix, component transfer, drop shadow, morphology, tile, image (element or picture), turbulence (≤ 10 octaves), convolve matrix (≤ 1024 cells and 2^30 cell-pixels), displacement map (straight-alpha map), diffuse and specular lighting with distant/point/spot lights (resvg's pixel and depth conventions).
- `transform-origin`, `vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke` (stroke outlined in host pixels: `render::Host` carries the render scale), `shape-rendering: crispEdges|optimizeSpeed` (aliased fills through `Canvas::set_antialiasing`).
- CSS: type/class/id/attribute selectors (`= ~= |= ^= $= *=`), `:root`, `:first-child`, `:last-child`, `:only-child`, `:nth-child`, `:nth-last-child`, `:not(...)`, descendant/child/`+`/`~`, `!important` ignored, `var()`, `currentColor`; `@media` (width/height/aspect-ratio min/max, orientation, prefers-color-scheme, `not`/`only`, nested ≤ 8) is evaluated at the intrinsic size for loading and at the render size when drawing: a document with media rules keeps its expanded source and prefetched pictures and rebuilds (≤ 4 cached variants) when the matching set changes. `@import` is ignored.
- The desktop scheme reaches documents through `rikka_image` (`ReadRequest`/`LoadRequest::color_scheme`); `rikka-ui-host`'s image cache passes the drawing `Ui`'s theme and re-rasterizes only entries whose document follows the scheme.
- Links never leave the document's folder: `from_bytes` documents open no files, opened ones reach only regular files under their own canonical folder.
- Deliberate behaviours: `gradientTransform`/`patternTransform` are not inherited through `href`; an id used twice resolves to the last element of the needed kind; internal entities expand to at most 64 MiB and 8 levels; feImage element references render in user space shifted by explicit `x`/`y`.
- Untrusted input is bounded (`tests/malformed.rs` checks per-thread peak allocation): gzip ≤ 64 MiB, ≤ 2^21 `<`, one `Budget` per document (16 MiB + 16 bytes per source byte; nodes, path points, glyph outlines, 512 per text character, marker instances, decoded rasters, parsed glyph documents), selector matching ≤ 2^27 steps (else `TooComplex`), `tree/levels.rs` depth 512 / layers 32 / nested resources 8 (markers and filters count as resources), 256 filter primitives, 16 live results.
- `tests/oracle.rs` compares every `tests/fixtures/*.svg` at 1× and 2× with resvg (test-only, built with its `text` feature; the test loads the faces rikka-text-catalog matches for the fixture fonts, Noto Color Emoji included, into resvg's fontdb): each channel within 24/255 of the 3×3 range of resvg's native and 4× supersampled renderings, mean error under 0.75/255. Features resvg lacks or gets wrong (non-scaling strokes, structural selectors, `@media`, displacement scale) are covered in `tests/rendering.rs`; COLR and SVG-in-OpenType glyphs in `tests/glyphs.rs` through a synthetic font the test installs as the process's only font.
